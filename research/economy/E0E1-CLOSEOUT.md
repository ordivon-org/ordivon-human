# HUMAN-ECON-001 E0–E1 Closeout

## Decision

The first substantive Ordivon Human study is now active and has completed its first evidence round.

The project no longer treats completion of a Human System Atlas as the prerequisite for research. The Atlas and Methods are support layers consumed by concrete questions.

## Completed

### E0 — outcome framework

Established a plural objective:

```text
present quality of life
+ material security
+ liquid resilience
+ real net wealth
+ earning capability
+ low harmful lock-in
+ economic autonomy
+ sustainable recovery
```

No universal score, saving rate, wealth target, or retirement threshold was admitted.

### E1 — state and transition model

Established the minimum distinctions among:

- income, consumption, assets, liabilities, and liquidity;
- active saving and valuation gains;
- liquid and illiquid wealth;
- flexible expenditure and infrequently adjustable commitments;
- financial capital and earning capability;
- asset ownership and actual control;
- ordinary volatility and correlated failure;
- net wealth and named option coverage.

### Evidence Review 001

Tested the initial mechanism claims against official frameworks, natural experiments, administrative data, household-finance models, and Chinese survey infrastructure.

### China 2025 baseline

Created a reproducible calculation from official National Bureau of Statistics values without inferring household saving or causal effects from national averages.

## Findings that survived

### F1 — liquidity is not net worth

Near-term mobilizable resources change default, consumption, forced-sale, and recovery outcomes independently of accounting wealth.

### F2 — gross saving is not whole-balance-sheet improvement

An increase in a visible saving account can be partly offset by new debt or reduced liquidity.

### F3 — commitments alter risk capacity

Obligations that are expensive or slow to adjust amplify moderate shocks and reduce refusal and exit power.

### F4 — human capital belongs in the economic risk model

Labour-income volatility, market correlation, social insurance, transferability, and work flexibility matter. Age alone is not a sufficient risk rule.

### F5 — wealth growth requires decomposition

Observed wealth change must be separated into active saving, earnings growth, transfers, leverage, valuation, costs, and losses.

### F6 — economic autonomy is a distinct outcome

Income or wealth alone does not reveal whether a person can wait, refuse, relocate, retrain, reduce work, recover from failure, or replace a controller.

### F7 — life quality cannot be proxied by expenditure

Spending can protect health, relationships, time, and capability, or create low-value lock-in. Expenditure amount and category are insufficient without outcomes and adjustability.

### F8 — Chinese aggregate data are context, not the answer

The national mean, median, urban/rural differences, and consumption composition show material heterogeneity but cannot identify joint household trajectories.

## Rejected claims

- net worth is sufficient for financial resilience;
- automatic saving necessarily creates equal net wealth;
- all recurring expenditure is equally compressible;
- younger people universally have more financial risk capacity;
- average income minus average consumption is the typical household saving rate;
- low expenditure necessarily means high efficiency;
- higher income necessarily means greater autonomy;
- an investment-first research program is sufficient for early economic independence.

## Still unresolved

- the quantitative importance of earning growth versus active saving and asset return at different initial wealth levels in China;
- which expenditure categories preserve life quality most efficiently;
- how to identify commitment burden in CHFS and CFPS;
- how family support changes autonomy when support is conditional or revocable;
- which proxies validly measure refusal, exit, retraining, and relocation capacity;
- how much liquid runway is appropriate under different income and support structures;
- which asset and debt strategies dominate for specific states.

## Infrastructure judgment

One small standard-library script is retained because it reproduces published aggregate calculations and their limits.

No database, data collector, dashboard, scoring service, optimization engine, personal profile, or restricted survey data were added.
