# HUMAN-ECON-001 E0–E9 First-Principles Closeout

## Decision

The first complete research cycle of HUMAN-ECON-001 is closed.

The program now answers:

> How can a person expand durable command over resources and future production without materially degrading present life quality, while increasing the range of actions they can freely choose?

The answer is a conditional mechanism map, not a universal financial plan.

## Final research spine

```text
E0  terminal outcomes
E1  wealth-formation mechanism
E2  stages and paths
E3  growth-engine magnitude
E4  life quality and capability investment
E5  capability conversion
E6  ownership and productive leverage
E7  capital accumulation and allocation
E8  continuity, execution, and failure
E9  conditional strategy map
```

## Final terminal outcomes

The program retains three terminal outcomes:

1. **present life quality** — a life worth living now;
2. **durable economic power** — controllable claims on resources and production across time;
3. **economic autonomy** — a larger feasible set of chosen actions.

Income, saving, liquidity, capability, ownership, leverage, commitments, and risk are instruments or constraints.

## Final mechanism

```text
capability
→ value creation
→ value capture
→ quality-preserving surplus
→ productive ownership
→ leverage
→ compounding
→ autonomy
```

Every transition can fail independently:

- capability may not meet valuable demand;
- value may be created but captured elsewhere;
- income may not become surplus;
- surplus may not become productive ownership;
- nominal ownership may lack control or transfer rights;
- leverage may increase maintenance faster than output;
- concentration or behavioural error may erase compounding;
- wealth may grow without improving life or choice.

## Final stage model

```text
economic dependence
→ labour-dominant independent income
→ stable surplus
→ scalable ownership
→ capital- and system-dominant wealth
→ high economic autonomy
```

A stage is defined by the current binding constraint, not age or one wealth threshold.

## Main magnitude conclusion

Financial return is proportional to the capital base.

A recurring annual income improvement `ΔY` has the same one-year magnitude as return on capital:

```text
K = ΔY / r
```

Therefore:

```text
small capital
→ capability, market access, recurring captured value, and ownership formation dominate

stable surplus
→ conversion into productive ownership and diversified claims dominates

large productive base
→ allocation, governance, preservation, and compounding dominate
```

This is a scale relationship, not a forecast.

## Life-quality conclusion

Consumption and wealth formation are not pure opposites.

Money and time may:

- preserve essential function;
- restore present life;
- build capability;
- create productive leverage;
- buy back usable time;
- produce enjoyment;
- create harmful lock-in;
- leak without meaningful value.

The relevant question is the marginal function of the next unit, not whether a retail category is morally good or bad.

## Capability conclusion

Private competence does not automatically produce income.

Durable conversion requires:

```text
competence
→ valuable demand
→ credible proof
→ market access
→ reliable delivery
→ transferable control
→ bargaining or ownership
```

In an AI-rich environment, routine execution becomes cheaper while problem selection, verification, integration, trust, distribution, accountability, and ownership become more important bottlenecks.

## Ownership conclusion

Ownership is a bundle of:

- residual income rights;
- residual control rights;
- transfer and exit rights;
- information and exclusion rights;
- maintenance responsibility;
- loss exposure.

True productive ownership creates residual claims that persist beyond the original labour and can survive, at least partly, owner absence or transfer.

The transition is:

```text
current labour
→ reusable process
→ replicable product
→ governed production system
→ transferable residual claim
→ diversified productive ownership
```

## Productive-leverage conclusion

Leverage may come from:

- tools;
- replication;
- distribution;
- organization;
- capital;
- borrowing.

The retained test is:

```text
net productive leverage
=
additional durable output or claims
− permanent maintenance
− fragility
− dependence
```

More people, more software, more debt, or more scale do not automatically create leverage.

## Concentration conclusion

Concentration can be productive when the owner has:

- control;
- private knowledge;
- high marginal reinvestment opportunity;
- asset-specific incentives;
- bounded failure.

It remains costly because income, wealth, identity, network, and obligations may fail together.

The correct transition rule is:

```text
retain concentrated exposure
while marginal control and creation value exceed
marginal continuity, option, and diversification value
```

Diversify as control value declines, internal reinvestment weakens, and concentration begins reducing autonomy.

## Capital-allocation conclusion

Capital serves functions rather than asset labels:

1. continuity;
2. options and exploration;
3. controlled productive opportunities;
4. diversified long-horizon productive claims;
5. protection and liability reduction.

For the next unit of capital:

```text
choose the feasible use that changes the binding constraint
by the largest durable amount
relative to time, attention, dependence, maintenance, and failure risk
```

Financial allocation must include labour income, business ownership, housing, debt, flexibility, insurance, jurisdiction, and control.

## Attention conclusion

Active allocation or trading is admitted only when:

```text
expected advantage × capital base
>
financial costs + error risk
+ attention and displaced-opportunity cost
```

Observation and adjustment are not free. Inattention and low decision frequency may be rational.

## Execution conclusion

The minimum execution architecture is:

```text
separate failure domains
→ bound exposure
→ scale through evidence
→ act only when state changes
→ preserve exit and recovery
→ delete rituals that change no decision
```

The principal failures are:

- repeated reset;
- action substitution;
- percentage blindness;
- luck–skill attribution error;
- loss chasing;
- success escalation;
- commitment ratchet;
- capability depletion;
- income without ownership conversion;
- founder dependence;
- platform tenancy mistaken for ownership;
- attention fragmentation;
- measurement and governance theatre;
- AI-induced agency drift.

## Final strategy form

A credible recommendation must state:

```text
starting state
→ binding constraint
→ dominant mechanism
→ absolute magnitude
→ life-quality and attention cost
→ dependence and control
→ ordinary failure and terminal failure
→ evidence ladder
→ stop or exit rule
→ next-stage transition
```

## Universal advice rejected

The program does not admit unconditional rules such as:

- one saving rate;
- one emergency-fund duration;
- one age-based risk allocation;
- one retirement number;
- entrepreneurship always dominates employment;
- public assets always dominate private ownership;
- concentration is always necessary or always irrational;
- all leverage is harmful or productive;
- lower spending necessarily improves the path;
- more financial activity creates more control;
- higher net worth necessarily creates autonomy.

## Infrastructure judgment

Retained:

- first-principles research documents;
- one small official public-average calculation;
- explicit source and inference boundaries;
- a thin continuity constraint.

Rejected:

- household microdata program;
- personal wealth score;
- portfolio optimizer;
- financial dashboard;
- behaviour-tracking platform;
- mandatory user profile;
- universal allocation rules;
- governance whose existence is its evidence of value.

## Research status

```text
E0–E9 first-principles cycle: complete
public-average calibration: complete
conditional strategy map: complete
personalized financial authority: not claimed
permanent data or product platform: rejected
next automatic E-series item: none
```

Future work must begin from a new concrete problem or a falsifier that exposes a named failure in this model. The existence of E10 is not presumed.
