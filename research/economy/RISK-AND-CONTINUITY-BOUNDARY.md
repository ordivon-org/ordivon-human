# Supporting Boundary — Risk, Liquidity, and Continuity

## Role

Risk and liquidity are not the main wealth engine.

They answer a narrower question:

> Can the person continue the wealth-formation path after an ordinary failure, delay, or shock?

## Minimum retained distinctions

- usable liquid resources are not identical to total net worth;
- fixed obligations reduce the ability to wait, refuse, or change path;
- debt and leverage can erase prior compounding;
- assets, income, and support may fail together;
- nominal ownership does not guarantee control or access;
- a growth path is defective when its ordinary failure mode permanently ends further attempts.

## Continuity rule

```text
do not maximize growth by accepting an ordinary failure mode
that destroys essential life, future capability, or the ability to continue
```

## Why the prior E2 data program was deleted

The former E2 expanded into CHFS/CFPS variable maps, synthetic fixtures, panel-link logic, disclosure controls, and a future microdata execution plan.

That work was technically valid but strategically mis-scoped:

- it studied household-state classification rather than the core wealth-formation mechanism;
- available data determined the research direction;
- descriptive precision could not establish what an individual should do;
- implementation cost exceeded its present decision value.

Git history preserves the prototype at revision `ec8142020f207f0f053e7db974d5cb47b80dbc3f`.

No protected data were acquired. No active microdata work remains planned.
