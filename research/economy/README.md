# HUMAN-ECON-001 — A Practical Path to Economic Autonomy

> **Status:** the first-principles cycle, practical E0–E9 reconstruction, and
> initial six-case structural review are complete. This is a conditional
> framework, not individualized advice or an empirically validated intervention.

## Executive judgment

Economic autonomy develops through branching transitions across work and
exchange, ownership and operation, and capital and optionality. The correct next
move is the smallest recoverable action that changes the current binding
transition without making ordinary failure destructive.

## Question

How can an ordinary person build durable income, productive ownership, capital,
and economic autonomy without sacrificing the conditions required to live and
continue?

## Method

Derive the minimum mechanism from first principles, calibrate scale with public
averages, compare rival paths and failure modes, pressure-test the structure with
privacy-reduced cases, and admit granular evidence only when it can change a
concrete decision.

## Findings

### Current answer

Economic autonomy develops when a person can improve the current binding
transition while preserving life quality, recoverability, obligations, and
control.

```text
find the unsupported transition
→ name the smallest binding set
→ choose one recoverable evidence-producing action
→ declare maximum acceptable loss
→ retain value even if the action fails
→ continue, scale, redesign, hold, recover, or stop
```

The model has three engines:

| Engine | Transition logic |
|---|---|
| Work and exchange | functioning → capability → proof → trusted exchange → surplus |
| Ownership and operation | repeated result → reusable ownership → repeatability → governed operation → residual claims |
| Capital and optionality | continuity → options → controlled claims → independent claims → protection → deliberate life use |

Four rails apply everywhere:

- life quality and functioning;
- continuity and recoverability;
- relations and obligations;
- control and maintenance.

## What a practical action must contain

```text
current bottleneck
bounded action
maximum acceptable loss
external evidence
value retained under failure
review date or evidence event
continue | scale | redesign | hold | recover | stop
```

An action is rejected when it depends on hidden privilege, guaranteed demand,
unbounded capital, exceptional success, or a false claim of passive ownership.

## Evidence

The evidence base includes first-principles constraints, mature economic and
organizational counterexamples, official public averages used only for scale,
six privacy-reduced structural cases, explicit falsifiers, and reproducible
aggregate and synthetic calculations.

## Limitations

The framework is supported by:

- first-principles constraints and mechanism comparison;
- mature economic and organizational counterexamples;
- official public averages used only for scale;
- six privacy-reduced cases;
- explicit falsifiers and stop rules;
- reproducible aggregate and synthetic calculations.

It does not provide an individual career verdict, guaranteed educational return,
universal saving rate, emergency-fund duration, portfolio allocation, market
forecast, tax or migration answer, or evidence that entrepreneurship is superior
to employment.

Current local labour-market, educational, legal, tax, migration, health, or
financial evidence may be necessary for high-consequence decisions.
