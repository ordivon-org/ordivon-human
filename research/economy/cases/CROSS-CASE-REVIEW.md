# E0–E9 Six-Case Cross-Case Review

> **Reader guidance:** this is the research synthesis for the initial six-case suite. Practitioners should start with [`../PRACTICE-GUIDE.md`](../PRACTICE-GUIDE.md).

## Scope and evidence status

This review compares six structural cases against the practical E0–E9 framework.

- CASE-001 is a privacy-reduced case derived from a real trajectory and may receive longitudinal evidence.
- CASE-002 through CASE-006 are composite cases constructed to pressure-test distinct economic relations.

The suite can test internal coherence, action differentiation, missing mechanisms, and feasibility assumptions.

It cannot establish population validity, causal effectiveness, representative outcomes, or a universally optimal path.

The correct maturity claim is:

> a coherent practical framework that has survived an initial structural pressure test, not an empirically validated science of economic autonomy.

## Cases reviewed

| Case | Dominant relation | First unsupported transition |
|---|---|---|
| CASE-001 | early capability formation | output → externally trusted repeated use |
| CASE-002 | precarious labour | fragmented labour → reliable exchange and refusal power |
| CASE-003 | stable employment | salary and surplus → portable options and bounded ownership |
| CASE-004 | independent professional work | repeated service → reusable controlled production |
| CASE-005 | operating ownership | founder-led business → owner-independent normal operation |
| CASE-006 | complex capital ownership | nominal wealth → usable low-maintenance autonomy |

## What survived the review

### 1. Terminal outcomes remain necessary

Present life quality, durable economic power, and economic autonomy changed the action ranking in every case.

Without present life quality, the model would overprescribe work, saving, and systemization.

Without durable economic power, it would mistake current income for security.

Without autonomy, it would mistake revenue, ownership, or net worth for the ability to choose.

### 2. The first unsupported transition is useful

Each case produced a materially different next action:

- narrow external proof;
- continuity and transferable proof;
- portable options and bounded ownership;
- one reusable production unit;
- one owner-independent operating unit;
- usable control, liquidity, and simplification.

This supports diagnosis by transition rather than by occupation, age, or wealth label.

### 3. Ownership must remain conditional

The cases did not support a universal move from labour to product, business, and system.

Premium direct work may remain rational.

Employment may support high autonomy when capability is portable, commitments are bounded, and independent claims exist.

Founder concentration may remain rational while control and marginal creation value remain high.

Ownership is valuable when it improves persistence, control, residual claims, or life—not because it is a higher-status category.

### 4. Net productive leverage remains a strong test

CASE-004 and CASE-005 confirmed that revenue, users, staff, automation, and delegation can increase owner burden faster than durable output.

The useful test remains:

```text
persistent output and claims added
− owner labour
− maintenance
− fragility
− external dependence
```

### 5. Capital must be assigned by function

The cases supported continuity, options, controlled production, independent claims, and protection.

CASE-006 exposed one omitted function: deliberate conversion of sufficient capital into time, health, relationships, mobility, creation, simplification, and contribution.

### 6. Evidence, boundaries, and stop rules differentiate action

The same mechanism can be rational at one evidence level and destructive at another.

Manual service before productization, one responsibility before broad delegation, one governance repair before liquidation, and an adjacent work test before long retraining all depend on bounded evidence ladders.

## What failed or required revision

### 1. The single linear path failed

The original practical sequence implied:

```text
capability
→ exchange
→ surplus
→ ownership
→ system
→ capital
→ autonomy
```

The cases do not support this as a compulsory progression.

- CASE-002 required capital for continuity before stable surplus.
- CASE-003 could reach meaningful autonomy without a business system.
- CASE-004 might rationally remain premium direct practice.
- CASE-005 could retain intentional owner operation.
- CASE-006 required life use and simplification after substantial ownership.

**Revision:** use three engines—work and exchange, ownership and operation, capital and optionality—rather than one ladder.

### 2. A single bottleneck can be false precision

CASE-002 contained coupled instability, weak proof, and insufficient continuity.

CASE-001 coupled retained capability and external legibility.

CASE-005 coupled founder dependence and concentration.

**Revision:** diagnose the smallest binding set when one constraint cannot change the transition alone, then run one intervention that distinguishes which constraint dominates.

### 3. Continuity was placed too late

The earlier path treated continuity mainly as an execution or capital concern near the end.

CASE-002 showed that continuity can be the primary growth mechanism. It creates schedule stability, refusal power, and the capacity to learn.

**Revision:** continuity and recoverability become an always-on rail and a capital function available at every stage.

### 4. Life quality was represented as a chapter rather than a rail

CASE-002, CASE-003, CASE-005, and CASE-006 showed that health, relationships, care, recovery, and chosen life alter every transition.

**Revision:** E5 remains a module, but life quality becomes a terminal outcome and cross-cutting constraint rather than a point passed before ownership.

### 5. The framework was too individual-isolated

External support, care obligations, employers, clients, platforms, law, institutions, and family arrangements repeatedly altered feasibility and control.

**Revision:** every diagnosis must state important external access, support, obligations, consent, and dependency. Resources should not be treated as fully individual when rights and commitments are shared.

### 6. Capital lacked a terminal-use function

The first five functions favored preservation and accumulation.

CASE-006 showed that continued accumulation can reduce autonomy through maintenance, complexity, and postponed life.

**Revision:** add deliberate life use and release as a sixth capital function.

### 7. One evidence ladder overfit early market entry

A ladder from observation to paid exchange to scale fits early production, but not governance, simplification, or capital use.

**Revision:** use separate evidence ladders for work and exchange, ownership and operation, and capital and governance.

### 8. “Validation suite” overstated the evidence

Composite cases can expose logical and practical failures but cannot validate real-world effectiveness.

**Revision:** call the six cases a structural pressure-test suite. Reserve longitudinal validation for real trajectories with observed outcomes.

## Revised model

### Three engines

```text
A. work and exchange
functioning → capability → proof → exchange → surplus

B. ownership and operation
repeated result → reusable ownership → repeatability
→ governed operation → residual claims

C. capital and optionality
continuity → options → controlled claims
→ independent claims → protection → deliberate life use
```

### Four rails

```text
life quality and functioning
continuity and recoverability
external relations and obligations
control and maintenance
```

### Execution rule

```text
identify the unsupported transition
→ name the smallest binding set
→ choose the matching engine
→ run one recoverable evidence-producing action
→ retain value under failure
→ continue, scale, separate, redesign, stop, hold, or recover
```

## Cross-case conflict review

### Stability versus growth

No contradiction remains when stability is treated by mechanism.

- In CASE-002, stability enables capability and refusal power.
- In CASE-003, excessive attachment to stability reduces external options.

The relevant question is whether stability increases or decreases future feasible action.

### Concentration versus diversification

No universal rule survived.

- CASE-005 may justify concentration where control and marginal creation value remain high.
- CASE-006 may require diversification when control weakens and failures correlate.

The decision depends on controlled creation value versus continuity, liquidity, and autonomy value.

### Direct labour versus ownership

No universal hierarchy survived.

- CASE-004 may rationally retain premium direct labour.
- CASE-005 requires systemization only if owner-independent operation serves the desired life.

The test is not whether labour disappears. It is whether the chosen production relation produces acceptable value, control, maintenance, and life.

### Accumulation versus use

Both can be correct.

- In early cases, accumulation protects continuity and creates options.
- In CASE-006, deliberate use may dominate further accumulation.

The decision changes when marginal future power falls below current terminal value and autonomy gained through use.

## Coverage limits

The six cases cover major production relations, but not every human constraint.

Underrepresented areas include:

- severe or fluctuating health limitations;
- intensive caregiving and joint household decisions;
- legal or institutional exclusion;
- forced migration or major political shocks;
- retirement decumulation under uncertain longevity;
- persistent discrimination or inaccessible markets.

These do not justify automatic case expansion.

A new case should be admitted only when a concrete trajectory shows that one of these conditions changes the framework’s action ranking or requires a new mechanism.

## Chapter revisions

- **E0:** replaced a single path and total stages with three engines, four rails, a state map, and the smallest-binding-set rule.
- **E5:** clarified that life quality and relational obligations apply throughout the system.
- **E8:** added deliberate life use and release as a sixth capital function.
- **E9:** replaced one evidence ladder and stage sequence with engine-specific ladders and branching dispositions.
- **Case suite:** reclassified from validation to structural pressure testing.

No E10 is required. The corrections belong inside the existing structure.

## Final judgment

The six-case review supports the framework’s central mechanism but rejects its remaining linear presentation.

The revised framework is:

> a practical, branching model for increasing capability, trusted exchange, surplus, productive ownership, governable claims, and usable autonomy under life-quality, continuity, relational, and maintenance constraints.

It remains incomplete as empirical science. Its next meaningful evidence should come from observed real trajectories, not additional conceptual expansion.
