# Ordivon Human Foundations

Human Foundations asks a different question from Human's applied problem cycles:

> **What minimum structures, processes, relations and distinctions are needed to
> study a human without reducing the person to one discipline, one score, one
> current state, or one social role?**

This programme does **not** replace Human's problem-first Charter. Applied cycles
still begin with concrete human problems. Foundations exists because repeated
work in `HUMAN-ECON-001`, `HUMAN-AI-001`, Game Foundations, Media Foundations,
and Ordivon World exposed the same conceptual debts: capability versus
performance, person versus context, desire versus goal, agency versus control,
experience versus observation, resources versus options, and current state
versus developmental history.

## Research boundary

Human Foundations is not:

- a universal ontology of all people;
- a personality inventory;
- a digital twin;
- a mandatory user/profile schema;
- a clinical classification;
- a human-value score;
- a claim that one discipline owns the complete Human model;
- an authorization system for acting on a person.

The programme instead treats `Human` as one reality that can require several
non-equivalent projections depending on the question and scale.

## Research method

Each foundation round should normally:

1. freeze the overloaded terms and inherited categories under test;
2. collect materially different external theories/frameworks as pressure tests;
3. separate constructs that occupy different causal, experiential, normative or
   measurement roles;
4. build competing models rather than adopting the first plausible taxonomy;
5. attack them with cross-context and boundary cases;
6. audit scale, timescale, first-person/third-person evidence and normativity;
7. delete distinctions that add no explanatory, predictive, decision or
   recovery value;
8. reconnect surviving distinctions to previous rounds and applied Human work;
9. preserve open rivals and UNKNOWN rather than force total synthesis;
10. let the next round be selected by the boundary exposed by the current one.

This is the Human adaptation of the research discipline already demonstrated in
Game Foundations and Media Foundations. The Human-specific additions are
**reflexivity, subjectivity, normativity, heterogeneity, multi-scale causation,
and authority/ethics**.

## Current round

[`HF0-PROBLEM-SPACE.md`](HF0-PROBLEM-SPACE.md) reconstructs the Human problem
space without assuming the legacy H0 Atlas is already the right ontology.

HF0's main result is not a replacement list of compartments. It is a projection
architecture plus a small set of anti-collapses:

```text
one Human reality
→ several question-relative projections
→ explicit scale / time / evidence / normative coordinates
→ minimum relation model for the phenomenon under study
```

The old [`../h0/ATLAS.md`](../h0/ATLAS.md) remains useful as a supporting
functional index. HF0 reclassifies it from a candidate top-level decomposition
into one compact **view library** whose categories may split, merge, or become
derived when a foundation question requires it.

## Current continuation

HF0 does not pre-plan HF1–HF20. Its exact continuation is recorded in
[`HF0-CONTINUATION.md`](HF0-CONTINUATION.md). The first forced neighboring
problem is the boundary among **Human, organism/body, subject, self, person,
actor and agent**, including persistence through time and extension through
ordinary tools.
