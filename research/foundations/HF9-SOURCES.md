# HF9 External Evidence and Source Ledger

## Evidence rule

HF9 studies transformations among representations. The same observed answer can be
produced by different interpretations, retrieval routes, heuristics, rules, models,
search strategies or tool interactions.

Each claim therefore separates:

```text
input representation
interpretation / problem representation
candidate inference process
normative criterion
resource / time constraints
external aids
current judgment / response
objective task outcome
```

The foundation never equates `normative error` with a unique cognitive mechanism.

# 1. Conditional reasoning: mental models are useful but incomplete

## Evans (1993) — critical appraisal of mental-model theory

- PMID: 8403833
- DOI: 10.1016/0010-0277(93)90056-2
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8403833/
- HF9 use: conditional inference patterns exposed phenomena not explained by the
  then-current mental-model account, including affirmation/denial patterns,
  wording differences and negative-conclusion bias.

### HF9 inference

```text
ConditionalReasoning != OneSettledMentalModelAlgorithm
```

Mental models remain an important theory family, not ontology authority.

# 5. Working memory constrains reasoning without defining it

## Copeland & Radvansky (2004)

- PMID: 15513254
- DOI: 10.1080/02724980343000846
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15513254/
- HF9 use: larger working-memory spans correlated with better syllogistic
  performance, while multiple reasoning models remained compatible with aspects of
  the data.

## De Neys (2006) — dual processing under load

- PMID: 16683931
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16683931/
- HF9 use: executive load selectively impaired correct reasoning when belief and
  logic conflicted, supporting a resource-demanding correction process without

## false-premise interference

- PMID: 15478753
- DOI: 10.3758/BF03195850
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15478753/
- HF9 use: interference susceptibility, working memory and processing speed jointly

### HF9 inference

```text
WorkingMemory supports Reasoning_D

Reasoning != WorkingMemoryCapacity
```

# 7. Probabilistic inference: representation format changes performance

## Gigerenzer & Hoffrage (1995)

- DOI: 10.1037/0033-295X.102.4.684
- Primary record: https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.102.4.684
- HF9 use: Bayesian algorithms became computationally simpler when information was
  expressed in natural-frequency form; many statistically naive participants then
  produced Bayesian-consistent inferences.

## Evans et al. (2000) — frequency-format boundary

- PMID: 11018509
- DOI: 10.1016/S0010-0277(00)00098-6
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11018509/
- HF9 use: frequency formats were not automatically superior; benefits depended on
  set-inclusion representation, and some frequency responses introduced new
  weighting errors.

## Hoffrage et al. (2015)

- PMID: 26528197
- PMCID: PMC4604268
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26528197/
- HF9 use: natural frequencies improved Bayesian reasoning in simple and more
  complex inference tasks.

## Galesic, Gigerenzer & Straubinger (2009)

- PMID: 19129155
- DOI: 10.1177/0272989X08329463
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19129155/
- HF9 use: natural-frequency representations improved medical screening inference

### HF9 inference

```text
ProbabilityError != NoProbabilisticReasoningCapacity
RepresentationFormat is part of ReasoningTask
```

# 28. Source-level synthesis

Across conditional, probabilistic, causal, analogical, insight and AI-assisted
reasoning, the surviving distinctions are:

```text
Inference != Retrieval
Inference != Association
Reasoning != FormalLogic
LogicalValidity != Believability
LogicalValidity != WorldTruthOfPremises
ReasoningProcess != NormativeCriterion

Type1Like != Type2Like
Type1/Type2 != TwoLiteralExhaustiveSystems
WorkingMemoryConstraint != ReasoningDefinition

ProbabilityRepresentation != ProbabilityContent
ProbabilityError != NoBayesianCapacity
BaseRateNeglect != UniversalBaseRateBlindness

Heuristic != Bias
Heuristic != Irrationality
Simple != Inferior
MoreInformation != BetterInference by definition

Observation != Intervention
Correlation != Causation
Prediction != CausalIdentification
CounterfactualSimulation != CausationDefinition
CausalJudgment != CausalTruth

SourceAccess != Mapping != Adaptation != Transfer
Analogy != SurfaceSimilarity
Expertise changes problem representation but is domain-specific

ProblemRepresentation != ProblemStatement
Search != ProblemSolving
Insight != AhaFeeling
Impasse != NecessaryForInsight
RepresentationChange and search can interact

AIAssistedJudgment != IndependentReasoning
HumanInLoop != ErrorCorrectionByDefault
Warning != CalibratedAIUse
StructuredHumanAIInteraction != UnguidedAIUse
```

The repeated residual is **selection and commitment among possible actions over
future time**. Reasoning can derive/evaluate representations and problem solving
can generate candidate paths, but many cases require choosing which option to
pursue, how much information/search to buy, when to stop, how to plan sequences,
when to explore versus exploit and when a provisional judgment becomes committed
action. That boundary should be exposed by HF9 rather than pre-assumed.
