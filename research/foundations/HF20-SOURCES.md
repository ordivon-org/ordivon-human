# HF20 — Evidence Bridge

HF20 is extracted from the completed deep route rather than researched independently.

Canonical deep evidence owner:

[`../deep-foundations/HD1-SOURCES.md`](../deep-foundations/HD1-SOURCES.md)

Key evidence classes supporting the extraction are:

1. **active sensing** — Human sniff dynamics and tactile sampling show evidence acquisition
   can be action-dependent;
2. **measurement intervention** — eye-movement/fixation procedures can alter binocular-
   rivalry dynamics;
3. **multisensory causal inference** — cue integration depends on inferred common/separate
   sources rather than reliability alone;
4. **recurrent causal perturbation** — TMS demonstrates feedback necessity for selected
   perceptual grouping tasks;
5. **sensory substitution/plasticity** — physical channel, cortical route, functional
   information and phenomenal quality can dissociate;
6. **development/sight restoration** — cue-integration policies depend on development and
   sensory experience rather than a fixed adult rule;
7. **proprioceptive/interoceptive dissociations** — body sensing is estimated,
   recalibratable and domain-specific;
8. **affordance studies** — perception for action can scale to organism-environment and
   person-plus-tool relations;
9. **illusion/model studies** — systematic perceptual divergences can arise from multiple
   computational/representational mechanisms;
10. **Human-machine comparisons** — artificial models can reproduce some human error
    structure without licensing representational/mechanistic identity.
