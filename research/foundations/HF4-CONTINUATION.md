- wanting/liking dissociation in food and alcohol;
- reward prediction error versus reward receipt;
- hunger/satiety changes in food reward;
- sickness changing effort sensitivity without equivalent reward-sensitivity
  change;
- sleep/state dissociation between wanting and work output;
- prospective effort discounting;
- effort-dependent enhancement of later reward evaluation;
- delay versus effort discounting;

  caveat;

- effort increasing near deadlines despite fatigue.

# HF5 — Need, Homeostasis, Allostasis, Interoception, Satiety, Stress, Fatigue and Recovery

## HF5 starting questions

1. What is `Need` relative to deficit, desire, wanting, goal and value?
2. What counts as homeostatic regulation, and which regulated variables actually
   have fixed versus moving targets?
3. What does allostasis add beyond feedback control?
4. What is interoception relative to receptor signal, neural representation,
   subjective bodily feeling and inference?
5. How do deprivation and satiety transform wanting, liking and decision value?
6. What is arousal relative to wakefulness, activation, autonomic state and
   motivation?
7. What is stress: stimulus, response, appraisal, load or state?
8. What is fatigue: subjective experience, performance decline, state estimate,
   altered effort cost or regulatory signal?
9. How should cognitive and physical fatigue be related?
10. What counts as recovery: restored capacity, reduced subjective cost, changed
    state, or all under different projections?
11. How do acute adaptation and chronic allostatic load differ?
12. How should first-person bodily evidence and physiological measures be combined?
13. Which bodily-state effects are specific versus domain-general?
14. What next foundation becomes unavoidable after bodily regulation is pressure-
    tested?

## Candidate HF5 falsifiers

- hunger versus satiety;
- thirst and osmoregulation;
- thermal regulation;
- sleep deprivation / sleep pressure;
- experimental sickness / inflammation;
- acute stress with reward versus safety effort;
- cognitive fatigue affecting physical effort valuation;
- physical versus cognitive fatigue dissociation;
- pain versus nociception;
- placebo/nocebo and interoceptive inference;
- panic/interoception manipulations;
- exercise, recovery and delayed fatigue;
- chronic stress/allostatic-load measures.

## Do not precommit

HF4 does not establish that:

- every need is conscious;
- every desire reflects a biological need;
- homeostasis means fixed-point constancy for every variable;
- allostasis is the final regulatory theory;
- interoception is direct error-free sensing;
- fatigue is one scalar resource deficit;
- stress is always harmful;
- stress always reduces motivation;
- rest automatically restores every form of capability;
- subjective fatigue equals physiological depletion;
- one biomarker measures allostatic load or recovery universally.

## Stop rule

Do not schedule HF6 now. HF5 must expose the next repeated neighboring distinction
from cross-system evidence rather than from a prewritten curriculum.
