# HF14 External Evidence and Source Ledger

## Evidence rule

HF14 separates two evidence roles that must not be collapsed:

```text
Empirical moral psychology
= evidence about how humans actually judge, decide, blame, allocate and attribute
  standing

Normative/conceptual primary texts
= candidate conceptual distinctions or normative theories
  not empirical proof of moral truth
```

Primary empirical studies are used for claims about moral cognition and behavior.
Primary philosophical/legal texts are used only to map contested normative concept
families where empirical studies cannot define the normative relation.

No source in this ledger is treated as proof that one moral theory is true.

# 1. Wrongness, blame and punishment are distinct

## Cushman (2008) — Crime and punishment

- PMID: 18439575
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cognition.2008.03.006
- Use: direct comparison of consequence, causation, belief and desire across moral
  judgment types. Wrongness/permissibility depended primarily on agent mental states,
  while blame/punishment jointly depended on mental states and causal connection to
  harmful consequences.

### HF14 inference

```text
Wrongness != Blameworthiness
Blameworthiness != PunishmentAppropriateness
Intent != MoralJudgmentTotality
CausalContribution affects judgment surfaces differently
```

# 56. Source-level synthesis

The strongest evidence-supported distinctions are:

```text
Wrongness != Blameworthiness != Punishment
Intent != Outcome != Causality != Foreseeability
ExPostOutcomeKnowledge != ExAnteForeseeability
NominalControl != EffectiveControl

Pain != Harm
PsychologicalHarm != PhysicalDamage
RiskOfHarm != RealizedHarm
HarmSeverity != Wrongness

Choice != ExperiencedWelfare
RememberedUtility != ExperiencedUtility
LifeSatisfaction != Affect
PopulationPreference != TargetExperiencedWelfare

Equality != Fairness
Inequality != Unfairness
Need != Merit
Fairness != Efficiency
ProceduralFairness != OutcomeFairness

ClaimRight != Liberty
LegalPower != PhysicalCapability
LegalRight != MoralRight

Promise != MereExpectation
PromiseMade != ValidMoralDuty by definition

CausalContribution != MoralResponsibility
Wrongdoing != Blameworthiness
Responsibility != Accountability
ObservedBlameAllocation != ResponsibilityTruth

PerceivedLegitimacy != NormativeLegitimacy
EmpiricalFit(F) != NormativeTruth(F)

AIExecution != AIMoralResponsibility
HumanInLoop != HumanResponsibilityByDefault
MachineExecution != MoralAuthorization

PerceivedAgency != PerceivedExperience
LowMoralAgency != LowMoralStanding by definition
ResponsibilityCapacity != RightsHoldingCapacity
```

The repeated unresolved question is not another moral judgment variable. It is the
**bearer/scope question**:

```text
Who or what can have welfare, be harmed, hold rights, bear duties, be responsible,
and count as morally considerable?
```

That is the HF15 moral-standing boundary.
