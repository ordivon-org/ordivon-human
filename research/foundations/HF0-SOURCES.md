# HF0 External Evidence and Framework Ledger

## Evidence rule

HF0 uses mature official frameworks, primary/review research and established
philosophical distinctions to generate constraints and counterexamples. A source
can support a local distinction without supplying the complete Human ontology.

The source families intentionally disagree in scope. That disagreement is useful
HF0 evidence.

## Functional / health / embodiment

### WHO — International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF)

- Authority: World Health Organization.
- URL: https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/international-classification-of-functioning-disability-and-health
- HF0 use: ICF separates body functions/structures, activity/participation and
  environmental factors. This pressure-tests any Human model that equates bodily
  condition with whole-person functioning or treats environment as residual
  noise.
- Transport limit: ICF is a health/functioning classification, not a general
  ontology of personhood, value, subjectivity or social life.

### IASP — revised definition of pain

- Authority: International Association for the Study of Pain.
- URL: https://www.iasp-pain.org/publications/iasp-news/iasp-announces-revised-definition-of-pain/
- HF0 use: pain is explicitly treated as a personal experience; pain and
  nociception are distinct; inability to verbally report does not negate the
  possibility of pain. This supplies a strong deletion witness for the
  first-person/third-person distinction.
- Transport limit: pain is one experiential domain, not a general theory of
  consciousness.

## Biobehavioural systems / cognition / motivation / social process

### NIMH Research Domain Criteria (RDoC)

- Authority: US National Institute of Mental Health.
- URLs:
  - https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/definitions-of-the-rdoc-domains-and-constructs
  - https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/constructs/social-processes
- HF0 use: RDoC distinguishes multiple functional domains and measures them
  across multiple units of analysis, while explicitly retaining development and
  environment. It also distinguishes arousal from motivation/valence and
  includes agency/self-knowledge under social processes.
- Transport limit: RDoC is a research framework for mental-health-relevant
  functional systems. It is not a universal Human ontology.

## Development / plasticity / history

### Lea, Tung & Archie (2018) — developmental plasticity

- DOI: 10.1093/emph/eox019
- Open record: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5798083/
- HF0 use: early environments can produce persistent later-life differences;
  developmental plasticity requires distinguishing inherited structure,
  environmental exposure, developmental response and later phenotype.
- Transport limit: the article centers evolutionary and health consequences and
  does not model the complete psychological/social person.

### Lifespan and developmental-plasticity literature

- Example review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10622369/
- HF0 use: Human state is historically produced and development remains partly
  plastic across the lifespan; current state cannot in general substitute for
  trajectory/history.
- Transport limit: specific developmental mechanisms and sensitive periods are
  domain dependent.

## Human–tool / situated capability

### Baber, Parekh & Cengiz (2014) — Tool use as distributed cognition

- DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00116
- Open record: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3932548/
- HF0 use: skilled action may depend on representations and constraints
  distributed across human, artifact, environment and object. This pressure-tests
  a purely internal capability model.
- Transport limit: distributed cognition is useful for task/capability analysis;
  it does not imply that every tool becomes part of the person or acquires the
  person's rights/identity.

## Agency / ownership / embodied self

### Braun et al. (2018) — senses of agency and ownership

- DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00535
- Open record: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5911504/
- HF0 use: sense of ownership and sense of agency can be experimentally
  dissociated. This falsifies any model that collapses bodily mineness, action
  authorship and objective control into one construct.
- Transport limit: phenomenal sense of agency is not identical to legal,
  political or normative autonomy.

### Limerick, Coyle & Moore (2014) — agency in human-computer interaction

- Open record: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4140386/
- HF0 use: technology changes action-feedback mappings and can alter experienced
  agency; human action can therefore be mediated without disappearing.
- Transport limit: HCI evidence does not by itself define personhood or moral
  responsibility.

## Self / person / persistence

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Personal Identity

- Current archival edition used: https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2024/entries/identity-personal/
- HF0 use: questions about us *as persons* differ from questions about us as
  living things, conscious beings or material objects; `self` is also used for
  several non-equivalent concepts. This is a direct conceptual pressure test on
  `Human = Person = Self`.
- Transport limit: philosophical positions remain contested and are not treated
  as empirical identity tests.

## First-person / third-person evidence

### First-person neuroscience and consciousness-measurement literature

- Open records:
  - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1459272/
  - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11652689/
- HF0 use: subjective experience is available from the first-person perspective
  in a way that another person's neural/behavioural state is not. Reports,
  objective task measures and physiology are related observations, not identical
  access routes.
- Transport limit: first-person reports can be noisy, criterion-dependent and
  incomplete; HF0 does not grant introspection infallibility.

## Social / institutional / environmental embedding

### WHO — social determinants / health equity

- URLs:
  - https://www.who.int/europe/health-topics/social-determinants-of-health/monitoring-health-inequities
  - https://www.who.int/health-topics/health-equity/building-the-evidence-for-action
- HF0 use: living, working, legal, political and economic conditions alter
  exposure, control and resources. This is a deletion witness against models
  that explain realized human outcomes solely from internal traits.
- Transport limit: social determinants of health do not explain every Human
  phenomenon or establish one causal weight across contexts.

### Bronfenbrenner bioecological tradition — PPCT review

- Open record: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10801006/
- HF0 use: Process–Person–Context–Time provides a useful rival to static trait or
  compartment models and emphasizes proximal processes and temporal embedding.
- Transport limit: the review also documents widespread misuse of the framework;
  HF0 therefore uses PPCT as a pressure test rather than importing its layers as
  ontology.

## Capability / choice / well-being / normativity

### UNDP — Human Development Reports

- 1990 report: https://hdr.undp.org/content/human-development-report-1990
- 2025 report: https://hdr.undp.org/content/human-development-report-2025
- HF0 use: human development distinguishes resources/income from capabilities,
  choices and the lives people have reason to value. The 2025 report applies the
  same people/choice framing under AI.
- Transport limit: this is a normative development framework, not a descriptive
  theory of every Human mechanism.

### OECD Well-being Framework

- URL: https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/measuring-well-being-and-progress.html
- HF0 use: current well-being is multidimensional and must be separated from
  resources that sustain future well-being. This supports outcome pluralism and
  temporal separation.
- Transport limit: policy measurement dimensions are not Human components.

## Rights / authority boundary

### Universal Declaration of Human Rights

- Authority: United Nations / OHCHR.
- URL: https://europe.ohchr.org/universal-declaration-human-rights
- HF0 use: Human research operates within a normative/legal world where dignity,
  equality and rights constrain what may be inferred or done to people. Accurate
  description or prediction does not itself create authority.
- Transport limit: HF0 does not derive its descriptive Human model from the
  UDHR; this source constrains normative and governance interpretation.

## Source-level synthesis

Across these families, no single decomposition survives as a complete Human
model. The strongest common pressure is instead toward a **multi-projection,
contextual and temporal account** that keeps at least these separations visible:

```text
organism/functioning != activity/participation != environment
experience != physiology != report != observed behaviour
agency != ownership != objective control
capacity/capability != one performance
resource != realized choice/outcome
current state != developmental history
person/self questions != merely biological-identity questions
prediction/description != normative authority
```

HF0 therefore treats disagreement among mature frameworks as positive evidence
against premature ontology closure.
