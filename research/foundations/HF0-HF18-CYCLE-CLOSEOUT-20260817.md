
# 28. FoundationReopenCondition

A frozen HF round may be reopened only when at least one of the following is concrete:

```text
A. repeated category error:
   the current distinction systematically causes wrong classifications across
   materially different domains;

B. incompatible primary evidence:
   strong new evidence directly falsifies a canonical factual/mechanistic claim;

C. missing neighboring distinction:
   multiple new cases fail for the same reason and cannot be represented without
   adding/revising a foundation concept;

D. contradiction across rounds:
   two frozen canonical claims cannot both hold under their stated qualifiers;

E. unusable consumer surface:
   applied projects repeatedly cannot map real cases without arbitrary hidden choices,
   and the problem is foundation-level rather than packaging/engineering;

F. normative authority leak:
   a descriptive foundation is repeatedly used to create unsupported authority or
   normative conclusions because its boundary is insufficient.
```

Not sufficient:

```text
interesting new paper
new terminology
one edge case
stylistic dissatisfaction
wanting a more complete taxonomy
another theory having different labels
```
