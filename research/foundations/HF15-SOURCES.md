# HF15 External Evidence and Source Ledger

## Evidence rule

HF15 uses three evidence classes and never collapses them:

```text
Empirical science
= evidence about cognition, experience-related markers, behavior, human standing
  judgments and legal/social effects

Legal primary authority
= evidence about institutionally recognized legal status/powers

Normative/philosophical primary argument
= competing theories of standing, interests, dignity/personhood or future-person
  relevance
```

None of these, alone, is treated as a universal proof of moral standing.

Sentience claims retain HF2:

```text
Experience != Report != Behaviour != Physiology
Phenomenon != Evidence != Inference != Theory
```

# 1. Perceived agency and experience are separable

## Gray, Gray & Wegner (2007)

- PMID: 17272713
- DOI: 10.1126/science.1134475
- Use: human mind-perception judgments formed two major dimensions, Agency and
  Experience, which related differently to moral judgments.

### HF15 inference

```text
PerceivedAgency != PerceivedExperience
MoralAgency and MoralPatiency must not be collapsed by psychology
```

Evidence role: human perception, not proof of actual mind structure or standing.

# 2. Moral typecasting

## Gray & Wegner (2009)

- PMID: 19254100
- DOI: 10.1037/a0013748
- Use: moral-agent and moral-patient roles showed reciprocal/inverse attribution
  patterns across seven studies.

### HF15 inference

```text
PerceivedMoralAgentRole != PerceivedMoralPatientRole
```

Again, this is a cognitive attribution result, not moral ontology.

# 14. Cognitive motor dissociation — large multicenter cohort

## Bodien et al. (2024)

- PMID: 39141852
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2400645
- Use: prospective six-center cohort of 353 adults with disorders of consciousness;
  task-based fMRI/EEG detected cognitive motor dissociation in 60 of 241 participants
  without observable command response (25%).

### HF15 inference

```text
NoObservableCommandResponse != NoVolitionalCognitiveEvidence
BehavioralDiagnosis != ExperienceState
```

This does not show every unresponsive patient is conscious.
