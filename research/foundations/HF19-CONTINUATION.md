# 6. NextFoundationAdmissionCondition

A future Human Foundations round is admissible only when all of the following are
materially satisfied:

1. **Repeated residual** — the same representational failure appears in multiple
   materially different contexts, not one edge case.
2. **Neighboring structure** — the failure is not already representable by selecting a
   different HF0–HF19 view/profile with explicit qualifiers.
3. **Decision/explanation value** — adding the distinction changes classification,
   explanation, prediction, decision quality, recovery, or error detection.
4. **Not engineering debt** — the failure is not merely missing packaging, tooling,
   schema exposure, documentation or consumer convenience.
5. **Not terminology churn** — a new label or literature tradition alone is
   insufficient.
6. **Evidence pressure** — primary/formal/field evidence shows that the missing
   distinction survives more than one model family or context.
7. **Boundary safety** — the proposed distinction does not silently collapse person,
   authority, welfare, legitimacy or moral standing into an economic variable.

Only after these gates are met should the new round be **named**.

# 8. Human × AI continuation rule

Do not ask “is AI labor or capital?” as if one label must win.

Ask independently:

```text
Which task role?
Who selects/reviews/overrides?
Who owns or merely accesses the system?
Is it a purchased service, asset service, tool, delegate or infrastructure component?
Which human tasks are substituted, complemented, created or removed?
Does it change current output only, or also human learning and organizational knowledge?
Which new dependencies/lock-in/control asymmetries appear?
```

```text
AIAssistance != HumanLearningGain
```
