# HF21 — Evidence Bridge

HF21 is extracted from completed HD2 rather than independently re-researched.

Canonical deep evidence owner:

[`../deep-foundations/HD2-SOURCES.md`](../deep-foundations/HD2-SOURCES.md)

The extraction is supported by converging evidence classes:

1. **cross-cultural facial meaning** — the same posed configuration does not map to one
   universal emotion/social-motive category;
2. **posed versus spontaneous expression** — recognition and dynamics differ materially,
   forcing expression-production/inference/classification separation;
3. **concept effects** — emotion concepts can alter perceptual representation of body
   expressions, so concepts are not merely post-perceptual labels;
4. **category + dimension neural structure** — valence/arousal and discrete-category
   information can coexist rather than requiring one winner;
5. **appraisal/event-feature structure** — inferred emotion distinctions require abstract
   situational/relational features beyond simple valence/arousal;
6. **amygdala lesion evidence** — intense internal fear/panic can occur despite bilateral
   amygdala damage, rejecting `amygdala = fear`;
7. **physiology/feeling dissociation** — comparable measured bodily perturbation can yield
   different reported fear/stress;
8. **interoceptive psychopathology** — adaptive precision/context sensitivity can change
   without reducing pathology to negative affect mean;
9. **developmental relational regulation** — caregiver presence can modulate child
   affective circuitry/regulation differently across development;
10. **mood-learning dynamics** — recent expectations/outcomes alter affect and induced mood
    can alter later learning;
11. **social emotion dissociation** — guilt and shame require relational/self-evaluative
    structure and are not one generic negative state;
12. **state perturbation** — sleep and pharmacological manipulations alter distinct neural,
    subjective and behavioural endpoints.

Representative primary sources are indexed in HD2-SOURCES by PMID. Consumers needing exact
study details should read that ledger rather than copying this compressed list.
