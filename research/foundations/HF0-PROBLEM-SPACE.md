# HF0 — Human Foundations Problem Space and Boundary Reconstruction

## 0. Status and research question

HF0 is the first Human Foundations round. It is a **foundation research record**,
not a personal model, intervention, platform specification, diagnosis, life
optimization system, or replacement for Human's problem-first applied research.

HF0 asks:

> **When Ordivon says `Human`, what different realities, roles and relations are
> being projected onto the same person, which inherited categories are actually
> fundamental, and what minimum distinctions must survive before later Human
> questions can be studied without category error?**

The purpose is not to finish an ontology. The purpose is to make bad reductions
harder and to expose the next foundation boundary.

---

# 1. Why HF0 is needed

The legacy Human System Atlas already did important first-pass work. It rejected
several reductions and retained Body, Cognition, Emotion/Motivation and
Capability because deleting any of them created explanatory failures.

That was sufficient for the applied problems then under study. It is not yet a
foundation result for three reasons.

## 1.1 The H0 categories mix different ontological roles

The current top-level list includes things that are not the same kind of thing:

```text
Body                   biological organization / substrate
Cognition              family of information processes
Emotion/Motivation     several valuation/regulation/action-allocation processes
Identity/Values        persistent self-organization + normative/evaluative content
Capability             relational ability under declared conditions
Behaviour              action/performance surface
Resources              available means / option conditions
Relationships          relations between persons
Institutions           external rule and power structures
Technology             artifacts / external capability
Time/History           trajectory coordinate and accumulated state
```

A useful index does not require these to be peers. A foundation model must not
silently treat them as peers merely because they appear at one indentation level.

## 1.2 Applied Human research already depends on distinctions H0 does not own

`HUMAN-ECON-001` requires opportunity, choice, resources, institutions,
commitment, quality of life, ownership and recovery.

`HUMAN-AI-001` requires independent versus joint capability, delegation,
verification, authorship, externalization, recovery and timescale coordination.

Game Foundations independently forced distinctions among Need, Value, Desire,
Preference, Goal, Commitment, agency, control and authorship. Media Foundations
independently forced distinctions among reality, observation, signal,
perception, recognition, awareness, inference, generation and action.

Human currently consumes many of those concepts without yet owning their Human
foundation boundaries.

## 1.3 Mature external frameworks disagree about the useful decomposition

WHO ICF, NIMH RDoC, developmental science, distributed cognition, agency/self
research, social-determinants work, capability/human-development frameworks and
personal-identity philosophy carve the person differently because they answer
different questions.

HF0 treats this disagreement as evidence against premature closure.

# 2. Method — Human adaptation of the Game / Media foundation paradigm

HF0 copies the **research algorithm**, not the object model, from Game and Media.

```text
surface categories
→ overloaded-term separation
→ competing projections
→ causal / relational role analysis
→ external triangulation
→ cross-context and boundary falsification
→ deletion / minimum-sufficient structure
→ reconnection to older layers
→ explicit UNKNOWN / rejected universals
→ next boundary exposed by the round
```

Human adds six mandatory audits:

```text
reflexivity
first-person subjectivity
normativity
heterogeneity
scale / timescale
authority / ethics
```

These are not extra Human components. They are research constraints.

---

# 3. Starting anti-assumption: `Human` is not one disciplinary object

A single concrete human can simultaneously be studied as:

```text
member of Homo sapiens
living organism
embodied sensorimotor system
experiencing subject
cognitive / learning system
valuing and motivated system
actor / controller
self-modeling individual
person with identity and continuity
relationship partner
member of groups and institutions
holder of rights, claims, resources and obligations
user of tools and external representations
participant in joint systems
historically developing trajectory
```

HF0 does **not** claim these are fifteen components inside a Human.

They are candidate **projections**. Some refer to internal organization, some to
processes, some to relations, some to normative status, some to social position,
and some to temporal identity.

The first durable HF0 principle is therefore:

```text
One Human reality may require multiple non-equivalent projections.
Projection utility does not make one projection the complete Human ontology.
```

# 4. Competing HF0 models

HF0 keeps several models alive long enough to expose what each loses.

## M1 — Compartment / system taxonomy

```text
Human = Body + Cognition + Emotion + Motivation + Identity + Capability + ...
```

### Strength

- navigable;
- useful for literature search and broad case coverage;
- prevents one-system reduction when categories are chosen carefully.

### Failure

It mixes structures, processes, relations, outcomes and contexts at one level.
Cross-cutting phenomena such as pain, learning, agency, skill, identity,
resource conversion and social participation do not stay inside one box.

**Disposition:** retain as a view index; reject as complete ontology.

## M2 — Biological organism

```text
Human = living Homo sapiens organism
```

### Strength

- gives a strong physical boundary and persistence substrate;
- explains viability, embodiment, development, ageing, injury and mortality;
- blocks theories that treat Human as disembodied software.

### Failure

Biological identity alone does not answer questions about experience, agency,
personhood, social role, meaning, rights, institutionally constrained options or
tool-mediated capability.

**Disposition:** indispensable projection; insufficient total model.

## M3 — Information-processing / cognitive agent

```text
observation → representation → belief/model → prediction → policy → action
```

### Strength

- powerful for perception, memory, learning, reasoning and decision problems;
- interfaces naturally with control theory, AI and Media perception research.

### Failure

It can make embodiment, affect, pain, social relation, normative commitment and
institutional power look like mere inputs to computation. Habit, reflex,
co-regulation and path-dependent development also resist a simple deliberative
agent loop.

**Disposition:** high-yield process projection; reject cognitive totalization.

## M4 — Preference / utility / choice actor

```text
preferences + constraints + beliefs → choice
```

### Strength

- compact for trade-offs, incentives, bargaining and resource allocation;
- useful when alternatives and consequences are explicit.

### Failure

Need, desire, preference, value, goal and commitment are not interchangeable.
Observed choice can be shaped by habit, coercion, missing options, institution,
misinformation, social obligation or impaired control. Preference satisfaction
also does not define well-being or moral value by itself.

**Disposition:** local decision model only; reject universal utility ontology.

## M5 — Experiencing subject / phenomenal self

```text
Human = locus of lived first-person experience
```

### Strength

- preserves pain, pleasure, affect, mineness, sense of agency and meaning;
- explains why third-person physiology cannot simply replace experience.

### Failure

Experience alone does not explain physiology, action capacity, social relation,
institutional position or long-run trajectory. First-person report is also an
observation channel with its own limits, not infallible access to every cause.

**Disposition:** irreducible evidence/projection family; insufficient total model.

## M6 — Social / relational / institutional person

```text
Human = role- and relation-bearing participant in social worlds
```

### Strength

- explains attachment, recognition, trust, language, norm, status, obligation,
power, permission, exclusion and collective action;
- prevents trait-only explanation of socially generated outcomes.

### Failure

Social location cannot replace organism, experience or individual cognition.
The same institution can affect people differently because of state, history,
capability and interpretation.

**Disposition:** indispensable for many outcomes; not the whole person.

## M7 — Situated / distributed human–environment system

```text
capability or cognition = Human × Tool × Representation × Environment × Others
```

### Strength

- explains real tool-mediated skill and Human×Agent capability;
- makes external memory, instruments, software, teammates and environments part
  of the causal system rather than decorative context.

### Failure

If expanded without a boundary, the person dissolves into every causal support.
A calculator can become part of a task system without becoming the person;
joint capability does not automatically become internal Human skill or personal
authority.

**Disposition:** retain situated-system analysis; preserve person/system
boundary explicitly.

## M8 — Developmental / historical process

```text
Human_t = f(prior state, development, exposure, action, relation, shock, learning)
```

### Strength

- explains plasticity, path dependence, sensitive periods, learning, ageing,
commitment and recovery;
- blocks static-profile essentialism.

### Failure

Trajectory language alone can obscure current structure, active mechanisms and
present constraints.

**Disposition:** time/history is constitutive for many claims, not merely metadata.

## M9 — Capability / freedom / well-being subject

```text
resources → real opportunities/capabilities → chosen functioning/outcomes
```

### Strength

- distinguishes means from real opportunities and outcomes;
- supports Human's existing outcome pluralism and autonomy concerns.

### Failure

This model contains normative commitments about what kinds of opportunity and
life matter. It is not a neutral biological or cognitive ontology.

**Disposition:** retain as normative/evaluative projection; never silently
substitute it for descriptive mechanism.

---

# 5. Result of the competing-model round

No candidate wins as `the Human model`.

More importantly, their failures are **orthogonal enough that combining them as
one flat list also does not solve the problem**.

HF0 therefore rejects:

```text
Human = one compartment taxonomy
Human = one optimization objective
Human = one agent loop
Human = one social role
Human = one subjective narrative
Human = one biological description
```

and retains:

```text
HumanReality
+ Question
+ Projection
+ Scale
+ Time
+ EvidencePosition
+ ModalRole
+ NormativeStatus
+ Boundary
```

as the minimum research grammar.

# 6. HF0 projection architecture

The projection architecture is deliberately **not a list of independent
subsystems**. A later question selects one or more projections and then builds
the minimum local causal model.

## P1 — Organism / embodiment

Questions about:

- viability and homeostasis;
- bodily structure and function;
- energy, sleep, pain, illness and recovery;
- sensation and movement;
- development, ageing and mortality.

Deletion failure: fatigue, injury, illness, sensorimotor access and biological
continuity become unexplained.

## P2 — Experience / subjectivity

Questions about:

- what is experienced;
- valence and felt state;
- pain, pleasure, effort and suffering;
- mineness, presence and first-person perspective.

Deletion failure: observable function is silently equated with lived state.

## P3 — Information / cognition / learning

Questions about:

- perception and attention;
- representation and belief;
- memory and knowledge;
- prediction, reasoning and imagination;
- metacognition and learning.

Deletion failure: information becomes action without a model of acquisition,
representation or update.

## P4 — Affect / valuation / motivation

Questions about:

- arousal and affect;
- salience and reward;
- need, desire and aversion;
- valuation and preference;
- goal selection, effort, persistence and disengagement.

Deletion failure: the direction and intensity of action are treated as if
information processing alone determines them.

HF0 explicitly does **not** retain `Emotion/Motivation` as one indivisible
primitive. It is a convenient H0 cluster awaiting later separation.

## P5 — Agency / action / control

Questions about:

- intention;
- action selection;
- motor, symbolic or delegated execution;
- control locus;
- action causality and feedback;
- authorship and intervention.

Deletion failure: intention, command, physical action and effect collapse into
one observed behaviour.

## P6 — Self / identity / personhood

Questions about:

- self-location and ownership;
- self-model and autobiographical organization;
- identity, roles and commitments;
- persistence through change;
- personhood, responsibility and normative status.

Deletion failure: biological continuity, felt selfhood, narrative identity,
social identity and legal/moral personhood are treated as synonyms.

## P7 — Relation / sociality / communication

Questions about:

- attachment and affiliation;
- communication and shared representation;
- trust, reciprocity and conflict;
- status, reputation and recognition;
- coordination and joint action.

Deletion failure: outcomes generated between people are incorrectly attributed
to isolated individual traits.

## P8 — Institution / norm / power / rights

Questions about:

- rules and roles;
- permission and prohibition;
- authority and obligation;
- incentives and sanctions;
- exclusion, access and power;
- rights and institutional recognition.

Deletion failure: feasible action is confused with physically possible action.

## P9 — Resource / environment / technology / situated capability

Questions about:

- time, money, information and material means;
- infrastructure and access;
- tools and external representations;
- AI and automation;
- environmental affordances/constraints;
- options, substitution and redundancy.

Deletion failure: realized capability is mistaken for an internal possession.

## P10 — Development / history / trajectory

Questions about:

- growth and maturation;
- learning and skill acquisition;
- accumulated exposure;
- habit and commitment;
- shocks, recovery and path dependence;
- ageing and decline.

Deletion failure: current measurements are treated as timeless essence.

### Critical interpretation

The ten projections are **navigation handles**. They do not imply ten natural
modules. Many phenomena require several simultaneously:

```text
pain = organism + experience + cognition + affect + action + context + history
skill = cognition + embodiment + practice/history + tool/context + action
agency = experience + cognition + intention/control + action/effect + social attribution
identity = organism/persistence + experience + memory + relations + institution + history
capability = actor state × learned structure × resource/tool/context × task demand
```

# 7. Statement types — H0's six kinds are not enough

H0 distinguished structure, state, capability, action, context and outcome, plus
observation.

HF0 retains that insight but expands it because several recurring category
errors still remain possible.

A Human claim should identify which of these it is actually about:

| Statement role | Example | Common collapse |
|---|---|---|
| identity / boundary | this continuing organism/person | identity = current properties |
| structure | sensory system, relationship network | structure = function |
| state | fatigue, fear, cash on hand | state = trait |
| process | encoding, appraisal, recovery | process = state label |
| disposition / trait | habitual response tendency | trait = immutable essence |
| capacity | maximum or latent ability under declared conditions | capacity = typical performance |
| situated capability | reliable achievement with declared supports | capability = internal skill |
| experience | pain, felt effort, sense of agency | experience = physiology/behaviour |
| intention / goal | selected or represented target | intention = action |
| action | movement, speech, command, delegation | action = effect |
| relation | trust, authority, ownership, dependence | relation = property of one node |
| context / opportunity | available tool, labour market, rule set | context = noise |
| outcome / consequence | task result, injury, income, well-being | outcome = decision quality |
| observation / evidence | test, self-report, sensor trace | measure = construct |
| norm / evaluation | desirable, fair, permitted, valuable | is = ought |

This is not a mandatory database schema. It is a category-error checklist.

# 8. Five coordinates every serious Human claim may need

HF0 finds that many disagreements are not about facts but about hidden
coordinates.

## 8.1 Scale

```text
molecular / cellular
organ / circuit
whole organism
experience / cognition
person / task
relationship / group
organization / institution
population / culture
historical / intergenerational
```

A relation proven at one scale does not automatically explain another.

## 8.2 Timescale

```text
momentary
episode
state over hours/days
habit / adaptation
skill / expertise
life stage
lifespan trajectory
intergenerational / historical
```

`current` and `persistent` are different claims.

## 8.3 Evidence position

```text
first-person report / elicitation
behaviour / task performance
physiological observation
artifact / trace / transaction
other-person / social observation
institutional record
population statistics
```

These channels can constrain one another without becoming equivalent.

## 8.4 Modal role

A claim may concern:

```text
actual state
possible action
capacity
available option
counterfactual capability
expected consequence
realized consequence
```

This is especially important for Human×tool and institutional analysis.

## 8.5 Normative status

```text
descriptive
predictive
causal
instrumental / decision-relevant
normative / evaluative
legal / institutional
```

A predictive relation cannot silently become a moral or legal entitlement.

# 9. Minimum dynamic grammar

HF0 rejects one universal causal graph, but recurring Human questions need a
small process vocabulary.

The most reusable current skeleton is:

```text
World / Context / History
        ↓ exposure, opportunity, constraint
Embodied Human State
        ↕ sensation / regulation / experience
Perception / Representation / Belief
        ↕ affect / valuation / salience
Need / Desire / Goal / Commitment
        ↓ selection / control
Intention / Policy
        ↓ body / language / tool / delegation / coordination
Action
        ↓
World Effect / Social Effect / Internal Effect
        ↓
Observation / Experience / Feedback
        ↓
Learning / Adaptation / Resource change / Relationship change
        ↓
Changed Human + Changed World
```

This is a **grammar**, not a requirement that every event passes through every
node. Reflexes, habits, automatic regulation, social co-regulation and
institutional effects can bypass deliberative stages.

A second durable loop is therefore:

```text
Human_t + World_t + Relations_t + Tools_t
→ Coupled Process
→ Human_(t+1) + World_(t+1) + Relations_(t+1) + Tool state_(t+1)
```

This keeps mutual change visible without claiming that Human boundaries vanish.

# 10. Cross-context falsifier matrix

HF0 deliberately uses cases that attack ordinary-language definitions.

| Case | Model attacked | Surviving distinction |
|---|---|---|
| sleeping or anaesthetized human | Human = active cognition/agency | current cognition/awareness != Human identity |
| infant before mature narrative self | Human = reflective self/explicit goals | person/organism/development precede mature self-model |
| transient fatigue or illness | performance = trait/capacity | state != capacity != typical performance |
| skilled expert denied required permission/access | ability = feasible action | internal skill != option/authority/situated capability |
| same person with calculator/AI/tool | external aid is irrelevant or fully internalized | situated capability != unaided skill |
| team produces result no member can alone | joint result = individual capability | joint-system capability != individual capability |
| coerced action | behaviour = preference/autonomy | action != endorsement != autonomous agency |
| habitual action without active deliberation | action requires explicit current goal | goal-directed history/habit != present explicit intention |
| false but sincerely held belief | belief = knowledge/truth | truth != belief != confidence |
| pain without matching simple nociceptive proxy | experience = physiological signal | pain/experience != one biomarker |
| nonverbal person/infant in pain | report absence = experience absence | reportability != experience existence |
| rubber-hand / body-ownership manipulation | body ownership = objective body boundary | experienced ownership != biological identity |
| action with altered feedback/automation | objective control = experienced agency | control/effect != sense of agency |
| migration or institutional rule change | outcome = stable personal trait | same actor + changed institution → changed option field |
| childhood adversity or training history | adult state is context-free | trajectory/history can become current structure |
| social support changes recovery | recovery is isolated internal trait | relations/resources can change functional trajectory |
| high income with low refusal power | resources = autonomy/well-being | means != capabilities/options != chosen outcomes |
| corporation called a legal person | person = biological human | legal personhood terminology != Human organism |
| autonomous software agent | agent = Human | agency/agent role != biological humanity |

The purpose is not that each row proves a complete model. Each row deletes one
otherwise tempting equivalence.

# 11. Reflexivity — Human observation can alter the object studied

Unlike a file format or passive physical signal, Human research can be
reflexive.

A measurement or label can become part of the later environment and self-model:

```text
test score
risk label
identity category
diagnosis
performance ranking
AI-generated interpretation
social reputation
```

may alter attention, expectation, treatment by others, motivation, opportunity or
future action.

Therefore:

```text
Observation_of_Human
may become
Intervention_on_Human
```

This does not mean every measurement has a large effect. It means HF0 cannot
assume measurement is causally inert.

# 12. First-person and third-person evidence

HF0 rejects both extremes:

```text
subjective report = infallible mechanism truth
```

and

```text
subjective experience = dispensable because physiology/behaviour is objective
```

The retained relation is:

```text
Experience
!= Report
!= Behaviour
!= Physiology
!= External Interpretation
```

These can be mutually constraining evidence channels.

IASP pain guidance provides a particularly strong boundary case: pain is a
personal experience, pain is not identical to nociception, and lack of verbal
communication does not by itself negate possible pain.

HF0 therefore retains **experience** as a first-class Human projection without
claiming introspective omniscience.

---

# 13. Description, causation and normativity must not collapse

Human research repeatedly crosses from mechanisms to questions such as:

- What matters?
- What is good for a person?
- What should be chosen?
- What is fair?
- What is autonomous?
- What is permitted?

These questions are not answered merely by observing what people currently do.

HF0 retains:

```text
Observed preference != welfare
Desire satisfaction != good life
Adaptation != desirability
Common behaviour != moral norm
Biological function != personal value
Prediction accuracy != decision authority
Capability != obligation to use that capability
```

UNDP capability/human-development frameworks and OECD well-being work are useful
here precisely because they make evaluative commitments explicit rather than
pretending income or one metric equals Human welfare.

# 14. Heterogeneity and distribution

HF0 rejects `AverageHuman` as a substitute for a person.

Variation can arise from:

```text
biology
age and development
learning and expertise
current body state
culture and language
relationship history
resources
institutions
technology
exposure and shocks
measurement context
```

A Human foundation claim should therefore state whether it proposes:

```text
species-level constraint
broad population regularity
distribution
context-dependent relation
state-dependent relation
development-dependent relation
individual-specific relation
```

Population evidence can be highly informative without determining one
individual's exact state or causal response.

# 15. Deletion audit of the legacy H0 Atlas

HF0 does not delete H0. It changes the interpretation of its categories.

| H0 category | HF0 disposition | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Body | **retain, reposition** | indispensable organism/embodiment projection; not merely one peer module |
| Cognition | **retain, split-capable** | high-yield family of information/learning processes; not a single box |
| Emotion & Motivation | **retain as temporary cluster, must split later** | affect, arousal, valuation, desire, effort and goal processes occupy different roles |
| Identity & Values | **reject as one joint primitive** | self/identity/persistence and value/commitment are different foundation problems |
| Capability | **retain, reclassify as relational** | capability depends on actor, condition, task, tools/resources and context; not purely internal subsystem |
| Behaviour | **retain as action/performance/observation surface** | behaviour is not a peer internal system and does not uniquely reveal intention |
| Economy & Resources | **retain as situated means/option field** | means are external/relational conditions, not Human essence |
| Relationships | **strengthen** | some Human states/capabilities/outcomes are relational or co-regulated |
| Institutions & Environment | **strengthen, keep boundary explicit** | rules, permission, power and exposure can causally change options/outcomes |
| Technology & External Capability | **strengthen as coupling projection** | tools alter real capability but do not automatically become internal skill or personal identity |
| Time & History | **promote from cross-cutting note to required coordinate** | development and path dependence can become present causal structure |

The strongest reclassification is:

```text
Capability is not one internal Human organ.
Capability is a relation between an actor and a class of outcomes
under declared states, supports, constraints and environments.
```

# 17. What HF0 does *not* establish

HF0 does not establish:

- a complete definition of personhood;
- a metaphysical theory of consciousness;
- a final theory of personal identity;
- a universal hierarchy of Human needs;
- a universal value function;
- that every capability should include every tool/context;
- a precise boundary for extended cognition or embodied tools;
- a complete social ontology;
- a causal graph connecting molecules to institutions;
- universal effect sizes for any Human relation;
- a list of traits that every person should maximize;
- that one individual's first-person report overrides all other evidence;
- that population evidence is irrelevant to individuals;
- any authority to diagnose, rank, manipulate or optimize a person.

# 18. Minimum HF0 retained model

After deletion pressure, the smallest model that still survives the full
falsifier matrix is not a subsystem list.

It is:

```text
HumanReality
  = a persisting, developing embodied human individual
    who can have first-person experience,
    acquire and transform information,
    be affected and value/seek/avoid,
    act directly or through relations/tools,
    participate in social and institutional worlds,
    and be changed by the history of those couplings.
```

For research, that reality is queried through:

```text
Projection
× Scale
× Timescale
× EvidencePosition
× ModalRole
× NormativeStatus
× Boundary
```

This sentence is intentionally broad and defeasible. It is **not** a sufficient
criterion for personhood, consciousness, agency or moral status.

The stronger operational conclusion is:

> **Do not ask which box a Human phenomenon belongs to before asking what kind of
> claim it is, at what scale and time, from which evidence position, relative to
> which boundary, and whether the claim is descriptive or normative.**

# 20. Exact boundary exposed by HF0

HF0 repeatedly relies on terms whose boundary is still unresolved:

```text
Human
Organism
Body
Subject
Self
Person
Actor
Agent
Control Locus
Tool-extended system
```

The cross-context falsifiers make this the first unavoidable neighbor:

- a sleeping or anaesthetized Human remains the same Human despite altered
  awareness and agency;
- an infant challenges reflective-self definitions;
- body-ownership illusions separate biological body from experienced mineness;
- software can be an Agent without being Human;
- legal `person` can be used beyond biological humans;
- tools can become constitutive of task capability without obviously becoming
  part of personal identity;
- personal identity persists through some large state changes, but exactly which
  continuity relation matters depends on the question.

Therefore HF0 does **not** choose Body physiology as HF1 merely because Body was
H0's first system.

The next round is forced by the boundary problem itself.

# 21. HF0 synthesis

HF0 began with the question `What is Human?` and found that the most useful first
answer is not a definition but a research discipline.

The old H0 map survives as a useful functional index, but not as a proven set of
peer primitives. Body remains indispensable. Cognition remains indispensable for
many questions. Emotion/Motivation must be separable. Capability becomes
explicitly relational. Behaviour becomes an action/performance surface. Social,
institutional and technological relations move from peripheral context toward
first-class causal projections. Time/history becomes constitutive rather than
metadata. First-person experience receives its own evidence boundary.

The deepest HF0 compression is:

```text
Human is not well modeled by choosing one privileged disciplinary projection.

One Human reality
+ multiple non-equivalent projections
+ explicit scale/time/evidence/normative/boundary coordinates
+ minimum local causal model
+ cross-context falsification
```

and the strongest anti-collapse is:

```text
description of a Human
!=
prediction of a Human
!=
explanation of a Human
!=
evaluation of a Human
!=
authority over a Human
```

HF0 is complete when this structure is durable enough to begin the boundary
round without precommitting the rest of the Human Foundations sequence.
