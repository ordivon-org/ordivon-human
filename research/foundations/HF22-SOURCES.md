# HF22 — Evidence Bridge

HF22 is extracted from completed HD3 rather than independently re-researched.

Canonical deep evidence owner:

[`../deep-foundations/HD3-SOURCES.md`](../deep-foundations/HD3-SOURCES.md)

Evidence classes justifying the extraction:

1. **relationship-specific attachment/co-regulation** — intervention, longitudinal and
   adult dyadic-stress evidence shows persistent caregiving/partner relations can alter
   regulation and recovery;
2. **current interaction versus persistent relation** — attachment, friendship, care and
   trust-history effects persist across gaps in interaction;
3. **relation versus representation** — secure-base/relation representations are shaped by
   relational history but remain distinct from the relationship itself;
4. **asymmetry/multiplexity** — attachment, trust, dependence, care and role relations need
   not be reciprocal or collapse into one dyad type;
5. **kinship/family boundaries** — social kinship, genetic relatedness and household
   membership can dissociate;
6. **subjective versus objective connection** — loneliness and social isolation are
   non-identical;
7. **cross-cultural relationship representation** — recurrent relation dimensions coexist
   with cultural/historical variation;
8. **Human×AI asymmetry** — Human closeness/connection/trust toward AI can be experimentally
   induced or label-sensitive without evidence for reciprocal machine feeling.
