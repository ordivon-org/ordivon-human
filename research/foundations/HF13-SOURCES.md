# HF13 External Evidence and Source Ledger

## Evidence rule

HF13 is a technical foundation round. Empirical claims are grounded primarily in
primary experiments, longitudinal studies or natural experiments. Reviews are not
used as substitutes for direct evidence when a primary falsifier is available.

The ledger deliberately separates:

```text
actual behavior
empirical/descriptive expectation
normative/injunctive expectation
personal normative judgment
moral evaluation
formal rule/legal status
compliance
sanction/enforcement
status/prestige/dominance
power/authority/legitimacy
```

because the same observed behavior can be generated by materially different social
structures.

# 1. Decentralized convention emergence

## Centola & Baronchelli (2015)

- PMID: 25646462
- PMCID: PMC4343158
- DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1418838112
- Use: large experimental populations interacting locally developed shared naming
  conventions without central leadership or an explicit population-wide goal;
  network topology strongly shaped local versus global convergence.

### HF13 inference

```text
Convention != ExplicitAgreement
ConventionEmergence != CentralPlannerRequirement
NetworkStructure affects ConventionDynamics
```

# 5. Long-run norm dynamics and empirical/normative expectations

## Szekely et al. (2021)

- PMID: 34526490
- DOI: 10.1038/s41467-021-25734-w
- Use: 30-day collective-risk experiment measured empirical and normative
  expectations daily, manipulated conditional expectations, and found stronger risk
  produced stronger cooperative norms that were more resistant to later erosion.

### HF13 inference

```text
EmpiricalExpectation != NormativeExpectation
CurrentPayoffStructure != CurrentNormStrength
NormState is history dependent
```

## Marshall et al. (2024/2025 publication cycle)

- PMID: 39531693
- DOI: 10.1037/pspa0000416
