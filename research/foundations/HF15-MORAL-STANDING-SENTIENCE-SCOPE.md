# HF15 — Moral Standing, Moral Agency, Moral Patienthood, Sentience, Interests, Vulnerability, Dignity and Scope of Concern

## 0. Status and question

HF14 reconstructed normative evaluation but repeatedly reached a bearer problem:

```text
Who can be harmed?
Who can have welfare?
Who can hold rights?
Who can bear duties?
Who can be responsible?
Who deserves direct moral consideration?
```

HF15 therefore asks:

> **What properties, relations or statuses make an entity eligible for direct moral
> consideration, moral protection or moral responsibility, and how should evidence
> and uncertainty about those conditions be represented?**

HF15 does not choose species, life, sentience, rationality, interests, dignity,
relations or legal recognition as the unique moral criterion by fiat.

---

# 1. Standing is not one scalar

HF15 rejects:

```text
MoralStanding(E) = one universal number
```

as foundational ontology.

Standing claims can refer to different normative roles.

---

# 2. Direct versus derivative moral relevance

An entity can matter because of effects on another bearer.

Example:

```text
historic building
```

may matter because humans value it.

That differs from claiming the building itself is a welfare bearer.

Thus:

```text
DerivativeMoralRelevance != DirectMoralStanding
```

---

# 3. Moral standing

Working framework-relative relation:

```text
MoralStanding_F(E,K,C)
= status of entity E as an eligible direct bearer/target of normative consideration
  of kind K under normative framework F and context C
```

K may include:

```text
welfare consideration
protection from harm
claim-right eligibility
respect/dignity
political membership
responsibility/duty eligibility
```

---

# 4. Standing is not identical treatment

Two entities can have standing while different rights/duties are appropriate because
of different capacities and relations.

Thus:

```text
MoralStanding != IdenticalTreatment
```

---

# 5. Equal standing is not identical rights bundle

```text
EqualMoralStanding != IdenticalRights
```

An infant and an adult can have equal basic standing while differing in voting,
contracting or responsibility capacities under many frameworks.

---

# 6. Standing is not claim weight

Being inside the moral domain does not settle how a claim competes with another.

Thus:

```text
InMoralScope != ClaimWeightSolved
```

---

# 7. Standing is not moral worth totality

Terms such as `worth`, `status`, `standing`, `importance` and `considerability` are
often conflated.

HF15 requires explicit operational meaning.

---

# 8. Standing is framework-relative where theory is contested

Use:

```text
Standing_F
```

rather than hiding normative theory inside a bare label.

---

# 9. Perceived standing

```text
PerceivedMoralStanding(H,E,K,C)
```


# 210. Competing standing models

## M1 — species membership / humanity

### Strength

Tracks strong human moral practice and equal-protection intuitions across variation in
individual capacity.

### Pressure

Species-boundary priority persists in human judgments even when capacities are
matched; empirical prevalence does not establish normative relevance.

### Disposition

Retain as explicit normative model family, reject hidden biological identity.

## M2 — life / biocentrism

### Strength

Extends concern beyond sentient beings and can explain value assigned to living
systems.

### Pressure

Must distinguish direct welfare patienthood from derivative/ecological value and
explain bacteria/plants/collectives.

### Disposition

Retain as competing family.

## M3 — sentience / valenced experience

### Strength

Directly connects standing to capacity for suffering/enjoyment and handles many
animals/infants without high agency.

### Pressure

Sentience evidence is difficult; sentience alone does not automatically specify all
rights, dignity, political membership or responsibility.

### Disposition

Strong patienthood candidate, not foundation identity.

## M4 — interest-based standing

### Strength

Can include temporally extended welfare, goals, needs and future persons without
requiring current report.

### Pressure

What constitutes an interest is theory-laden; functional goals can be mistaken for
welfare interests.

### Disposition

Retain with typed interests.

## M5 — rational/moral agency/personhood

### Strength

Explains responsibility, reciprocity, autonomous claims and political membership.

### Pressure

Infants, severe impairment and many animals challenge use as necessary condition for
all patienthood/standing.

### Disposition

Retain for agency/personhood roles, reject as universal patienthood criterion.

## M6 — capacity to value

### Strength

Jaworska-style approaches preserve agency-relevant valuing under cognitive decline
without requiring ideal rationality.

### Pressure

Very young infants and some unconscious/sentient patients may lack this capacity yet
remain plausible patients.

### Disposition

Retain as one agency/respect route.

## M7 — dignity/status

### Strength

Can ground equal protection without ranking humans by current cognitive capacity.

### Pressure

Dignity's basis and scope are contested; label can conceal species or metaphysical
assumptions.

### Disposition

Require framework-explicit use.

## M8 — relational/care/dependence

### Strength

Captures vulnerability, social embeddedness and obligations arising from dependence
and care.

### Pressure

Must explain isolated, distant or socially unrecognized patients.

### Disposition

Retain as relational reason family, not standing totality.

## M9 — legal/institutional recognition

### Strength

Precisely determines enforceable legal capacities.

### Pressure

Corporations and unjust/exclusionary legal histories show legal status cannot equal
moral patienthood/truth.

### Disposition

Keep legal standing separate.

## M10 — anthropomorphic/social recognition

### Failure

Robot/animal standing judgments are manipulable by appearance, framing, food
categorization and mind attribution.

### Disposition

Treat as psychology of standing attribution, not normative criterion.

## M11 — pluralist/layered standing

### Strength

Allows welfare patienthood, agency, legal personhood, dignity and political membership
to vary independently.

### Cost

Requires explicit conflict/weighting/aggregation rules.

### Disposition

HF15 minimum representation favors typed profiles over one scalar, without declaring
pluralism itself the final moral theory.

## M12 — uncertainty/precaution as standing

### Failure

Precaution is a decision policy under uncertainty, not a truth condition.

### Disposition

Separate evidence, standing credence and action threshold.
