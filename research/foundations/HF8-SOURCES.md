# HF8 External Evidence and Source Ledger

## Evidence rule

HF8 studies representational and epistemic status rather than memory persistence
alone. Each source is interpreted by separating:

```text
representational vehicle / implementation
content / referent
memory/access route
current endorsement / belief
objective truth / task criterion
source/provenance
confidence
capability / transfer
```

No behavioral or neural result is allowed to settle a philosophical definition of
`knowledge` or `representation` by itself.

---

# 1. Semantic knowledge without normal episodic recollection

## Tulving, Hayman & Macdonald (1991) — K.C.

- PMID: 1832430
- DOI: 10.1037//0278-7393.17.4.595
- HF8 use: severely amnesic K.C. acquired some new semantic facts and retained them
  for long intervals despite profound episodic-memory impairment.

## Baddeley, Vargha-Khadem & Mishkin (2001) — developmental amnesia

- PMID: 11371313
- DOI: 10.1162/08989290151137403
- HF8 use: patient Jon developed normal-range intelligence/language/general
  knowledge despite severe episodic-memory impairment; recognition/recollective
  phenomenology showed a different profile.

## Martins et al. (2004) — prospective semantic learning in childhood amnesia

- PMID: 15076772
- DOI: 10.1097/00001756-200402090-00033
- HF8 use: two amnesic children acquired new concepts despite major episodic
  disturbance.

## Gabrieli, Cohen & Corkin (1988) — H.M. boundary evidence

- PMID: 3377896
- DOI: 10.1016/0278-2626(88)90027-9
- HF8 use: H.M. showed severe impairment in postmorbid semantic acquisition,
  preventing the opposite overclaim that semantic learning is independent of
  medial-temporal/episodic systems in all cases.

## O'Kane et al. (2004) — residual semantic learning in H.M.

- PMID: 15224979
- HF8 use: despite profound amnesia, H.M. showed limited but meaningful postmorbid
  famous-person semantic knowledge with semantic cue support.

### HF8 inference

```text
SemanticKnowledge != EpisodicRecollection
```

but also:

```text
SemanticLearning is not universally independent of episodic/MTL mechanisms
```

The relation is architecture- and task-dependent.

# 2. Knowing-how versus knowing-that

HF8 inherits HF7 skill-memory evidence.

## Cohen & Squire (1980)

- PMID: 7414331
- DOI: 10.1126/science.7414331
- HF8 use: mirror-reading skill acquisition despite declarative-memory impairment.

## Gabrieli et al. (1993) — mirror tracing including H.M.

- PMID: 8136066
- HF8 use: skill learning/retention can coexist with poor explicit memory for the
  training episode.

### HF8 inference

```text
KnowingHow_D != KnowingThat(P)
```

as operational capability surfaces.

HF8 does not claim the philosophical categories are absolutely independent or
exhaustive.

# 3. Source monitoring: content versus provenance

## Johnson, Hashtroudi & Lindsay (1993) — Source Monitoring Framework

- PMID: 8346328
- DOI: 10.1037/0033-2909.114.1.3
- HF8 use: source judgments are inferential attributions based on qualities of
  mental experience, prior knowledge/beliefs and context; source attribution can
  fail separately from item/content memory.

## Lindsay & Johnson (1989) — eyewitness suggestibility/source

- PMID: 2725271
- DOI: 10.3758/bf03198473
- HF8 use: orienting participants to source-monitoring information changes
  suggestibility/source attribution, showing provenance judgment is an active
  cognitive operation rather than a content tag guaranteed by memory.

## Hashtroudi, Johnson & Chrosniak (1989) — aging/source monitoring

- PMID: 2803603
- DOI: 10.1037//0882-7974.4.1.106
- HF8 use: different source-discrimination tasks show different aging effects.

### HF8 inference

```text
ContentAvailable != SourceKnown
SourceAttribution != HistoricalSource by definition
```

Provenance is a separate epistemic variable.

# 4. Concepts: prototype, exemplar and rule-like representations

## Bowman & Zeithamova (2018)

- PMID: 29437891
- HF8 use: concept-learning behavior and model-based fMRI supported prototype-like
  generalization in many participants under that task, with VMPFC/anterior
  hippocampal correlates.

## Bowman & Zeithamova (2020)

- PMID: 32105147
- PMCID: PMC7363543
- DOI: 10.1037/xlm0000824
- HF8 use: training-set coherence changed category learning/generalization and the
  relative fit/use of prototype versus exemplar strategies; recognition of
  individual examples did not mirror generalization in a simple way.

## Mack et al. / rule and exemplar transfer (2023)

- PMID: 36638230
- HF8 use: rule-based and exemplar-based transfer recruited distinguishable
  behavioral/neural systems under a category-learning paradigm.

## Nosofsky et al. natural-science category learning

- PMID: 29058939
- DOI: 10.1037/xge0000369
- HF8 use: an exemplar-memory model gave strong accounts of classification and
  generalization in a high-dimensional rock-category task.

## age and prototype/exemplar generalization

- PMID: 36222646
- PMCID: PMC10074256
- DOI: 10.1037/pag0000714
- HF8 use: young and older adults could achieve comparable broad categorization
  while differing in inferred prototype/exemplar strategy weighting.

## older-adult prototype representations (2026)

- PMID: 41763160
- HF8 use: current evidence that VMPFC prototype-related representations can
  support concept generalization in both younger and older adults; useful against
  one age-invariant mechanism claim.

### HF8 inference

```text
Concept != PrototypeOnly
Concept != ExemplarSetOnly
Concept != RuleOnly
```

Representational strategy is task-, training- and individual-sensitive.

# 5. Schema: facilitation and bias

## van Kesteren et al. / schema-dependent encoding

- PMID: 23770537
- HF8 use: prior-knowledge congruency improved later memory while changing relative
  mPFC/MTL recruitment.

## Brod et al. / experimentally learned spatial schema

- PMID: 25505319
- HF8 use: experimentally established schemas altered learning/retrieval of new
  associations.

## Brod et al. / real-world medical knowledge acquisition

- PMID: 27488631
- HF8 use: growth of medical-domain knowledge changed later encoding and neural
  recruitment for schema-related material.

## assimilation of novel information into schema (2022)

- PMID: 35710624
- HF8 use: overlearned fictitious schemas facilitated later learning/consolidation
  of related novel information.

## clinicians' causal-coherence memory distortion

- PMID: 29985030
- DOI: 10.1037/xlm0000589
- HF8 use: domain schemas/causal expectations can bias remembered cases toward
  greater causal coherence.

## congruence and false recollection

- PMID: 17891682
- HF8 use: congruent suggested information was more likely to be accepted and to
  acquire phenomenological similarity to perceived information.

### HF8 inference

```text
SchemaBenefit can coexist with SchemaBias
Schema != Truth
SchemaCongruence != Veridicality
```

A schema is useful structure, not epistemic authority.

# 6. Mental models and reasoning

## Johnson-Laird (2010)

- PMID: 20956326
- PMCID: PMC2972923
- DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1012933107
- HF8 use: mental-model theory proposes reasoning over represented possibilities
  and predicts systematic errors when reasoners fail to represent all relevant
  possibilities.

## arithmetic word-problem mental-model evidence

- PMID: 19900669
- HF8 use: recognition/transfer patterns supported construction of a relational
  situation representation beyond literal wording in arithmetic problems.

## current modal-reasoning synthesis (2025)

- PMID: 39012580
- PMCID: PMC11836090
- DOI: 10.3758/s13423-024-02518-z
- HF8 use: contemporary formulation of reasoning over possibilities; retained as a
  theory family, not as proof that every Human representation is a mental model.

### HF8 inference

```text
MentalModel != AllRepresentation
MentalModel != BeliefSet
```

Mental models are a candidate form for possibility/relational reasoning.

# 7. Understanding and the illusion of explanatory depth

## Rozenblit & Keil (2002)

- PMID: 21442007
- PMCID: PMC3062901
- DOI: 10.1207/s15516709cog2605_1
- HF8 use: people systematically overestimate explanatory depth for mechanisms/
  systems; attempting detailed explanation reduces self-assessed understanding.

## Mills & Keil (2004)

- PMID: 14698687
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jecp.2003.09.003
- HF8 use: developmental evidence that explanatory-depth calibration differs by
  age and knowledge type.

## Fisher & Keil (2016) — curse of expertise

- PMID: 26369299
- DOI: 10.1111/cogs.12280
- HF8 use: domain expertise can coexist with miscalibrated explanatory confidence.

## Gaviria & Corredor (2025)

- PMID: 39231853
- PMCID: PMC12053339
- DOI: 10.3758/s13421-024-01616-6
- HF8 use: current evidence linking explanatory-depth illusion to individual
  memory/cognitive-load differences.

### HF8 inference

```text
FeelingOfUnderstanding != DemonstratedUnderstanding
Expertise != PerfectMetacognitiveCalibration
ExplanationProbe can reveal missing structure
```

# 8. Explanation, abstraction and transfer

## Rittle-Johnson (2006)

- PMID: 16460521
- DOI: 10.1111/j.1467-8624.2006.00852.x
- HF8 use: self-explanation/instruction manipulations affected mathematical
  learning and transfer in children under the study conditions.

## Matthews & Rittle-Johnson (2009)

- PMID: 18937952
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jecp.2008.08.004
- HF8 use: conceptual versus procedural instruction produced different explanation
  quality/conceptual outcomes; self-explanation prompts were not uniformly
  beneficial.

## Williams et al. (2022) — explanation and hypothesis generation

- PMID: 35344899
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cognition.2022.105100
- HF8 use: explanation promoted generation of broader/abstract hypotheses in a
  category-learning task but did not improve hypothesis evaluation.

## conditional-reasoning transfer

- PMID: 2927318
- HF8 use: explanation effects on transfer depended on initial problem type and
  target relation.

### HF8 inference

```text
Explanation != Understanding
ExplanationBenefit_D != ExplanationBenefit_E
Explanation can alter representation/hypothesis generation without guaranteeing evaluation
```

# 9. Belief updating, correction and source reliability

## belief updating/source reliability (2025)

- PMID: 39986181
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cognition.2025.106090
- HF8 use: across experiments, participants adjusted belief updating to source
  reliability/trustworthiness/expertise and discounted retracted information under
  the reported conditions.

## belief regression after correction

- PMID: 36174261
- PMCID: PMC10710738
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cognition.2022.105276
- HF8 use: memory for correction strongly predicted later belief regression;
  remembering a correction and believing it are related but nonidentical processes.

## corrective feedback and belief

- PMID: 27762579
- DOI: 10.1037/xlm0000322
- HF8 use: misconception revision was more likely when corrective feedback was
  believed and when supporting explanation was provided.

## evaluating belief before correction

- PMID: 32302243
- DOI: 10.1080/09658211.2020.1752731
- HF8 use: actively evaluating a mistaken belief before retraction altered later
  correction effectiveness in the experiments.

## backfire reliability analysis

- PMID: 35130012
- PMCID: PMC9283209
- DOI: 10.1037/xge0001131
- HF8 use: apparent backfire rates were strongly related to measurement/reliability
  and were not a robust universal response to correction.

## misinformation reminders / encoding (2025)

- PMID: 40618302
- HF8 use: reminder-based corrections improved correction recognition and belief
  updating while eye tracking implicated attention to corrective details.

### HF8 inference

```text
Belief != Memory
BeliefUpdate != MemoryUpdateOnly
SourceReliability matters
Correction != UniversalBackfire
```

# 10. Knowledge can be available yet neglected

## Fazio et al. (2015) — knowledge does not protect against illusory truth

- PMID: 26301795
- HF8 use: repeated statements received higher truth judgments even when
  participants demonstrated relevant factual knowledge; available knowledge was
  not always used in current judgment.

### HF8 inference

```text
KnowledgeAvailable != KnowledgeUsedInJudgment
```

and:

```text
Belief/Judgment_t depends on retrieval/fluency/context as well as retained knowledge
```

This reconnects HF7 retrieval to HF8 belief.

# 11. Internet search: access versus internal knowledge

## Fisher, Goddu & Keil (2015)

- PMID: 25822461
- DOI: 10.1037/xge0000070
- HF8 use: online explanation search inflated participants' estimates of their own
  internal knowledge across multiple experiments.

## Fisher, Smiley & Grillo (2022)

- PMID: 33557708
- DOI: 10.1080/09658211.2021.1882501
- HF8 use: participants who searched online while studying later performed worse
  on internal learning assessments despite equal or higher confidence than those
  directly presented the same information.

## Ward (2021)

- PMID: 34686595
- PMCID: PMC8612631
- DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2105061118
- HF8 use: internet use increased confidence in future unaided knowledge/memory,
  consistent with misattribution of external access to internal capacity.

### HF8 inference

```text
AccessibleExternally != KnownInternally
SearchSuccess != InternalUnderstanding
ExternalRetrievability can distort self-assessment of knowledge
```

# 12. Knowledge division across human/AI partners

## Fisher & Oppenheimer (2021)

- PMID: 33829826
- DOI: 10.1037/xap0000310
- HF8 use: participants working with knowledgeable human or AI partners
  overestimated later unaided performance; misattribution was reduced when credit
  attribution was clearer.

### HF8 inference

```text
TeamKnowledge != IndividualKnowledge
JointSuccess != IndividualCompetence
```

This is the epistemic analogue of HF1/HF6 capability boundaries.

# 13. Generative AI: performance versus learning/understanding

## Bastani et al. (2025) — high-school mathematics field experiment

- PMID: 40560616
- PMCID: PMC12232635
- DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2422633122
- HF8 use: GPT-4 access strongly improved assisted practice performance. Under the
  standard GPT-like condition, students later performed worse on an unassisted
  exam than control students; a scaffolded/hint-oriented tutor mitigated the
  learning harm.

## medical-student custom GPT RCT

- PMID: 39425996
- DOI: 10.1080/0142159X.2024.2413023
- HF8 use: a structured AI-supported self-directed-learning intervention improved
  measured self-directed learning and critical-thinking outcomes in that medical
  education design.

## ChatGPT versus expert feedback clinical reasoning RCT

- PMID: 39656920
- HF8 use: ChatGPT-generated versus expert feedback produced broadly similar later
  clinical-reasoning outcomes overall, with task-specific differences.

## LLM-assisted PBL nursing RCT (2025)

- PMID: 40261697
- HF8 use: structured LLM-assisted PBL produced modestly higher measured critical
  thinking than traditional PBL under that experiment.

### HF8 inference

```text
AIAssistedPerformance != InternalizedKnowledge
AIUse != LearningHarm by definition
AIUse != LearningGain by definition
ToolDesign + task + learner strategy + delayed independent test matter
```

# 14. Current online-search dependence evidence

## Bittner & Storm (2026)

- PMID: 41014960
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cognition.2025.106329
- HF8 use: online search increased subsequent propensity to search again without a
  corresponding drop in internal-confidence judgments under the experiments.

### HF8 inference

Repeated external knowledge access can change retrieval strategy/dependence even
without a conscious judgment that internal knowledge is weak.

# 15. Source-level synthesis

The strongest cross-source separations are:

```text
RepresentationVehicle != RepresentationalContent
RepresentedContent != Referent/WorldTruth
Representation != Belief
Representation != Knowledge

SemanticKnowledge != EpisodicRecollection
KnowingHow_D != KnowingThat(P)
ContentAvailable != SourceKnown

Concept != PrototypeOnly
Concept != ExemplarOnly
Concept != RuleOnly
Schema != Concept
Schema != Truth
SchemaBenefit can coexist with SchemaBias
MentalModel != AllRepresentation

FeelingOfUnderstanding != DemonstratedUnderstanding
Explanation != Understanding
Recall != Understanding
Expertise != PerfectSelfKnowledge

Belief != Memory
BeliefStrength/Confidence != Truth
KnowledgeAvailable != KnowledgeUsedInJudgment
SourceReliability != SourceIdentity

ExternalAccess != InternalKnowledge
JointKnowledge != IndividualKnowledge
AIAssistedPerformance != InternalizedUnderstanding
```

The repeated residual across mental models, explanation, belief updating and
conceptual transfer is **inference**: representations become useful only because a
Human can derive, compare, revise, simulate and act on relations not explicitly
presented. HF8 can type the representational/epistemic objects, but it cannot fully
explain the transformations among them. That residual points to reasoning,
inference, causality, counterfactuals, judgment and problem solving as the next
boundary.
