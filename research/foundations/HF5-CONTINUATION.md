## Homeostasis / allostasis

HF5 retains:

```text
Homeostasis
= maintaining viability-relevant variables/relations within functional bounds
  through coordinated regulation

Allostasis
= context-sensitive and often anticipatory changes in regulatory activity,
  targets or effectors that prepare for changing demands
```

Neither is accepted as one complete Human ontology.

# HF6 — Adaptation, Learning, Plasticity, Habituation, Sensitization, Resilience, Development and Aging

## HF6 starting questions

1. What counts as adaptation rather than transient compensation?
2. What is learned versus merely physiologically changed?
3. What is plasticity relative to learning, skill and adaptation?
4. How does habituation differ from fatigue, extinction and tolerance?
5. How does sensitization differ from attention/vigilance and pathology?
6. Is resilience resistance, recovery, adaptation, preserved function, or a typed
   family of outcomes?
7. How can an acute beneficial adaptation create chronic cost?
8. How do developmental windows change what can be learned/adapted?
9. What happens to plasticity, reserve and recovery with aging?
10. How should path dependence be represented in Human capability and identity?
11. How should reversible, slowly reversible and effectively irreversible changes
    be separated?
12. When does training change internal capacity versus only task familiarity or
    strategy?
13. What does recovery mean when the post-recovery system is better/worse/different
    rather than restored?
14. What next foundation emerges from persistent change across time?

## Candidate HF6 falsifiers

- HPA habituation to repeated stress;

## Do not precommit

HF5 does not establish that:

- all adaptation is beneficial;
- all plasticity is learning;
- all learning is conscious;
- habituation is mere fatigue;
- resilience is a fixed trait;
- recovery returns the same system;
- development is monotonic improvement;
- aging is uniform decline;
- plasticity disappears in adulthood;
- one biomarker measures resilience;
- increased performance proves durable internal capability.
