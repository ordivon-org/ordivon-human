## Main findings

### 1. The object/method distinction is structural

The project now separates:

```text
research/h0/  human systems and relations
methods/      measurement, inference, and validation
```

This is not merely a directory rename. It prevents method questions—causal inference, N-of-1 design, data collection, or AI evaluation—from becoming the project’s substitute object.

### 2. Four P0 systems survive first deletion tests

- **Body** is necessary to explain energy, sleep, sensation, movement, illness, stress physiology, recovery, development, and ageing.
- **Cognition** is necessary to explain representation, attention, memory, learning, reasoning, control, and metacognition rather than merely labelling behaviour.
- **Emotion and Motivation** are necessary to explain salience, value, threat, reward, effort, persistence, stress, approach, avoidance, and regulation.
- **Capability** is necessary to distinguish potential, current state, knowledge, independent skill, situated ability, joint-system performance, transfer, adaptation, and recovery.

Their internal subdivisions are not frozen.

### 3. Capability is the main integration surface

P0 shows that Capability cannot be inferred from cognition or one task alone:

```text
Capability
≈ Body
× Cognition
× Emotion/Motivation
× Knowledge and practice
× Resources and opportunity
× Tools and relationships
× Task and institutional context
```

This is not a literal universal equation. It records a bottleneck principle: one near-zero factor can dominate observed achievement.

### 4. Emotion and motivation are not residual noise

They allocate attention and energy, encode value and threat, shape learning and effort, and determine persistence or disengagement. Low action may reflect low expected return, low control, real risk, fatigue, missing skill, or rational abandonment rather than defective character.

### 5. Body is more than health status

Body is simultaneously:

- substrate;
- regulator;
- source of sensory and interoceptive information;
- action system;
- capacity and constraint;
- outcome of behaviour, relationships, institutions, and environment.

WHO ICF’s separation of body functions, activity, participation, and environmental context is retained as a boundary, not imported as a universal Ordivon ontology.

### 6. Cognition and acquired capability must remain distinct

Attention, memory, language, control, reasoning, literacy, numeracy, adaptive problem solving, expertise, and real-world execution do not occupy one level.

P0 places basic information-processing mechanisms under Cognition and treats acquired, reliable, transferable achievement under Capability.

### 7. Cross-system relations require direction and conditions

P0 records 20 seed relations and four feedback loops. A durable relation must name:

- direction;
- mechanism or mediator;
- scope and conditions;
- potential falsifier;
- explanatory failure prevented.

“Everything affects everything” is not a usable graph.

## Mature frameworks reused

P0 directly reuses or compares:

- WHO ICF for body/function/activity/participation/environment boundaries;
- NIH Toolbox for cross-lifespan cognitive, emotional, motor, and sensory measurement domains;
- NIMH RDoC for cognitive, valence, arousal/regulatory, social, and sensorimotor functional constructs and multi-method analysis;
- OECD social and emotional skills work for developable action-regulation patterns and context;
- OECD PIAAC and Skills Outlook for literacy, numeracy, adaptive problem solving, social/emotional skills, background, opportunity, and low-stakes performance limits.

No complete framework is copied into an Ordivon ontology.

## Deliberate non-implementation

P0 added no:

- human database or schema;
- personal profile;
- measurement dashboard;
- questionnaire or psychometric battery;
- health or cognitive diagnostic system;
- AI training or evaluation pipeline;
- personal data collection;
- new CI or governance service.

The only executable method remains the pre-existing synthetic M0 simulation.
