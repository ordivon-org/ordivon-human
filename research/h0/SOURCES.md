# H0 P0 Sources

Accessed 2026-08-02. P0 prioritizes official frameworks and primary measurement research. Sources are retained to anchor terms and boundaries, not to confer universal authority on one framework.

## Body and functioning

1. World Health Organization. **International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF).** Distinguishes health-related body functions and structures while representing functioning in environmental context.  
   <https://www.who.int/classifications/international-classification-of-functioning-disability-and-health>

2. National Institutes of Health. **NIH Blueprint Research Tools — NIH Toolbox for Assessment of Neurological and Behavioral Function.** Describes brief measures of motor, cognitive, sensory, and emotional function, normed across ages 3–85 for longitudinal and intervention research.  
   <https://www.nih.gov/nih-blueprint-neuroscience-research/research-tools>

3. National Institute on Aging. **NIH Toolbox.** Describes the Toolbox as a multidimensional common measurement set covering cognitive, emotional, motor, and sensory function.  
   <https://www.nia.nih.gov/research/resource/nih-toolbox>

## Cognition

4. National Institute of Mental Health. **RDoC Matrix.** Current functional domains and constructs, including attention, perception, declarative memory, language, cognitive control, working memory, arousal/regulation, and sensorimotor systems across multiple units of analysis.  
   <https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/constructs/rdoc-matrix>

5. National Institute of Mental Health. **Definitions of the RDoC Domains and Constructs.** Defines six current domains and emphasizes developmental and environmental context, interacting constructs, and multiple measurement methods.  
   <https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/definitions-of-the-rdoc-domains-and-constructs>

6. Weintraub, S. et al. (2013). **Cognition assessment using the NIH Toolbox.** Describes executive function, episodic memory, language, processing speed, working memory, and attention measures across ages 3–85.  
   <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23479546/>

7. Akshoomoff, N. et al. (2014). **NIH Toolbox Cognitive Function Battery: composite scores of crystallized, fluid, and overall cognition.** Describes the battery's major cognitive domains and developmental validation.  
   <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4103789/>

## Emotion and motivation

8. National Institute of Mental Health. **Development and Definitions of the RDoC Domains and Constructs.** Defines negative valence, positive valence, arousal/regulatory, cognitive, and social-process constructs relevant to threat, reward, effort, learning, and regulation.  
   <https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/development-and-definitions-of-the-rdoc-domains-and-constructs>

9. National Institute of Mental Health. **Behavioral Assessment Methods for RDoC Constructs.** Reviews tasks and measurement limitations across valence, cognition, social processes, and arousal/regulatory systems.  
   <https://www.nimh.nih.gov/about/advisory-boards-and-groups/namhc/reports/behavioral-assessment-methods-for-rdoc-constructs>

10. OECD. **Assessment framework of the OECD Study on Social and Emotional Skills.** Reviews conceptualization, development, malleability, context, and measurement of social and emotional skills.  
    <https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/assessment-framework-of-the-oecd-study-on-social-and-emotional-skills_5007adef-en.html>

11. OECD. **Social and emotional skills.** Current overview of constructs such as self-control, stress resistance, co-operation, sociability, and curiosity and their associations with education, health, and well-being.  
    <https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/social-and-emotional-skills.html>

## Capability and skills

12. OECD. **Survey of Adult Skills 2023 — Reader's Companion.** Defines and explains measurement of adult literacy, numeracy, and adaptive problem solving and their relation to background, skill development, use, and life chances.  
    <https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/survey-of-adult-skills-2023_3639d1e2-en.html>

13. OECD. **Survey of Adult Skills 2023 Technical Report.** Documents assessment, sampling, scaling, background questionnaires, quality control, and data operations for PIAAC 2023.  
    <https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/survey-of-adults-skills-2023-technical-report_80d9f692-en.html>

14. OECD. **OECD Skills Outlook 2025.** Examines literacy, numeracy, adaptive problem solving, social and emotional skills, background, opportunity, lifelong learning, and how skills translate into economic and social outcomes.  
    <https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-skills-outlook-2025_26163cd3-en.html>

15. OECD. **Widening opportunities by investing in 21st-century skills.** Clarifies that low-stakes assessment performance may reflect typical engagement rather than maximal capacity and defines adaptive problem solving as goal pursuit in changing situations using cognitive and metacognitive processes and available resources.  
    <https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-skills-outlook-2025_26163cd3-en/full-report/widening-opportunities-by-investing-in-21st-century-skills_762bbcca.html>

## Source-use rules

- Official classification does not become Ordivon ontology by default.
- A measurement battery is not a complete theory of its target system.
- A policy framework can identify important outcomes while remaining weak on mechanism.
- A psychiatric research framework cannot be generalized to all human functioning without case evidence.
- Population associations remain distinct from within-person change and causal intervention.
- P0 records disagreements and missing constructs rather than forcing false consensus.
