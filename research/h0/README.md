# Human System Atlas

> **Role:** supporting vocabulary and relation map. “H0” is the directory's
> historical identifier, not a reading prerequisite or an active completion
> track.

The Atlas provides selectable distinctions for problem-driven studies. It is not
a complete model of a person and does not need every category filled before a
concrete study proceeds.

## Current consumer

`HUMAN-ECON-001` uses the Atlas to represent:

- body and functioning constraints on work, learning, and recovery;
- cognition for planning, judgment, and adaptation;
- emotion and motivation for stress, reward, persistence, and risk;
- capability for reliable achievement, transfer, and tool use;
- relations among liquidity, commitment, support, control, and recovery.

## Retention rule

A system, subdivision, or relation survives only when an applied study uses it
and deletion causes a named failure in explanation, measurement, or action
ranking. No completion target requires a universal ontology.
