# H0 Closeout

## Decision

H0 retains Ordivon Human in a narrower form.

The repository has demonstrated a current research gap at the intersection of:

- cross-domain human state and context;
- population-to-individual inference;
- longitudinal and N-of-1 evidence;
- plural outcomes and explicit value assumptions;
- human–AI capability transfer, dependence, and agency.

It has not demonstrated a need for a platform, comprehensive ontology, personal-data store, or universal measurement system.

## Main findings

### 1. The first model was over-classified

The initial ten levels mixed different kinds of objects. H0 replaced them with:

- selectable person-state domains;
- context;
- action;
- event or intervention;
- time and history;
- observation and measurement;
- explicit outcomes and value assumptions.

This is smaller and more causal.

### 2. Population evidence has three separate uses

Population observations may:

- describe a distribution;
- predict under the existing data-generating process;
- provide a prior for an intervention effect.

They do not determine personal causal response. The H0 simulation produced:

- observational difference: `−0.3480`;
- true population average effect: `+0.3996`;
- target effects: `+1.0500` and `−0.3100`.

The signs alone prove why the quantities cannot share one field or verbal label.

### 3. More personal data is not causal identification

Repeated individual observation adds value only when design assumptions support the claim. Randomization, timing, carryover, trend, external events, reversibility, measurement quality, and intervention stability matter more than raw record volume.

### 4. AI assistance is not one treatment

Examples, hints, critique, complete answers, execution, retrieval, memory, and social interaction can produce different learning and dependence effects.

H0 therefore separates:

```text
model output
joint human–AI system capability
retained human capability
human agency, replacement, and exit
```

A task-success metric cannot represent all four.

### 5. Outcome pluralism survived deletion

Health, capability, income, autonomy, relationships, well-being, persistence, and meaning can move in different directions. A study must state the outcome owner, horizon, values, displaced outcomes, and risk bearer.

## Structures deleted or rejected

H0 removed or rejected:

1. time and development as one peer level;
2. separate permanent top-level compartments for biological substrate and bodily function;
3. separate permanent top-level compartments for cognition and identity;
4. behaviour as a static state level;
5. resources and environment as universal variable roles;
6. stable trait as a role rather than estimated persistence;
7. trajectory as primitive stored truth;
8. latent construct as if directly observed;
9. causal roles embedded permanently into variable definitions;
10. a universal human schema;
11. a scalar human or augmentation score;
12. a collector, dashboard, database, model pipeline, or CI suite during H0.

## What remains uncertain

- whether the compact model transfers beyond the five H0 cases;
- how much individual evidence is needed before it improves real decisions;
- which N-of-1 designs are practical outside fast, reversible interventions;
- how assistance effects change across tasks, people, models, interfaces, and months or years;
- whether agency and dependence can be measured without turning them into misleading proxies;
- whether Ordivon Game supplies useful behavioural evidence or creates artificial-world bias.

## Next frontier

H1 is ready but not automatically active.

The highest-value next study is not broad data collection. It is one bounded measurement-and-longitudinal experiment that compares at least two observation methods and demonstrates whether the compact H0 distinctions change an actual inference.

Candidate starting points:

1. human–AI capability transfer on a real recurring Ordivon task;
2. sleep, energy, and work-quality measurement with explicit within-person design;
3. a synthetic-to-real validation of population priors plus individual updating.

The first candidate has the strongest direct connection to current Ordivon use, but H1 should begin only after its objective, burden, privacy class, and stopping rule are specified.

## Final H0 status

```text
model: retained, reduced
repository: retained, research-only
implementation: one synthetic simulation only
data collection: not admitted
platform work: not admitted
H1: ready, inactive
```
