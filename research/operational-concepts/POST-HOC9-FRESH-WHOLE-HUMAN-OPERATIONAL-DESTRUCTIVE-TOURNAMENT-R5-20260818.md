# 3. Identity-targeted intervention evidence weakens identity-specific mechanism claims

Round 4 treated identity-targeted interventions as evidence of independent operational relevance.
Round 5 asks a harder question:

> Do these interventions work **because identity itself is the distinct operative mechanism**, or because they change planning, efficacy, salience, goals, behavior, social feedback or future-outcome consideration?

The answer is currently mixed.

A randomized possible-selves study among newly retired adults found no significant between-condition effects on either physical-activity identity or physical activity.

Recent intervention syntheses similarly show heterogeneous effects. Many possible-self trials show no significant identity or behavior differences, while some positive effects appear plausibly mediated or moderated by:

```text
action planning
planning self-efficacy
task self-efficacy
consideration of future consequences
baseline readiness/intention
```

These are already ordinary HOC5/HOC1/HOC2 variables.

Therefore:

```text
IdentityTargetedIntervention
!= IdentitySpecificMechanismProof
```

This directly weakens A3 and A9.


# 7. Identity transition also reduces into transition composition

Identity transitions are real and often consequential.

But their action grammar depends on what materially changed:

```text
capability changed      → HOC1/HOC3/HOC7
role changed            → HOC6
relationship changed    → HOC6/HF22
habit/context changed   → HOC9
health/function changed → HOC7
values/goals changed    → HOC5
memory/story changed    → HF7/HF8/HF23
```

The self-model projection answers:

```text
How is H currently representing this change in relation to self?
```

That can matter to intervention selection.

But the intervention is not generically determined by `identity transition` itself.

Therefore:

```text
IdentityTransitionCase
= transition-lens composition
```

rather than a generic Human control layer.

# 8. Future-self continuity survives — but as a specialized motivational representation

This is the hardest residual.

Classic experiments show that greater present–future self continuity is associated with more future-oriented saving, and exposure to age-progressed future-self representations can shift saving behavior under experimental conditions.

Recent experiments also continue to manipulate future-self continuity and test its effects on intertemporal choice.

Therefore Round 5 retains:

```text
FutureSelfContinuityView
```

as a real practical projection.

But:

```text
FutureSelfContinuity
!= TemporalDiscounting
```

and also:

```text
FutureSelfContinuity
!= OneGenericIdentityMechanism
```

The action owner remains the target decision:

```text
saving / delayed reward / long-horizon choice
→ HOC5/HF10

health behavior
→ HOC5/HOC7/HOC9

future planning
→ HOC5 + HF7/HF8/HF9
```

Future-self continuity functions as a self-referential representational/motivational input.

That is sufficient to preserve its empirical value without HOC10.

# 10. Contextual identity activation undermines permanent identity-state assumptions

Identity-based motivation work emphasizes that different identities can become salient under different contextual cues and can change the interpretation of difficulty and action.

Chinese self-orientation experiments also show conflicts among relationship-oriented, familistic/group-oriented and other-oriented self constructions rather than one universal self axis.

Thus:

```text
IdentitySalience(H,t,C1)
!= IdentitySalience(H,t,C2)
```

and:

```text
OneIdentityCentralityScore
!= GenericCrossCulturalHumanState
```

This does not make identity unreal.
It makes purpose/context-specific projection the safer generic architecture.
