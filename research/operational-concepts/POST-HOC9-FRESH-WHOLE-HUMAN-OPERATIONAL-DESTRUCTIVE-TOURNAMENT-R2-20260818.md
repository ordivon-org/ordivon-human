# Post-HOC9 Fresh Whole-Human Operational Destructive Tournament — Round 2

## 0. Canonical frontier remains unchanged

```text
HF0–HF23 = preserved

HD0–HD10 = completed
NextDeepRoute = UNKNOWN

HOC0–HOC9 = completed / frozen
HOC10     = UNKNOWN / not admitted
NextHOC   = UNKNOWN

```

Round 2 does not inherit any candidate as winner.

The competitors are:

```text
A. lived / experienced welfare
B. experienced agency / meaningful control
C. social connection / belonging / loneliness

E. no-new-HOC / consumer-specific composition
```

The target is not conceptual importance. The target is a stable recurring practical hole that remains after correct HOC1–HOC9 composition.

HF2 already gives the critical epistemic grammar:

```text
Experience != Report
Experience != Behaviour
Experience != LaterRecall
FirstPersonEvidence != ThirdPersonEvidence
NoDetectedResponse != NoExperience
```

It explicitly recognizes first-person evidence about:

```text
pain
vividness
uncertainty
mineness
perceived agency
subjective temporal structure
```

HF21 already gives affective distinctions:

```text
Affect != Emotion
Feeling != EmotionEpisodeTotality
Valence != Emotion
Appraisal != Emotion
Expression != Experience
RegulationOfExperience != RegulationOfExpression
Mood != MomentaryValence
PainExperience != EmotionCategory
```

Therefore the round-2 residual is **not** a missing Human foundation.

The question is downstream:

> Do practical consumers need a reusable decision-oriented grammar for Human lived experience strongly enough that repeated direct projection from HF2/HF21 is no longer adequate?

No FoundationReopenCondition is triggered.

# 2. Rival A — `Welfare` as the new operational family

## 2.1 Why Welfare looked strong

Existing HOCs intentionally stop short of welfare totality:

```text
HOC5: Preference != Welfare
HOC7: Health != WelfareTotality
HOC6: InteractionPleasantness != CoordinationQuality
HOC1: Capability != experienced quality of life
```

Real decisions can diverge when objective success and lived outcome diverge:

```text
high function + severe suffering
high productivity + severe distress
successful treatment + intolerable burden
high social contact + loneliness
low productivity + much better comfort
```

This establishes a real hole.

## 2.2 Why `Welfare` itself fails

`Welfare` silently bundles several non-identical targets:

```text
momentary affect
pain / comfort
experienced distress
life/domain evaluation
meaning / worthwhileness
autonomy / agency experience
social connectedness
preference satisfaction
health/function consequences
normative claims about what is good for the person
```

These need not move together.

A person can report:

```text
negative affect + strong meaning
pain + high life evaluation
low momentary pleasure + strong goal endorsement
high satisfaction + constrained agency
```

Therefore:

```text
OneWelfareState
```

is too broad for the generic HOC layer.

## 2.3 Disposition

```text
Welfare = REJECT as one generic operational family / scalar
```

Retain `welfare` only as a consumer-level synthesis whose exact normative/experiential components are declared.

The search continues because the lived-experience hole remains after deleting Welfare.

# 3. Rival B — Experienced Agency / Meaningful Control

## 3.1 Strong irreducible-looking cases

HOC5/HOC6/Harness can establish:

```text
goal ownership
formal authority
permission
override availability
who actually executed
who causally controlled what
role allocation
```

Yet a Human may experience:

```text
low authorship despite formal control
high authorship despite low causal control
pressure despite nominal choice
alienation despite endorsed goal completion
```

Thus:

```text
ExperiencedAgency
!= ActualCausalControl
!= FormalAuthority
!= Permission
!= GoalOwnership
!= NumberOfChoices
```

## 3.2 Destructive falsifier: felt agency can be manipulated without equivalent control change

Automation evidence gives a particularly strong falsifier.

Action-contingent feedback can increase reported sense of agency in an automatic-control condition even when the feedback is irrelevant to actual task control.

Therefore a generic Human model must not use:

```text
FeltAgency
→ infer ActualControl
```

or:

```text
ActualControl
→ infer FeltAgency
```

without evidence.

## 3.3 Why Agency nevertheless fails as an independent family

Once a generic experiential target is available, the practical object becomes:

```text
ExperiencedAgencyCase
= LivedExperience(target = authorship/control/mineness)
+ HOC5 goal / intention / delegation / override
+ HOC6 role / dependence / authority relation
+ Harness/Runtime objective action-control evidence
```

The uniquely Human residual is the **experience of authorship/control**.
The rest is already owned.

Deleting a standalone agency family does not destroy the decision grammar if this experience dimension can be represented generically.

## 3.4 Disposition

```text
ExperiencedAgency = COMPOSITE / specialized experiential projection
```

Not admitted as an independent generic HOC.

# 4. Rival C — Social Connection / Belonging / Loneliness

## 4.1 Why HOC6 is insufficient alone

HOC6 can represent:

```text
relationship persistence
interaction history
trust
reliance
dependence
support
availability
coordination
repair
```

but none of these entails:

```text
Human feels connected
Human feels they belong
Human is not lonely
```

Objective social isolation and subjective loneliness can diverge materially.

Therefore:

```text
SocialStructure != ExperiencedConnection
```

## 4.2 Destructive cases

```text
C1 many contacts + intense loneliness
C2 few contacts + low loneliness / sufficient connection
C3 reliable support + low belonging
C4 poor coordination + strong emotional connection
C5 good coordination + social alienation
```

These change support decisions.

## 4.3 Why Social Connection fails as an independent family

Its structure decomposes cleanly into:

```text
HOC6 relational/social structure
+ experiential target: connection / belonging / loneliness
+ HOC7 health/function only if the use case requires it
```

The missing part is again not the relationship relation itself, but **what that relation is experienced as by the Human**.

## 4.4 Disposition

```text
SocialConnection/Loneliness = COMPOSITE / specialized experiential projection
```

No independent HOC admission.

# 5. The unnamed survivor — Experiential Outcome / Lived Experience

Round 2 therefore discovers a more general residual than the three named candidates.

Working referent:

> A purpose-bound operational representation of what a current episode, condition, interaction, environment or support regime is experienced as by the Human, when that experienced outcome can change a practical decision and cannot be inferred safely from objective function, behavior, goals, health, relationship structure or system performance.

This is not a new ontology of consciousness.
It is an operational bridge from HF2/HF21 into practical decisions.

Provisional names remain deliberately non-canonical:

```text
Experiential Outcome
Lived Experience
Experienced State/Outcome
Human Experiential Surface
```

No name has yet won.

---

# 6. Minimal survivor grammar

A candidate generic grammar can be written without introducing one global experience score.

## 6.1 LivedExperienceTargetSpec

```text
LivedExperienceTargetSpec = {
  Human,
  target experiential dimension/question,
  episode/domain/context,
  time interval / recall horizon,
  support/environment state,
  intended decision,
  consequence,
  elicitation/report channel,
  cultural/developmental interpretation context
}
```

Possible target dimensions are not assumed additive:

```text
pleasantness / unpleasantness
pain / discomfort
experienced fatigue/burden
anxiety/fear/distress
interest/enjoyment
connection/loneliness
agency/authorship/control feeling
felt pressure/coercion
worthwhileness/meaning when genuinely required
other explicitly declared experiential content
```

## 6.2 ExperientialEvidenceBundle

```text
ExperientialEvidenceBundle = {
  direct first-person report,
  repeated/ecological report if available,
  spontaneous report/communication,
  behavioural evidence,
  physiological/neural evidence if relevant,
  proxy/caregiver report,
  reportability/communication constraints,
  memory/recall interval,
  measurement protocol,
  contradictory evidence,
  uncertainty,
  provenance
}
```

Core law:

```text
FirstPersonReport
!= ExperienceByDefinition
```

but for many target experiences it can be the highest-specificity available evidence.

Third-person channels constrain but do not transparently replace the first-person phenomenon.

## 6.3 LivedExperienceProfile

```text
LivedExperienceProfile(H, Target, interval) = {
  target content/dimension,
  intensity where meaningful,
  frequency,
  duration,
  variability,
  context/trigger relations,
  positive/negative/mixed character,
  experienced burden/benefit,
  relation to goals/function where relevant,
  report/evidence quality,
  uncertainty
}
```

No requirement exists that every field applies to every target.

## 6.4 ExperienceTrajectory

```text
ExperienceTrajectory = {
  repeated LivedExperienceProfiles,
  interventions/support changes,
  objective state changes,
  adaptation/habituation evidence,
  change points,
  unresolved divergence,
  uncertainty
}
```

## 6.5 ExperienceObjectiveDivergenceCase

A central practical object is:

```text
ExperienceObjectiveDivergenceCase = {
  objective outcome/function/system state,
  experienced outcome,
  direction of divergence,
  possible explanations,
  consequence,
  decision relevance,
  evidence strength
}
```

Examples:

```text
function good / experience bad
function poor / experience acceptable
system success / agency poor
social network dense / loneliness high
biomarker improved / lived burden worse
```

## 6.6 ProxyEvidenceBoundary

For infants, some disabled/non-speaking Humans, severe illness and other constrained-report regimes:

```text
ProxyReport
!= FirstPersonExperience
```

but proxy/behaviour/physiology may still provide bounded evidence.

The operational object must represent:

```text
what is directly reported
what is inferred
by whom
through which channel
with which known false-positive/false-negative paths
```

## 6.7 NextBestExperienceOperation

Potential outputs include:

```text
ASK / ELICIT_DIRECTLY
CLARIFY_EXPERIENCE_TARGET
SAMPLE_CLOSER_TO_EPISODE
TRACK_TRAJECTORY
COMPARE_OBJECTIVE_AND_EXPERIENTIAL_OUTCOME
CHANGE_SUPPORT
REDUCE_BURDEN
RESTORE_COMFORT
RESTORE_MEANINGFUL_CONTROL
CHANGE_INTERFACE / AUTOMATION POLICY
CHANGE_SOCIAL SUPPORT MODE
ESCALATE_HEALTH/SAFETY OWNER
COLLECT_MORE_EVIDENCE
NO_INTERVENTION
```

The operation is conditioned by authority and neighboring-domain rules.

# 7. Cross-regime deletion tests

## 7.1 Work / productivity

Case:

```text
output high
errors low
fatigue moderate
but sustained distress/aversiveness severe
```

HOC1/4/5 cannot safely infer the lived outcome.

Decision may change from:

```text
continue unchanged
```

to:

```text
change task design / support / schedule / role
```

Survivor: YES.

## 7.2 Learning

Case:

```text
retention and transfer improve
but learning experience becomes highly aversive / humiliating / anxiety-laden
```

HOC3 can prove learning success but not whether the support regime is experientially acceptable.

Survivor: YES, though pedagogical legitimacy remains external.

## 7.3 Health / palliative care

Case:

```text
biological markers stable or improved
function partly preserved
but pain/suffering burden severe
```

or:

```text
lower aggressive intervention intensity
+ better comfort / less suffering
```

HOC7 deliberately preserves this distinction but does not generalize the experiential grammar beyond health.

Survivor: STRONG YES.

## 7.4 Disability / assistive support

Case:

```text
same functional success
+ different experienced effort / discomfort / dignity / control
```

Function-with-support alone is insufficient.

Survivor: YES.

## 7.5 Caregiving

Case:

```text
care tasks completed
care recipient safe
caregiver function intact
but lived burden becomes intolerable
```

HOC4 load and HOC6 role/dependence are relevant but may not capture experienced burden itself.

Survivor: YES.

## 7.6 Children / development

The target remains relevant, but evidence grammar becomes harder:

```text
language/report capacity changes rapidly
adult proxy may misread experience
concept meaning differs developmentally
```

This does not falsify the family; it makes `reportability/evidence ceiling` mandatory.

Survivor: YES WITH STRONG EVIDENCE BOUNDARY.

## 7.7 Ageing / frailty

Case:

```text
objective independence decreases
but personally valued comfort/connection/meaning improves
```

or the reverse.

A capability-only or health-only model can route poorly.

Survivor: YES.

## 7.8 Coercive / power-asymmetric context

Case:

```text
compliance high
performance high
nominal choices exist
but experienced pressure/fear/control loss is high
```

HOC5 already blocks `behavior = preference`, yet practical support may still need the experience target.

Survivor: YES, while coercion legitimacy remains Security/Governance/Institution owned.

## 7.9 Human–Agent automation

Case:

```text
joint performance improves
Human remains nominal authority
but experienced authorship/control declines
```

or feedback artificially raises felt agency without increasing actual control.

This is exactly an objective/experiential divergence case.

Survivor: STRONG YES.

## 7.10 Social relation

Case:

```text
network/contact/support high
but loneliness high
```

HOC6 alone cannot answer the lived question.

Survivor: YES.

# 8. Rival models for the survivor

## M1 — One global welfare / happiness score

```text
all lived outcome → scalar
```

Failure:

```text
life evaluation, affect, pain, meaning, agency and connection can diverge
cross-cultural weighting differs
consumer decisions target different surfaces
```

Disposition: REJECT.

## M2 — Affect-only model

```text
lived experience = valence/arousal
```

Failure:

```text
agency/authorship
connection/loneliness
worthwhileness
qualitative pain/effort
```

are not safely reducible to valence/arousal alone.

Disposition: REJECT as universal model; retain affect as one target family.

## M3 — Self-report-is-truth model

```text
reported x = experienced x
```

Failure:

```text
reportability limitations
recall bias
concept/language differences
children/nonverbal cases
strategic/social reporting
```

Disposition: REJECT identity; retain first-person report as privileged but fallible evidence.

## M4 — Third-person inference replacement model

```text
behaviour/physiology/model can replace first-person evidence
```

Failure:

HF2 already rejects this transport. Same behaviour can coexist with different experience and no universal external marker is earned.

Disposition: REJECT.

## M5 — Health-related QoL model as generic owner

Failure:

work, learning, relationships, coercion and Human–Agent agency cases are not health questions by default.

Disposition: retain HOC7 as health-specific consumer/bridge; do not expand it to all experience.

## M6 — Existing HOCs plus ad-hoc consumer fields

This is the strongest no-new-HOC rival.

It can work locally:

```text
one product stores pain
another stores loneliness
another stores agency
another stores distress
```

But repeated consumers then independently recreate the same missing grammar:

```text
subjective target
first-person evidence
proxy boundary
recall horizon
objective/experienced divergence
trajectory
uncertainty
intervention reflexivity
forbidden inferences
```

This repeated reconstruction is evidence that a generic operational layer may have practical value.

Disposition: NO-NEW-HOC hypothesis weakened, not yet fully falsified.

# 11. Privacy / governance destructive test

This candidate is unusually dangerous because the evidence can include intimate:

```text
pain
distress
fear
loneliness
meaninglessness
felt coercion
sexual/intimate experience
religious/spiritual experience
agency/control experience
```

A generic HOC would therefore need a **minimum-necessary acquisition rule**:

```text
CollectExperienceEvidence
only when
  target decision is explicit
  + expected decision value is material
  + less intrusive evidence is insufficient
  + collection authority/consent is valid
  + retention/use scope is bounded
```

It must also support:

```text
NOT_MEASURED
DECLINED_TO_REPORT
NOT_REPORTABLE
PROXY_ONLY
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
```

as legitimate states.

A system must never interpret refusal as negative experience or suspicious behavior.

The candidate cannot pass A10 without these constraints surviving further tests.

# 12. Cross-cultural destructive test

A universal scalar becomes especially unsafe across cultures.

Different traditions may differently weight or express:

```text
high-arousal vs low-arousal positive states
individual autonomy vs relational harmony
personal achievement vs family/community flourishing
explicit emotional disclosure
meaning / spirituality / connection
```

Therefore the survivor must be:

```text
TargetSemanticContext-aware
```

not merely translated labels with assumed metric invariance.

Cross-cultural difference is not evidence that experience is unmeasurable.
It is evidence that:

```text
SameQuestionString
!= SameConstructMeaning automatically
```

and that consumer decisions must respect validated transport scope.

# 13. Human–Agent special perturbation

Agent systems introduce new failure modes not central in pre-Agent measurement traditions.

## 13.1 Experience shaping through interface policy

An Agent can alter:

```text
friction
salience
choice set
feedback timing
explanations
social tone
automation level
```

and therefore change the very experience it later measures.

```text
ExperienceMeasurement/SupportPolicy
may be ExperienceIntervention
```

## 13.2 Synthetic empathy does not reveal experience

An Agent generating empathic language does not establish that its inference about Human experience is accurate.

```text
EmpathicResponseQuality
!= ExperienceInferenceAccuracy
```

## 13.3 Personalized experience optimization can become manipulation

Optimizing predicted enjoyment, engagement or felt agency can be used to maximize operator/product objectives instead of Human-endorsed outcomes.

Therefore:

```text
PredictedPositiveExperience
!= HumanGoal
!= Consent
!= PermissionToShapeBehaviour
```

This is a hard bridge to Security/Governance.

## 13.4 Agent support can improve objective outcome and worsen lived outcome

This is the strongest Agent-era reason the survivor may deserve generic operational status.

Without an explicit lived-outcome surface, an Agent can optimize HOC1/HOC3/HOC5/HOC6 success while making Human experience systematically worse and never observe the missing objective.
