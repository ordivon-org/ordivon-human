# HOC5 Evidence Ledger

## 1. Achtziger, Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2008 — implementation intentions and goal shielding

`Implementation intentions and shielding goal striving from unwanted thoughts and feelings.`
Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin. 2008;34(3):381–393.
DOI: 10.1177/0146167207311201
PMID: 18272806.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18272806/

Two field experiments concerned dieting and athletic goals.

Retained pressure:

```text
if-then plans can support ongoing goal pursuit under some interference conditions;
implementation support can affect goal attainment without proving stronger goal value.
```

HOC5 use:

```text
GoalIntention != ImplementationPolicy
ImplementationGain != MotivationIncrease
```

---

## 2. Allan et al. 2013 — planning intervention benefit is heterogeneous

`The best laid plans: planning skill determines the effectiveness of action plans and implementation intentions.`
Annals of Behavioral Medicine. 2013.
DOI: 10.1007/s12160-013-9483-9
PMID: 23456214.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23456214/

Study 1 prospectively examined intentions/action planning and planning skill (n=72); Study 2 experimentally randomized participants to a planning intervention in an online food-diary task (n=144).

Retained pressure:

```text
plan quality/effectiveness can depend on planning skill/profile;
planning interventions are not uniformly beneficial in the same way for every Human.
```

HOC5 use:

```text
PlanPresent != GoodPlan
SamePlanningIntervention != SameBenefit
```

---

## 3. Wieber et al. 2017 — implementation plans can generalize or misfire

`More than planned: Implementation intention effects in non-planned situations.`
Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin. 2017.
PMID: 28666568.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28666568/

Three experiments examined goal performance in situations similar/dissimilar to specified critical situations and requiring planned/different responses.

Retained pressure:

```text
planned responses can facilitate behavior in intended/sufficiently similar cases;
automaticized planned responses can also impair performance when the response is inappropriate.
```

HOC5 use:

```text
AutomaticPlanExecution != ContextualOptimality
ImplementationPlan needs override/context/expiry rules where stakes matter.
```

---

## 4. Zhou et al. 2026 — MCII and academic procrastination

Xiaoxue Zhou et al.
`Beyond positive thinking: A randomized trial of mental contrasting with implementation intentions to curb academic procrastination.`
Acta Psychologica. 2026;262:106168.
DOI: 10.1016/j.actpsy.2025.106168
PMID: 41601124.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41601124/

Randomized controlled study, N=81, undergraduate students at one private Chinese university, comparing MCII with a positive-thinking strategy.

Retained pressure:

```text
MCII reduced task aversiveness and improved willingness to initiate academic tasks relative to control under the study protocol;
effects were assessed through short follow-up.
```

HOC5 use:

```text
DelayPattern can be intervention-sensitive;
procrastination should not automatically be treated as stable character.
```

Transport remains limited by sample/context/outcome measurement.

## 5. Hyndman & Bisin 2026 — deadline replication failure

Kyle Hyndman, Alberto Bisin.
`Replication of "Procrastination, Deadlines, and Performance: Self-Control by Precommitment".`
Psychological Science. Online 2026-07-15.
DOI: 10.1177/09567976261460772
PMID: 42456006.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42456006/

Replication of Study 2 from Ariely & Wertenbroch with adult university participants.

Retained pressure:

```text
deadline manipulations had negligible effects on the reported primary performance/survey outcomes in this replication;
the classic pattern did not reproduce.
```

HOC5 use:

```text
Deadline != UniversalSelfControlTool
```

This does not prove deadlines never work; it rejects unconditional transport of the earlier effect.

## 6. Goschke & Bolte / coasting-shifting experimental evidence (2020 publication)

`On the Mechanics of Goal Striving: Experimental Evidence of Coasting and Shifting.`
Motivation Science. 2020/2021 record.
DOI: 10.1037/mot0000157
PMID: 33834088.
PMCID: PMC8022896.

PubMed/PMC:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33834088/
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8022896/

Two experiments manipulated feedback on progress toward competing speed/accuracy goals.

Retained pressure:

```text
better-than-needed progress toward one goal reduced subsequent focal-goal progress and shifted resources toward the alternative goal.
```

HOC5 use:

```text
ProgressSignal can change ActionAllocation.
LowerEffortAfterProgress != MotivationLoss by definition.
```

## 7. Hopstaken et al. 2016 — reward, sustained attention and effort

`Rewards boost sustained attention through higher effort: A value-based decision making approach.`
Biological Psychology. 2016.
DOI: 10.1016/j.biopsycho.2016.07.019
PMID: 27498294.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27498294/

Across experiments, higher reward was associated with improved sustained-attention performance and higher effort-related measures; sustained performance also carried subjective cost in choice measures.

HOC5 use:

```text
incentives can alter current effort allocation;
effort willingness, reward value and endorsed long-horizon value must remain separate.
```

## 8. Murayama et al. 2015 — autonomy-support / choice manipulation

`Live as we choose: The role of autonomy support in facilitating intrinsic motivation.`
International Journal of Psychophysiology. 2015.
DOI: 10.1016/j.ijpsycho.2015.08.009
PMID: 26344180.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26344180/

Experimental manipulation of opportunity to choose between tasks of equal difficulty with behavioral/electrophysiological measures.

Retained pressure:

```text
choice/autonomy-support context can alter motivation-related responses under the protocol.
```

HOC5 use:

```text
MeaningfulChoice/PerceivedControl may be motivational inputs;
ChoiceCount != Autonomy.
```

# 10. Evidence ceiling

The cited studies establish scoped intervention effects and failure cases.
They do not justify a universal motivational equation, one optimal plan, one deadline schedule or one productivity policy.

```text
HOC5
= Ordivon operational synthesis
  + scoped experimental action-control operators
  + explicit ownership/autonomy/transport boundaries.
```

`MotivationProfile`, `FrictionMap`, `ExecutionFailureInference`, `PrecommitmentPolicy` and `NextBestExecutionAction` require consumer-specific validation.
