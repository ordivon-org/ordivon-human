# 10. Why HOC5 does not already own emotion regulation

HOC5 can represent:

```text
emotion regulation as a goal
emotion-triggered friction
fear/anticipated affect as an action input
planned regulation action as one means
```

But generic goal/action machinery cannot choose among affective strategies without a
specific affective model.

Minimal counterexample:

```text
same goal: remain effective during an upsetting event
same capability
same opportunity
same explicit commitment

A uses reappraisal
B uses suppression
C uses distraction
D seeks interpersonal co-regulation

```

HOC5 can record that different means were selected.
It cannot by itself represent:

```text
which affective endpoint each strategy targets
whether expression changed but feeling did not
whether short-term relief produced later cost
whether strategy fit the current affect intensity/context

```

This mirrors the logic that admitted HOC9 after HOC5:


# 13. External evidence strengthens the missing operational family

Current evidence does not support one simple adaptive/maladaptive strategy hierarchy.

A 2026 coordinated analysis of twelve ambulatory datasets found substantial between-person
variability for every studied regulation strategy; most variance in apparent effectiveness
was explained by the intensity of the affective state being regulated, strongly supporting
person-strategy-situation fit rather than fixed strategy ranking.

Systematic/meta-analytic evidence also shows emotion-regulation difficulties are
transdiagnostically relevant across many mental disorders and are direct intervention
targets.

Interpersonal emotion-regulation research explicitly treats social interaction as a means
of managing one's own or another person's feelings/expressions.

Cross-cultural meta-analysis shows associations between common strategies and mental-health
outcomes vary with cultural context.

Educational research further shows emotion regulation is practically consequential in
nonclinical work/teaching contexts, affecting teacher well-being, classroom interaction and
student outcomes.

Therefore the candidate is not confined to psychiatry.
