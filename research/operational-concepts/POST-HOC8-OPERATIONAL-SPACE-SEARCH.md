# Post-HOC8 Human Operational-Space Search

## 0. Search rule

The original HOC0 inventory is no longer treated as exhaustive.

Search across:

```text
previously named product concepts
foundational residuals
pre-Agent behavior-change traditions
Agent-era support/automation pressure
cross-project ownership
concepts exposed only after HOC1–HOC8 composition
```

Allowed outcomes:

```text
NEW_HOC
COMPOSITE_VIEW
PROJECTION_ONLY
EVIDENCE_ONLY
CROSS_PROJECT_BRIDGE
OWNED_ELSEWHERE
UNRESOLVED_COMPOSITION
PHASE_CLOSURE
```

---

# 1. Identity / self-model / role continuity

Real residual:

```text
self-concept
self-schema
self-evaluation
role identity
multiple identities
identity conflict
future-self continuity
self-model updating
```

HF1 already proves that bodily, agentive, reflective, autobiographical/narrative and social/role self-relations can dissociate and that SelfModelMaintenance != SelfModelUpdating.

Operationally this could support long-horizon personalization and life transitions.

But current action needs can often be represented through:

```text
HOC1 self/capability estimates
HOC2 metacognitive confidence
HOC5 goals/commitments/roles
HOC6 social roles/relationships
HOC8 self-knowledge/boundary evidence
HD10 scoped person-difference projections
```

A total identity profile also creates strong privacy, manipulation and self-fulfilling-label risks.

Disposition:

```text
REAL RESIDUAL / DEFERRED OPERATIONAL BRIDGE
not HOC9
```

Deletion of an explicit identity HOC does not yet break as many near-term operational decisions as deletion of habit/contextual-control modeling.

---

# 2. Habit / routine / automaticity

HOC5 already needs:

```text
habit/cue conflict
habitual distraction
cue engineering
implementation intentions
```

but cannot answer:

```text
Is repeated behavior actually habit-like or merely repeated deliberate action?
Which cue/context currently evokes the response?
How stable is the cue-response relation across contexts?
Does current outcome devaluation change behavior?
Is the behavior automatic, skilled, routine, or all three in different senses?
What happens after context shift?
When should an Agent preserve a beneficial routine versus interrupt it?
How should a bad routine be redesigned without silently changing Human goals?
```

Deletion leaves a practical hole.

Disposition:

```text
GENUINE NEW HOC
```

```text
COMPOSITE / HEALTH-AFFECT-WELFARE BRIDGE
```


Disposition:


# 10. Why Habit wins over Identity for this round

Identity deletion leaves many useful operations still expressible through scoped self-estimate, goal, role, relationship and knowledge projections.

Habit deletion leaves recurring action-control cases with no explicit owner:

```text
same goal + same capability + same motivation
but old cue triggers unwanted response

new environment removes the cue and behavior disappears

routine works under normal context but fails dangerously under exceptional context

repetition rises without automaticity

high skill exists without habitual selection

Agent automation turns repeated explicit decisions into an externally cued routine
```

These cases directly alter practical intervention choice.

```text
HOC9 = Habits, Routines, Automaticity and Contextual Behavioral Control
```

It does not reopen any Foundation.
