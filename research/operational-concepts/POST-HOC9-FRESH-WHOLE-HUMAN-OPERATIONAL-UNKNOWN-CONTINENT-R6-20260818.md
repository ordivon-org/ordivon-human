# 2. First broad result: many domains share a decision-participation problem

Across otherwise very different regimes, a repeated practical question appears:

```text
Does the Human meaningfully participate in the decision that affects them,
with enough support to understand/express/revise/implement their choice,
while retaining the appropriate decision role and without support silently becoming substitution, coercion or institutional bypass?
```

This appears in:

```text
supported decision-making for intellectual disability
transition-age disability services
child participation in healthcare/services
reproductive agency
survivor-centred violence response
mental-health treatment planning
end-of-life / family-centred medical decisions
forced-displacement humanitarian participation
Human–Agent recommendation / persuasion / delegation
```

Round 6 names the residual provisionally:

```text
Supported Decision Participation
Self-Determination under Support
Decision Voice / Participation under Support
```

No name is canonical.

---

# 3. Why this initially looks like a missing HOC

The deletion witness is real.

Two cases can have similar:

```text
basic capability
stated preference
goal content
health state
available options
```

while differing in:

```text
whether the Human is asked
whether information is accessible
whether communication support exists
whether the Human's view changes the decision
whether a family/professional/Agent substitutes their own judgment
whether the Human can refuse/revise/override
whether institutional authority recognizes the Human's decision role
```

That difference can materially change:

```text
treatment choice
service configuration
reproductive outcome
care plan
use of coercion
participation/engagement
trust and future help-seeking
Agent delegation behavior
```

So the residual cannot be dismissed as mere rhetoric.

# 26. SupportedDecisionParticipationProtocol retained

Round 6 retains:

```text
SupportedDecisionParticipationProtocol
```

as an unnumbered cross-cutting consumption protocol.

Minimal reasoning grammar:

```text
1. Declare the exact decision and consequence.
2. Identify the substantive owner and legal/institutional authority regime.
3. Record the Human's actual decision role; do not infer it from diagnosis, age, support need or role label.
4. Elicit goals/preferences/values using accessible communication.
5. Assess only decision-relevant understanding/capability, under realistic support.
6. Name supporters/advisers/proxies and their authority separately.
7. Record dependence, power asymmetry, pressure/coercion and option-set restrictions.
8. Distinguish advice/support from substitution.
9. Check whether the Human can revise, refuse, stop or override where the domain permits.
10. Record whether Human input actually affected the outcome.
11. Route legal/rights disputes to Governance/Institution; do not adjudicate them from Human scores.
12. Update/expire rapidly when support, health, authority, communication or decision context changes.
```
