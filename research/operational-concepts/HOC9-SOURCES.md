# HOC9 Evidence Ledger

## 1. Wood, Tam & Witt 2005 — changing circumstances disrupt habits

Wendy Wood, Leona Tam, Melissa Guerrero Witt.
`Changing circumstances, disrupting habits.`
Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. 2005;88(6):918–933.
DOI: 10.1037/0022-3514.88.6.918
PMID: 15982113.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15982113/

Retained pressure:

```text
students transferring contexts showed habitual exercising/newspaper/TV behavior survived more when relevant performance contexts remained stable;
context changes could disrupt habitual performance and increase correspondence with current intentions;
intention change alone did not explain all disruption.
```

HOC9 use:

```text
ContextShiftEvent is a first-class habit probe/intervention.
Habit expression is cue/context dependent.
```

---

## 2. Stojanovic, Grund & Fries 2022 — context stability and intentional habit building

`Context Stability in Habit Building Increases Automaticity and Goal Attainment.`
Frontiers in Psychology. 2022.
PMID: 35756236.
PMCID: PMC9226889.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35756236/

Study 1 manipulated context stability during six weeks of study-habit formation; Study 2 analyzed naturally varying context stability in an app dataset.

Retained pressure:

```text
context stability predicted greater automaticity and habit-repetition goal attainment in both datasets;
automaticity partially mediated the context-stability relation to goal attainment.
```

HOC9 use:

```text
ContextStabilityProfile is operationally meaningful.
StableContext can facilitate habit formation but is not sufficient proof.
```

## 3. Vázquez-Millán et al. 2026 — devaluation validity critique

Antonio Vázquez-Millán et al.
`The evaluation of devaluation: Deficient outcome devaluation leads to wrongly considering goal-directed actions as habits.`
Behavior Research Methods. 2026;58(8):224.
DOI: 10.3758/s13428-026-03099-6
PMID: 42414812.
PMCID: PMC13342024.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42414812/

Preregistered conceptual replication plus reanalysis of prior datasets.

Retained pressure:

```text
participants showing apparently habit-like outcome-insensitive responding were disproportionately those for whom the devaluation manipulation was ineffective;
suboptimal devaluation can misclassify goal-directed actions as habits.
```

HOC9 use:

```text
OutcomeInsensitiveResponse != ProvenHabit.
OutcomeSensitivityProbe must verify manipulation validity.
```

## 4. context-response association experiments, 2023

`Measuring context-response associations that drive habits.`
Journal of the Experimental Analysis of Behavior. 2023.
PMID: 38047612.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38047612/

Two experiments used practice on a sequential task, a cue-response association measure and later performance under cognitive load.

Retained pressure:

```text
more task practice strengthened measured cue-response associations;
stronger associations predicted task success under the study protocol;
practice effects were not explained by stronger goal intentions/motivation in the same way.
```

HOC9 use:

```text
Cue-response association evidence can supplement frequency/automaticity reports.
Repetition can alter control without proving a universal habit mechanism.
```

## 5. Wood, Quinn & Kashy 2002 — habits in everyday life

`Habits in everyday life: thought, emotion, and action.`
Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. 2002.
PMID: 12500811.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12500811/

Retained pressure:

```text
habitual everyday behavior was associated with less behavior-congruent thought than nonhabitual behavior and lower reported stress during habit performance under the studied diary methods.
```

HOC9 use:

```text
habit can reduce repeated deliberative burden;
this benefit does not imply contextual optimality.
```

## 6. Orbell & Verplanken 2010 — cue-contingent automaticity

Sheina Orbell, Bas Verplanken.
`The automatic component of habit in health behavior: habit as cue-contingent automaticity.`
Health Psychology. 2010;29(4):374–383.
DOI: 10.1037/a0019596
PMID: 20658824.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20658824/

Retained pressure:

```text
habit-related automaticity is meaningfully tied to cue-response relationships rather than frequency alone.
```

HOC9 use:

```text
CueContextMap + AutomaticityProfile should remain separate but connected.
```

## 7. Dahm et al. 2026 — practice does not guarantee lower attentional cost

Stephan Frederic Dahm et al.
`Motor sequences resist automatization as attentional demands increase with sequence learning.`
npj Science of Learning. 2026;11(1):26.
DOI: 10.1038/s41539-026-00412-y
PMID: 41844636.
PMCID: PMC13144694.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41844636/

Participants practiced a sequence across ten sessions under instruction/cue conditions and were tested under single/dual-task conditions.

Retained pressure:

```text
robust sequence learning did not produce the expected monotonic reduction in sequence-specific dual-task cost;
trained-sequence attentional costs could remain/increase despite learning.
```

HOC9 use:

```text
Practice != AutomaticityGuarantee.
AutomaticityProfile != Skill/LearningTrajectory.
```

## 8. Current longitudinal habit-formation evidence, 2026

`What Makes a Habit? Investigating Potential Determinants of Habit Formation.`
Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin. 2026.
PMID: 42053513.
DOI: 10.1177/01461672261440446.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42053513/

Preregistered reanalysis of a six-month longitudinal dataset, N=254.

Retained pressure:

```text
habit trajectories included both formation and degradation clusters;
behavior frequency, context stability, behavioral complexity/difficulty and other factors related to automaticity trajectories under the dataset/model.
```

HOC9 use:

```text
HabitFormationTrajectory need not be monotonic.
No universal days-to-habit rule is admitted.
```

# 10. Evidence ceiling

The external sources establish scoped context, cue, automaticity and measurement effects.
They do not establish:

```text
one universal habit mechanism
one universal habit-strength metric
one universal formation duration
that every repeated behavior is habitual
that outcome-insensitivity always proves habit
that automatic behavior is always good or bad
```

HOC9 remains a practical synthesis with explicit evidence ceilings.
