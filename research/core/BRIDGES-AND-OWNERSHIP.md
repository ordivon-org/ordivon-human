# Human Bridges and Ownership

This document is a **current-owner overlay**. It does not renumber or delete Human research identities and does not rewrite historical source ledgers.

## Binding HF14–HF18 claim-level split

### HF14

```text
Human cognition/evaluation side → Human
norm applicability/deontic validity → Ordivon Normative
```

### HF15

```text
Human recognition/response → Human
standing/entitlement/obligation/permission → Ordivon Normative
```

### HF16

```text
population/welfare/preference inputs → Human
normative aggregation/priority → Ordivon Normative
mechanism/procedure → Game/Economics where appropriate
```

### HF17

```text
human plurality / empirical choice → Human
normative conflict/admission/consequence → Ordivon Normative
generic mechanism → Game/Economics
```

### HF18

```text
human strategic behavior → Human
generic strategy/incentive/mechanism → Game/Economics
validity/legitimacy/obligation → Ordivon Normative
operational mediation → Harness/Host
```

Therefore Human no longer acts as sole semantic authority for the mixed normative claims historically grouped under HF14–HF18. The HF identities remain valid compatibility/history handles.

## Other major owner boundaries

- **World:** external physical/reality facts, objective environment/place, acoustic waveform/propagation and external state. Human owns perception, representation, experience, learning, action and person-relative estimates.
- **Harness:** Agent/tool/run/orchestration/evidence-interface execution architecture. Human owns Human capability, cognition, learning, affect, verification, delegation effects and Human-side authority/consent. `HumanInLoop != MeaningfulControl`.
- **Game:** play/game/challenge/rules/case determination and generic game mechanisms. Human owns player-side perception, learning, action, affect, capability, relation and decision processes.
- **Media / symbolic:** Media owns external musical/artifact/expression objects; World owns physical signal; Human owns music cognition. HF23 owns thin Human-side generative-symbolic-system structure; communication-use remains HF12.
- **Economics / Finance / Institution:** Human may supply empirical behavior, welfare/preference inputs, work/production and person-side constraints; generic economic mechanisms, financial claims and institutional/legal authority remain with their appropriate owners.
