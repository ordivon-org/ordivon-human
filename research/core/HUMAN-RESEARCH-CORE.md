# Human Research Core

This directory is the **thin canonical navigation layer** for Ordivon Human research. It does not replace the detailed research corpus. Canonical reports, source ledgers, continuation records, negative results and applied evidence remain at their existing paths.

## Current architecture

| Layer | Current state | Canonical detail |
|---|---|---|
| Human Foundations (HF) | `HF0–HF23 = preserved / frozen`; `HF24 = UNKNOWN / not admitted` | [`../foundations/`](../foundations/) |
| Human Deep Foundations (HD) | `HD0–HD10 = completed history`; `HD11–HD16 = stage-complete history`; `NextHumanDeepRoute = UNKNOWN` | [`DEEP-DOMAIN-MAP.md`](DEEP-DOMAIN-MAP.md) |
| Human Operational Concepts (HOC) | `HOC0–HOC10 = frozen`; `HOC11 = UNKNOWN / not admitted` | [`OPERATIONAL-SURFACES.md`](OPERATIONAL-SURFACES.md) |

| Applied / bridge research | Human–Agent capability and Human economy remain separate applied surfaces | [`BRIDGES-AND-OWNERSHIP.md`](BRIDGES-AND-OWNERSHIP.md) |
| Methods / historical views | preserved; not current Human ontology | [`HISTORY-INDEX.md`](HISTORY-INDEX.md) |

No numeric successor is implied. In particular this core does **not** admit `HF24`, `HOC11`, or any new Human deep route.

## Layer invariant

```text
Deep != Primitive
OperationallyUseful != Foundation
NotFoundation != Useless
```

HF, HD and HOC remain separate layers. Family maps in this core are navigation/compression views over stable identities; they do not renumber, merge or delete the underlying research objects.

## Authority and ownership

Human owns Human-side empirical, cognitive, experiential, behavioral and operational claims within its admitted scope. It does **not** independently own generic normative semantic truth. The current claim-level owner overlay for `HF14–HF18` is binding for navigation and routing while the HF identities remain preserved for compatibility and research history. See [`BRIDGES-AND-OWNERSHIP.md`](BRIDGES-AND-OWNERSHIP.md).
