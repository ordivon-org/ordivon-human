## Current operational status

```text
HOC0–HOC10 = frozen
HOC11 = UNKNOWN / not admitted
WholeHumanOperationalClosure = NOT ESTABLISHED
WholeHumanExhaustion = NOT CLAIMED
```

HOC1–HOC6 and HOC8–HOC10 are high-value reusable decision/action surfaces. HOC7 remains explicitly non-diagnostic and authority-bounded.

### HOC10 guardrail

HOC10 is episode-first and privacy-sensitive. It does not authorize default continuous affect sensing, a permanent/global emotion-regulation score, or inference from affect to consent/intervention authority.

## Protocol boundary

`ExperientialProjectionProtocol`, `SelfModelProjectionProtocol` and `SupportedDecisionParticipationProtocol` are first-class practical assets but remain **unnumbered protocols**, not HOCs. See [`OPERATIONAL-PROTOCOLS.md`](OPERATIONAL-PROTOCOLS.md).

Detailed HOC reports, source ledgers, tournaments and closeouts remain under [`../operational-concepts/`](../operational-concepts/). Practical post-HOC high-information investigations remain under [`../high-information/`](../high-information/) and do not create another numbered operational layer.
