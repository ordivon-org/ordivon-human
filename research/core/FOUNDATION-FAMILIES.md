## HF14–HF18 current-owner overlay

HF identities remain stable. The overlay changes **claim routing**, not historical numbering.

| HF identity | Human owns | Neighbor owner(s) own |
|---|---|---|
| HF14 | Human cognition/evaluation side | **Ordivon Normative:** norm applicability and deontic validity |
| HF15 | Human recognition/response | **Ordivon Normative:** standing, entitlement, obligation and permission |
| HF16 | population, welfare and preference inputs | **Ordivon Normative:** normative aggregation and priority; **Game/Economics where appropriate:** mechanism/procedure |
| HF17 | Human plurality and empirical choice | **Ordivon Normative:** normative conflict, admission and consequence; **Game/Economics:** generic mechanism |
| HF18 | Human strategic behavior | **Game/Economics:** generic strategy, incentive and mechanism; **Ordivon Normative:** validity, legitimacy and obligation; **Harness/Host:** operational mediation |
