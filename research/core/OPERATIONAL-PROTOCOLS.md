## ProtocolSubstitutionTest

A candidate is a protocol rather than an HOC when replacing it with explicit calls to substantive owners preserves the domain action, and the remaining loss is chiefly evidence discipline, projection, comparison, routing or composition boilerplate.

All three retained protocols passed that test in the post-HOC9 closure audit.

```text
Methodological reuse != substantive-family ownership
Protocol discovery != HOC admission
```

Do not assign HOC numbers to these protocols for symmetry.
