# Research Questions

## Active H0 questions

### HUMAN-001 — Minimum dynamic human model

**Question:** What is the smallest model that can represent materially different questions about health, cognition, capability, resources, relationships, environment, development, and human–AI augmentation without collapsing them into one score?

Evidence paths:

- compare mature frameworks;
- encode several contrasting case studies;
- remove each proposed level and identify whether a real inference fails;
- compare against a flat variable table and a purely narrative account.

Falsification or narrowing:

- a simpler existing framework handles the same studies without loss;
- proposed layers do not change inference, measurement, or intervention design;
- the model produces terminology but no decision or explanatory gain.

### HUMAN-002 — Measurement taxonomy and provenance

**Question:** Which minimum distinctions among trait, state, behaviour, resource, context, event, trajectory, outcome, latent construct, and measurement process prevent recurring statistical errors?

Evidence paths:

- reanalyse published and personal-development examples;
- identify errors caused by cross-sectional snapshots, proxy substitution, or within/between-person conflation;
- test whether provenance and uncertainty change conclusions.

Falsification or narrowing:

- distinctions can be deleted without changing any analysis;
- the taxonomy cannot be applied consistently across independent studies;
- measurement overhead exceeds error reduction.

### HUMAN-003 — From association to personal intervention

**Question:** Under what conditions can population evidence inform a decision for one person, and what additional within-person evidence is needed?

Evidence paths:

- hierarchical and Bayesian models;
- longitudinal and N-of-1 designs;
- intervention and natural-experiment comparisons;
- simulations of heterogeneity, regression to the mean, carryover, and measurement error.

Falsification or narrowing:

- individual updating produces no better calibration or decisions than population averages;
- repeated personal measurement is too noisy or reactive to add value;
- the proposed design cannot distinguish intervention effects from trends and shocks.

## Ready after H0

### HUMAN-004 — Capability formation and transfer

How do health, practice, feedback, environment, education, tools, and opportunity interact to produce transferable real-world capability rather than only test performance?

### HUMAN-005 — Human–AI agency

When does AI increase durable human agency, and when does it create dependence, deskilling, goal displacement, manipulation, or concentration of control?

### HUMAN-006 — Life-course divergence

Which combinations of initial conditions, sensitive periods, feedback loops, shocks, and institutions produce cumulative advantage, disadvantage, resilience, or path reversal?

### HUMAN-007 — Plural outcomes and trade-offs

How should studies represent improvements that benefit one outcome or participant while harming another, especially across different time horizons?

## Not admitted yet

The following are intentionally not projects:

- a universal human ontology;
- a total life-logging application;
- a personal optimization score;
- an AI therapist or doctor;
- a population ranking engine;
- a digital twin claiming complete predictive fidelity;
- a mandatory Ordivon user model.

They may be reconsidered only after a bounded question and evidence demonstrate necessity and acceptable consequence.
