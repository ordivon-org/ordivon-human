# HUMAN-AI-001 Evidence-First Contraction

## Question

After building R1–R6, does `HUMAN-AI-001` actually need a dedicated direct-Human
measurement program, or can mature external Human evidence carry most of the
burden while Ordivon research concentrates on the system-specific residuals?

## Verdict

The first R1–R6 apparatus was methodologically valid, but its next-action policy
was too eager to move from population evidence to direct Human re-testing.

The corrected research order is:

```text
credible external Human evidence
        ↓
mechanism, moderator, and transport boundary
        ↓
Ordivon-specific structural analysis
        ↓
natural Ordivon / Human×Agent dogfood
        ↓
residual experiment only if a real decision remains unresolved
```

This is a contraction, not a rejection of individual heterogeneity. Methods M0
already demonstrates that a population average can differ from an individual
effect. The same M0 result also shows why data volume alone does not create
identification. The correct response is **selective transport analysis**, not
universal N-of-1 replication.

## Why external evidence is sufficient for the general layer

Current evidence already constrains several Human mechanisms strongly enough that
Ordivon should adopt them as defaults rather than reproduce them on one person:

1. **Retrieval and transfer.** Pan & Rickard's meta-analysis (2018, DOI
   `10.1037/bul0000151`) synthesized 192 transfer effects from 122 experiments
   (N=10,382) and found that retrieval practice can improve transfer relative to
   re-exposure, with substantial moderator dependence.
2. **Cognitive offloading and calibration.** Ngai & Gilbert (2026, DOI
   `10.1186/s41235-026-00714-0`) found across two controlled experiments that
   prediction paired with feedback reduced metacognitive bias and improved
   offloading choice; prediction alone did not reliably do so. The authors also
   explicitly leave cross-domain generalization open.
3. **AI learning is policy-dependent.** Kestin et al. (2025, DOI
   `10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6`) showed that a deliberately scaffolded AI tutor
   can improve learning in an authentic course setting. Separate 2026 randomized
   evidence/preprints report different retained-learning effects under more
   answer-oriented or automation-heavy assistance. Therefore `AI exposure` is
   not one intervention.
4. **Automation intervention.** Griffiths et al. (2024, DOI
   `10.1016/j.apergo.2024.104288`) found that stronger manual conflict-detection
   skill predicted faster and more accurate intervention after automation
   failure, while leaving substantial variance unexplained. This supports an
   intervention-substrate question without implying full manual expertise is
   universally required.
5. **Jagged AI capability.** Dell'Acqua et al. (2026, DOI
   `10.1287/orsc.2025.21838`) found large task-contingent differences in AI-aided
   knowledge-work performance. Delegation therefore needs task/failure evidence,
   not category labels alone.

These sources do not answer every Ordivon policy. They do answer enough of the
**general Human layer** that re-running the same mechanism on one ordinary user
would usually have low information gain.

## What external evidence does not answer

The remaining high-value questions are structural:

- Which Ordivon results have enough recurrence, half-life, verification leverage,
  or recovery value to deserve Human internalization?
- Which evidence projection is sufficient for Human supervision of a specific
  Host/Harness/Runtime decision without replaying every Agent step?
- Which actual Ordivon state changes should interrupt the Human loop?
- Which recovery substrate matters for Ordivon's real provider, authority,
  persistence, network, and execution failure modes?
- When does a new Human-facing mechanism outperform existing documents, Host
  continuity, or ordinary natural use enough to justify its maintenance cost?

These are `General Human science × Ordivon system` transport questions. They are
not primarily psychometric questions.

## R1–R6 disposition

| Line | Previous next step | Contracted disposition |
|---|---|---|
| R1 learning/distillation | run direct raw/summary/distillation comparison | **dormant fixture**; first solve knowledge-allocation and promotion policy from external learning science + Ordivon knowledge characteristics + natural later use |
| R2 capability surfaces | populate an individual multi-surface matrix | **retain distinctions, delete scorecard**; observe only surfaces naturally exposed or needed for a live decision |
| R3 selective audit | run blinded Human full/selective/conclusion review | **system/interface first**; replay historical failures, adversarially attack evidence surfaces, and use direct Human comparison only if residual interface uncertainty survives |
| R4 delegation/recovery | inject bounded failures and score Human recovery | **natural recovery first**; use actual provider/interface/authority failures before manufacturing perturbations |
| R5 timescale | retrospective + prospective system comparison | **retain active**; this is genuinely Ordivon-specific and external literature does not choose the trigger semantics |
| R6 calibration | seed standing Human prediction series | **contract strongly**; forecasts are optional tools for selected consequential, resolvable decisions, not a research quota |

## Personal / residual experiment admission

A direct Human experiment is reopened only when:

1. external evidence and Ordivon structural analysis leave competing policies;
2. choosing among them changes a real decision;
3. there is a named reason to expect meaningful heterogeneity in the target
   context, or repeated natural evidence contradicts the transported prior;
4. the result can resolve on a useful timescale;
5. natural dogfood cannot provide the same information at lower burden;
6. the experiment remains bounded and reversible.

If those conditions are absent, **no direct Human experiment is required**.

## What remains active now

The active frontier contracts to three main problems:

### C/E — Evidence projection and knowledge promotion

Use external learning/human-factors constraints plus historical Ordivon failures
to determine what the Human-facing evidence surface must contain. Test fields and
promotion gates by deletion and adversarial replay before requesting direct Human
measurement.

### D — Natural recovery substrate

Observe future real incidents. Ask which retained Human/world-model substrate
actually changed intervention, provider switching, or recovery. Do not create a
standing capability battery.

### G / R5 — Timescale coordination

Continue the already frozen prospective comparison of checkpoint, fixed-cadence,
and event-triggered coordination across eligible Ordivon Tasks. This is a
system-level policy question and remains the strongest direct experiment from
Round 1.

## Implication for the Human Learning Harness

The contraction makes a dedicated Harness **less**, not more, justified. The
current evidence supports a research model, evidence-transport rule, and a small
set of Ordivon system policies. A new subsystem is admitted only after lighter
surfaces repeatedly fail on a named problem.

## Final compression

```text
Do not study the person because measurement is available.
Study the general Human mechanism from the best external evidence.
Study Ordivon's residual system problem where the external evidence stops.
Use direct Human experimentation only when that residual cannot otherwise be
resolved and the answer would change action.
```
