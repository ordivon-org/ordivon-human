# HD16 — Primary Evidence Ledger

## 1. Acquired amusia vs language

Sihvonen et al. 2024, J Neurosci. PMID 38423761. DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1922-23.2024.

Independent 97-patient prospective cohort: acquired-amusia lesion network centered on right superior temporal cortex and distinct from aphasia network.

Dibbs & Moeller 2025, Neurology. PMID 39889265. DOI 10.1212/WNL.0000000000213410.

Isolated acquired amusia after right temporal stroke.

Peretz et al. classic amusia-without-aphasia cases provide historical convergent evidence.

## 2. Internal music fractionation

Piccirilli et al. 2000, JNNP. PMID 10990522. DOI 10.1136/jnnp.69.4.541.

Pure amusia with melodic impairment and preserved rhythm patterns, normal speech/environmental-sound processing.

Pitch and Rhythm Perception after TBI, PMID 34573194.

Pitch and rhythm deficits only partially co-occur; verbal STM often preserved despite musical deficits.

Verbal and musical STM after stroke, PMID 28088063.

Individual double dissociations between verbal and musical short-term memory.

## 3. Beat perception vs action

Bégel et al. 2017, Neuropsychologia 94:129–138. PMID 27914979. DOI 10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2016.11.022.

Poor explicit beat/rhythm perception can coexist with relatively preserved synchronization and implicit temporal benefit.

Use:

```text
BeatPerception != BeatSynchronization
ExplicitTiming != ImplicitTiming
```

## 4. Congenital amusia

Hoarau et al. 2024, Neuropsychologia 202:108960. PMID 39032629. DOI 10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2024.108960.

Broad pitch/music/speech/auditory battery; supports strong pitch vulnerability while testing cross-domain consequences.

Lagrois & Peretz 2019, Cortex. PMID 30685578. DOI 10.1016/j.cortex.2018.11.036.

Pitch-deafness and beat deficits can co-occur but dissociate in some individuals.

## 5. Perception vs reward

Mas-Herrero et al. 2014, Current Biology. PMID 24613311. DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2014.01.068.

Specific musical anhedonia: low music pleasure despite preserved music perception and normal monetary reward responses.

Martínez-Molina et al. 2016, PNAS. PMID 27799544. DOI 10.1073/pnas.1611211113.

Music anhedonia associated with reduced auditory-cortex/ventral-striatum coupling while monetary-reward activation remains normal.

Belfi et al. 2017, Neuropsychologia. PMID 28159618. DOI 10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2017.01.030.

Rare acquired musical anhedonia after focal brain damage.

## 6. Perception vs reward/imagery/memory in current lesion case

2025 right temporal resection case, PMID 41313951.

Deficits in scale/key/contour/interval with preserved speech-in-babble, normal music reward, and average beat perception, musical imagery and memory.

This is high-value within-route dissociation evidence.

## 7. Musical emotion

Amusic adults can show impaired pitch processing with partially preserved recognition/intensity of musical emotion depending on task and cues.

Key primary sources:

```text
PMID 26226563
PMID 30047757
```

Use:

```text
PitchCompetence != MusicalEmotionTotality
EmotionRecognition != EmotionalIntensity
```

## 8. Shared auditory streams without identity

Post-stroke aprosodia and amusia can share right ventral auditory-stream damage (PMID 34661326), showing overlap between prosodic/music processing.

This is a warning:

```text
SharedAuditoryPathway != SharedSemanticOwner
```

# Evidence conclusion

The evidence strongly supports music-related target selectivity and several narrow process families, but it does not support one peer Human music owner after HF20/HF8/HD12/HF11/HF4/HF21/HF6/HF7 restoration.
