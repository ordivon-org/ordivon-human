# 1. First firewall — three different senses of “body”

The route must begin by separating:

```text
PhysicalBody
RepresentedBody
ExperiencedBody
```

They are not interchangeable.

## PhysicalBody

World/HF1-side facts such as:

```text
tissue / limb / organ / morphology
biological parthood
physical pose and movement
actual contact
actual body location
causal action trajectory
```

## RepresentedBody

Human-side encoded or estimated body-related content such as:

```text
limb position estimate
body configuration
reachable-body geometry
body-part relations
current body-state estimate
structural body model
self/action attribution content
```

## ExperiencedBody

Phenomenal variables such as:

```text
felt mineness
felt bodily presence
felt ownership
felt movement
felt agency
felt self-location
```

Therefore:

```text
PhysicalBody != RepresentedBody != ExperiencedBody
```

A change in one can alter another without identity.

---

# 2. Biological parthood is not body ownership

A biological hand remains part of an organism even when a patient disowns it.
A rubber/virtual/prosthetic hand can acquire some experienced ownership without becoming biological tissue.

Therefore:

```text
BiologicalParthood
!= BodyOwnership
```

Allocation pressure:

```text
biological / organismic membership → HF1 + World
experienced ownership             → HF2/HF20/HF8 boundary under HD15
```

HD15 must never use subjective ownership to rewrite objective organism identity.

---

# 3. `BodyRepresentation` is an umbrella, not one format

Current literature uses body representation for multiple distinct objects:

```text
current limb-position estimate
posture/configuration estimate
body size / shape estimate
structural body-part model
sensorimotor action representation
perceptual body appearance
semantic/conceptual body knowledge
experienced body ownership
```

These cannot be collapsed.

Use:

```text
BodyRepresentation
= declared family label
```

unless the content, timescale, reference frame and consumer are specified.

---

# 4. Body schema and body image must remain separate — but not reified into two universal boxes

A useful operational contrast survives:

```text
BodySchema
≈ online / action-oriented body-state representation used to guide movement

BodyImage
≈ perceptual / conscious / conceptual representation of body appearance or body properties
```

Healthy kinaesthetic-illusion experiments and neuropsychological cases show perception/action bodily representations can dissociate.

But two cautions are mandatory:

```text
BodySchema != AllImplicitBodyProcessing
BodyImage != AllConsciousBodyProcessing
```

and:

```text
SchemaImageDissociation
!= ProofOfExactlyTwoBodyRepresentations
```

Current evidence is compatible with more than two typed representational families.

---

# 5. `BodyModel` is a different term again

`Body model` is often used for relatively stable structural expectations or priors such as:

```text
corporeal form
body-part topology
plausible posture
size/shape constraints
self-body structural regularities
```

Classic rubber-hand work shows synchrony alone is not always sufficient: corporeal plausibility, posture and reference-frame constraints matter.

But active control can also induce bodily effects for unusual/non-corporeal proxies in some paradigms.

Therefore:

```text
BodyModel
!= FixedHumanShapeTemplateByDefinition
```

and:

```text
StructuralPrior
× SensorimotorContingency
× Task/Context
```

must be tested rather than assumed.

---

# 6. Current body-state estimate is not the same as body schema or ownership

A useful narrower object is:

```text
BodyStateEstimate(t)
```

with declared coordinates such as:

```text
limb position
orientation
joint configuration
movement state
uncertainty
reference frame
```

It can be updated by vision, proprioception, vestibular evidence, touch and action prediction.

But:

```text
BodyStateEstimate
!= BodyOwnership
```

A major empirical reason is that proprioceptive drift and subjective ownership dissociate.

---

# 7. Proprioception is evidence, not transparent body truth

Proprioceptive signals contribute strongly to bodily estimation and ownership phenomena.

However:

```text
ProprioceptiveSignal
!= TrueBodyState
!= BodyOwnership
```

Proprioceptive uncertainty can increase rubber-hand ownership illusions; movement-derived proprioceptive signals can support ownership even when some local cutaneous afferents are blocked.

This makes proprioception an HF20 evidence channel and calibration problem, not a semantic owner of bodily selfhood.

---

# 8. `Proprioceptive drift` is a measure of localization change, not ownership by definition

This is one of HD15-A's strongest measurement corrections.

Multiple experiments show:

```text
ProprioceptiveDrift
can occur
without SubjectiveOwnership
```

and mechanically changing sensed hand position does not necessarily change ownership strength.

Therefore:

```text
ProprioceptiveDrift
= hand/body-location readout under a declared task
!= BodyOwnershipMeasureByDefinition
```

A study may report both; they must remain separate outcome columns.

---

# 9. Touch, vision and synchrony are inputs/manipulations, not the ownership process

The classical rubber-hand manipulation often uses:

```text
visual hand proxy
+
seen touch
+
felt touch
+
temporal/spatial congruence
```

But ownership can be induced with different cue combinations, including active/passive movement and nonvisual variants.

Thus:

```text
VisuotactileSynchrony
!= BodyOwnership
```

It is one manipulation capable of changing evidence for a common body-related cause.

---

# 10. Vestibular signals participate but do not equal self-location or ownership

Vestibular perturbation can influence body-part ownership and body localization.

Spatial-navigation work also showed vestibular perception, displacement integration and navigation can dissociate.

Therefore:

```text
VestibularSignal
!= SelfLocation
!= BodyOwnership
```

The vestibular contribution must be typed as sensory evidence feeding a declared body/spatial target.

---

# 11. Interoception is not one bodily-self owner

Interoceptive/affective bodily signals can modulate ownership and self-identification effects.

But experimental effects differ across ownership, self-identification and interoceptive readouts.

Therefore:

```text
Interoception
!= BodyOwnership
!= SelfIdentification
```

HF5/HF20 own organismic/interoceptive sensing and regulation surfaces; HD15 asks how such evidence participates in bodily-self inference/experience.

---

# 12. Body ownership — use a typed `BodyOwnershipEpisode`

Instead of one scalar trait, define:

```text
BodyOwnershipEpisode = {
  target_body_or_part,
  biological_or_proxy_status,
  part_or_full_body_scale,
  perspective,
  sensory_evidence,
  motor_contingency,
  spatial_alignment,
  structural_plausibility,
  current_report_or_behavioral_readout,
  onset_and_duration,
  uncertainty
}
```

This prevents one questionnaire item from silently becoming an ontology.

---

# 13. Body-part ownership and full-body ownership are distinct levels

Healthy experiments show local part ownership can be induced while full-body ownership is weak or absent; scrambled-body and hierarchical multi-part paradigms directly separate the two.

Therefore:

```text
BodyPartOwnership
!= FullBodyOwnership
```

Possible relations include:

```text
local → global integration
whole-body context → local modulation
```

but neither direction is definitionally mandatory.

This leaves a real `PartWholeBodyIntegrationProblem` for HD15-B.

---

# 14. Disownership is not merely low ownership

Clinical disownership can involve positive misattribution of a biological limb to another person, rapid perspective-dependent reversals, or body-part-specific delusional attribution.

In healthy illusion paradigms, increased proxy ownership does not automatically imply equivalent loss of biological-body ownership.

Therefore:

```text
Disownership
!= LowOwnershipByDefinition
```

and:

```text
ProxyOwnershipGain
!= BiologicalBodyDisownership
```

Record positive ownership and positive disownership separately where the paradigm permits.

---

# 15. Ownership and self-location can dissociate

Full-body VR experiments show:

```text
SelfLocationChange
can occur
without corresponding FullBodyOwnershipChange
```

under some perspective/spatial-overlap conditions.

Thus:

```text
SelfLocation
= experienced/estimated position of the self/body in environment
!= BodyOwnership
```

HD14 owns generic spatial-state/reference-frame architecture; HD15 owns the bodily-self research problem at the Human side of that interface.

---

# 16. First-person perspective is not body ownership

First-person visual perspective can strongly constrain full-body illusion formation, but perspective and ownership can be manipulated separately.

Therefore:

```text
FirstPersonPerspective
!= BodyOwnership
```

and:

```text
VisualPerspective
= evidence / task geometry
```

not a semantic owner.

Perspective-dependent reversals in somatoparaphrenia make this especially important.

---

# 17. Self-identification is not automatically ownership

Avatar/full-body paradigms sometimes ask whether a participant identifies with, resembles, or feels located in a proxy.

These questions can track different manipulations.

So:

```text
SelfIdentification
!= BodyOwnership
!= SelfLocation
```

Every study must specify which judgment was actually collected.

---

# 18. `Embodiment` is an umbrella outcome, not a primitive

In VR/prosthetic/tool literature, embodiment may bundle:

```text
ownership
agency
self-location
body-schema change
self-identification
anatomical plausibility
presence
```

Therefore:

```text
Embodiment
= umbrella / composite unless decomposed
```

Never infer all components from a single `embodiment score`.

# 20. Ownership and agency dissociate directly

Moving-rubber-hand experiments provide a clean manipulation:

```text
passive synchronous movement
→ ownership can remain
→ agency can disappear
```

and anatomically incongruent proxy posture can reduce ownership while leaving agency comparatively preserved.

Therefore:

```text
BodyOwnership
!= SenseOfAgency
```

Interaction is real; identity is false.

---

# 21. Action intention is not one event

Agency experiments distinguish at least:

```text
distal intention
proximal/immediate intention
action selection
motor preparation
motor execution
```

They can influence agency measures differently.

Therefore:

```text
Intention
!= MotorCommand
!= AgencyJudgment
```

HF10/HF11 already provide decision/action sequencing; HD15 should not invent a second action ontology.

---

# 22. Motor prediction / efference is not agency by itself

Action prediction can contribute prospective evidence for agency, but agency judgments can also use retrospective outcome information.

Current causal stimulation and EEG/BMI evidence support temporally and functionally differentiated agency-relevant processing.

Therefore:

```text
MotorPrediction
!= SenseOfAgency
```

and:

```text
ProspectiveAgencyEvidence
!= RetrospectiveAgencyEvidence
```

remain open architecture questions for HD15-B.

---

# 23. Motor control, control detection and self-attribution are separate targets

Clinical experimental work shows these levels can have different impairment profiles.

Use:

```text
MotorControl
= ability to produce/steer the movement

ControlDetection
= discrimination of sensorimotor contingency / whether output matches self-control

SelfAttribution
= judgment that I authored/caused the event
```

Thus:

```text
MotorControl != ControlDetection != SelfAttribution
```

A task that measures one must not be labeled `agency` without qualification.

---

# 24. Feeling of agency and judgment of agency need not be the same measurement level

Use:

```text
FeelingOfAgency
= pre-reflective / phenomenal control experience where evidence supports that interpretation

JudgmentOfAgency
= explicit report / attribution under task instructions
```

The distinction is methodological, not a claim that two unique neural modules exist.

Explicit judgment depends strongly on question wording, outcome knowledge, confidence and inference.

---

# 25. Implicit agency measures are not transparent agency meters

Kinematic conflict signatures can carry information absent from explicit agency reports.

But the reverse inference is invalid:

```text
ImplicitDifference
→ AgencyMechanism
```

without a task-specific causal argument.

An implicit measure may track:

```text
sensorimotor prediction error
temporal expectancy
action-effect learning
motor adaptation
attention
response strategy
```

rather than phenomenal agency.

---

# 26. Intentional binding receives a strong firewall

Intentional binding is temporal attraction/compression between action and outcome under particular paradigms.

It has often been used as an implicit agency measure.

However:

* explicit agency and binding sometimes correlate;
* temporal expectancy can contribute;
* observed actions can also produce binding-like effects;
* a 2024 experimental challenge found no evidence for a specifically “intentional” binding component under its tests;
* binding changes during learning.

Therefore:

```text
IntentionalBinding
= temporal-perception/action-effect phenomenon under declared paradigm
!= SenseOfAgencyByDefinition
```

HD12 owns the temporal-estimation side; HD15 may consume binding only as one agency-related measurement surface.

---

# 27. Threat / skin-conductance response is not ownership by definition

Threat to a rubber/virtual hand can evoke autonomic responses.

But threat-related physiological response can dissociate from ownership manipulation and can occur even when ownership is weak.

Therefore:

```text
ThreatSCR
!= BodyOwnership
```

It is an affective/autonomic response to a body-related or threat-related event under a declared context.

HF21/HF5 participation must remain visible.

---

# 28. Questionnaire report is a measurement surface, not the phenomenon itself

An ownership/agency questionnaire depends on:

```text
item wording
scale anchors
comparison/control items
participant interpretation
memory interval
expectancy / demand characteristics
metacognitive access
```

Thus:

```text
QuestionnaireScore
!= Mechanism
```

HD15 evidence should retain item-level semantics when possible.

---

# 29. Neural localization is implementation evidence, not owner identity

Lesion, stimulation, intracranial, fMRI and EEG evidence can establish:

```text
necessity pressure
causal contribution
timing
network dependence
selective implementation
```

But:

```text
BrainRegion
!= BodyOwnership
BrainRegion
!= Agency
```

Distributed lesion/disconnection effects in post-stroke disownership make a one-region semantic owner especially implausible.

---

# 30. Somatoparaphrenia is a syndrome/phenotype, not the definition of disownership

Somatoparaphrenia can involve delusional attribution of a contralesional limb to another person.

It may co-occur with hemiplegia, neglect, somatosensory impairment and anosognosia, but these are not definitional identities.

Perspective manipulations can transiently restore ownership judgment in some cases.

Therefore:

```text
Somatoparaphrenia
!= GenericBodyOwnershipFailure
```

and:

```text
ClinicalLabel
!= Mechanism
```

---

# 31. Anosognosia for hemiplegia is not somatoparaphrenia

Motor-awareness deficits and disturbed limb ownership can overlap strongly after right-hemisphere stroke, but selective cases and lesion analyses support separation.

Therefore:

```text
AnosognosiaForHemiplegia
!= Somatoparaphrenia
!= Neglect
```

This is a crucial lesion-based destructive test for any unitary bodily-self theory.

---

# 32. Neglect is not body disownership

Personal or spatial neglect can alter attention toward body/peripersonal space and interact with body illusions.

But neglect can occur without somatoparaphrenia, and ownership disturbance can have different lesion/behavioral profiles.

Therefore:

```text
Neglect
!= Disownership
```

HF3 attention/access must be restored before claiming a new bodily-self owner.

---

# 33. Alien-hand phenomena are not simple loss of agency

`Alien hand` is itself a heterogeneous clinical family involving involuntary/poorly controlled action, estrangement, conflict or abnormal attribution depending on syndrome.

Do not compress it into:

```text
AlienHand = NoSenseOfAgency
```

HD15-B should use well-characterized cases only when exact motor control, awareness, ownership and attribution are separately reported.

---

# 34. Development proves bodily-self weighting is not fixed

Children and adults can show different visual/proprioceptive weighting while explicit embodiment follows a different developmental pattern.

Developmental RHI studies show at least two separable trajectories:

```text
visual–tactile contribution to explicit embodiment
visual–proprioceptive contribution to hand localization
```

Thus:

```text
AdultCueWeighting
!= DevelopmentalConstant
```

and again:

```text
Localization
!= Ownership
```

---

# 35. Active and passive conditions must be typed

Action can change ownership/agency, but `active` is not one manipulation.

Separate:

```text
voluntary action selection
self-generated movement
motor command availability
efference/prediction
movement-contingent visual feedback
passive matched kinematics
external movement
```

Moving-RHI studies show ownership can survive passive movement while agency does not.
Other studies find similar ownership strength across active, passive and visuotactile induction.

Therefore:

```text
ActiveAdvantage
!= Universal
```

---

# 36. Real / rubber / virtual / robotic / prosthetic / tool targets must not be conflated

For every proxy, specify:

```text
biological status
corporeal appearance
anatomical plausibility
control mapping
latency
spatial overlap
perspective
feedback modalities
persistence of use
```

A virtual avatar, rubber hand, myoelectric robot hand, prosthesis and ordinary tool are not one regime.

---

# 37. Tool embodiment is not prosthetic embodiment

A tool may alter action-space/body-schema measures without being experienced as a body part.

A prosthesis may have long-term body-related functional and ownership relations unlike a transient tool.

Thus:

```text
ToolUse
!= ToolEmbodiment
!= ProstheticEmbodiment
```

and all remain distinct from biological incorporation.

---

# 38. Avatar ownership is not personal identity

VR can generate ownership and agency toward bodies that differ substantially from the participant's physical body.

But:

```text
AvatarOwnership
!= PersonalIdentity
!= LegalIdentity
```

HF1 identity and Interface avatar mechanics remain separate owners.

---

# 39. BMI-mediated agency is a distinct Human-machine regime

Brain-machine interfaces can separate:

```text
neural intention-related state
physical limb movement
external device action
feedback
agency judgment
```

This makes BMI a powerful destructive regime.

But:

```text
BMIControl
!= NaturalMotorExecution
```

and the decoding/execution mechanism belongs to Computing/Interface, while HD15 studies the Human-side agency/ownership effects.

# 40. `Bodily self-consciousness` is also an umbrella

Use it only as a research family over variables such as:

```text

self-location
perspective
agency
bodily presence/mineness
self-identification
```

Do not turn the family name into one process.
