# 1. Why the whole search was necessary

HD9 closed a major biological domain but did not establish global Human coverage.
The inventory deliberately prevented local adjacency from becoming direction authority.

The fresh search therefore asks:

```text
What can the current Human model describe about a generic Human?
What can it describe about variation among Humans?
What can it describe about one Human changing across contexts and time?
What can it infer from population-level measurement without confusing that with an individual's mechanism?
What changes when capability is partly realized through tools or persistent agents?
```

The distinction is crucial:

```text
UniversalHumanSkeleton != IndividualHumanModel
PopulationStructure != IndividualDynamics
ObservedPerformance != InternalCapability
StableDifference != FixedEssence
```

Yet no current HF owns the grammar of stable heterogeneity itself.

## 7.2 Low current ownership

HD7 reconstructed **sources of genetic variation**, not the phenotype architecture those sources may influence.

```text
GeneticVariation != Personality
Heritability != TraitIdentity
PolygenicPrediction != IndividualMechanism
```

HF6 owns change; it does not by itself define stable person-specific distributions.
HF3/HF7/HF9 own cognitive processes; they do not decide what `intelligence` denotes.
HF1 owns identity/persistence; it does not own descriptive dispositional structure.

## 7.3 Repeated category-error risk

The literature itself forces separations between:

```text
trait and state
person and situation
stability and invariance
ability and performance
latent factor and causal mechanism
population structure and individual dynamics
capacity and supported capability
```

These are directly reusable world-model boundaries.

## 7.4 Strong falsifier density

High-information tests include:

```text
same person across sharply different situations
same group-level factor model / different individual dynamics
same test score / different process profile
same observed performance / different tool or agent support
stable mean / changing state distribution shape
cross-cultural non-replication or altered factor structure
developmental continuity with substantial mean-level change
intervention-induced state change without trait erasure
```

```text
CognitionAtWork != BrainProcessOnly
```

However, its natural unit often includes:

```text

```

That makes it primarily a **cross-cutting bridge / Human×World×Institution×Harness ownership problem**.

It remains a serious later route or cross-project audit, but it is less boundary-clean as the immediate Human deep route than individual-difference architecture.
