# Post-HD9 Human-Wide Direction Search — Evidence Ledger

## Rule

These sources constrain direction selection; none is treated as a universal ontology by itself.

```text
PublishedConstruct != HumanPrimitive
FactorModel != CausalMechanism
Prediction != Identity
CrossCulturalReplication != UniversalByDefinition
AgentPerformanceGain != HumanLearning
```

---

# S01 — Fleeson & Jayawickreme (2015), Whole Trait Theory

Citation:

William Fleeson & Eranda Jayawickreme. “Whole Trait Theory.” Journal of Research in Personality 56 (2015): 82–92. DOI: 10.1016/j.jrp.2014.10.009. PMID 26097268; PMCID PMC4472377.

Pressure:

```text
TraitDescriptor != FixedBehavior
TraitDescription can be represented as a distribution of states
Stable person differences can coexist with large within-person variability
Explanatory social-cognitive processes != descriptive trait factor
```

Direction implication: personality is not exhausted by a trait vector or a measurement scale.

---

# S02 — Schmiedek et al. (2020), within-person cognitive structure

Citation:

Florian Schmiedek, Martin Lövdén, Timo von Oertzen & Ulman Lindenberger. “Within-person structures of daily cognitive performance differ from between-person structures of cognitive abilities.” PeerJ 8:e9290 (2020). DOI: 10.7717/peerj.9290. PMID 32551201; PMCID PMC7292017.

Design:

```text
101 adults
9 cognitive tasks
100 occasions across ~6 months
```

Pressure:

```text
BetweenPersonAbilityStructure != WithinPersonCognitiveStructure
Population g-like covariance != one person's dynamic organization
```

This is one of the strongest direct falsifiers of using population psychometric structure as an individual mechanism model.

# S03 — Gurven et al. (2013), Big Five among Tsimane

Citation:

Michael Gurven et al. “How Universal Is the Big Five? Testing the Five-Factor Model of Personality Variation Among Forager–Farmers in the Bolivian Amazon.” Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. PMID 23245291; PMCID PMC4104167.

Pressure:

The translated Big Five inventory did not robustly recover the expected five-factor structure in the studied Tsimane samples under the authors' tests.

```text
EticTraitVocabulary != GuaranteedCrossCulturalStructure
MeasurementTransport != OntologyTransport
```

The result is not proof that the Big Five is false everywhere; it is a strong transport falsifier.

# S04 — Lukaszewski et al. / niche-diversity cross-national personality structure

Source surfaced in the global search as “Niche Diversity Predicts Personality Structure Across 115 Nations,” PMCID PMC13171031.

Pressure:

Personality covariance/dimensionality varies with socioecological niche diversity in the reported cross-national analysis.

```text
PersonalityStructure may be context-sensitive at population scale
PopulationFactorization != context-free human essence
```

Representative primary evidence:

Antonio Terracciano et al., Baltimore Longitudinal Study of Aging analysis of Guilford-Zimmerman temperament data, PMCID PMC2754731.

Prospective toddlerhood-to-middle-childhood temperament stability study, PMCID PMC2902199.

```text
RelativeStability != NoDevelopmentalChange
Temperament/trait continuity can coexist with mean-level and individual trajectory change
```

# S06 — Wang et al. (2021), executive processes and fluid intelligence

Citation:

Tengfei Wang et al. “How Executive Processes Explain the Overlap between Working Memory Capacity and Fluid Intelligence: A Test of Process Overlap Theory.” Journal of Intelligence 9(2):21 (2021). PMCID PMC8167629.

Pressure:

The tested executive-process account did not reduce fluid-intelligence overlap to the proposed specific executive components; common EF explained substantial shared variance while specific components did not provide the simple expected mapping.

Direction implication:

```text
IntelligenceFactor != one already-known executive process by default
```

# S07 — Grinschgl, Papenmeier & Meyerhoff (2021), cognitive offloading

Citation:

Sandra Grinschgl, Frank Papenmeier & Hauke S. Meyerhoff. “Consequences of cognitive offloading: Boosting performance but diminishing memory.” Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology 74(9):1477–1496 (2021). DOI: 10.1177/17470218211008060. PMCID PMC8358584.

Pressure:

Externalizing cognitive work can improve immediate task performance while changing subsequent internal memory outcomes.

```text
SupportedPerformance != InternalMemoryState
ImmediateCapability != RetainedLearning
```

# S08 — Wu et al. (2025), Human–GenAI collaboration

Citation:

Suqing Wu, Yukun Liu, Mengqi Ruan, Siyu Chen et al. “Human-generative AI collaboration enhances task performance but undermines human’s intrinsic motivation.” Scientific Reports 15, 15105 (2025). DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-98385-2.

Design:

Four online experiments, total N=3,562, comparing Human–ChatGPT collaboration and solo conditions across writing/idea-generation tasks.

Pressure:

```text
CollaborativePerformanceGain != PersistentSoloPerformanceGain
AIContribution != HumanSkillAcquisition
JointOutcome != HumanIndependentCapability
```

The experiments also pressure perceived control and motivation boundaries.

# S09 — Hutchins, distributed cognition / cognition in the wild

Primary-author publication surface:

Edwin Hutchins, UC San Diego publication archive and research pages, including:

```text
Cognition in the Wild (1995)
How a Cockpit Remembers Its Speeds (1995)
Distributed Cognition: A New Foundation for Human-Computer Interaction Research (Hollan, Hutchins & Kirsh, 2000)
Material Anchors for Conceptual Blends (2005)
```

Pressure:

```text
CognitiveProcess != BrainBoundByDefinition
TaskSystemMemory != IndividualMemoryOnly
ArtifactStructure can participate in cognitive work
```

Direction implication: a serious rival to purely person-internal units, but ownership naturally spans Human, artifacts, groups and institutions.

# S10 — Engeström, activity theory / expansive learning

Primary-author archival source:

Yrjö Engeström. Learning by Expanding: An Activity-Theoretical Approach to Developmental Research (1987; later author-hosted editions/introductions at UCSD LCHC).

Key pressure from the author's framework:

```text
activity may be object-oriented
artifact-mediated
collective
historically changing
embedded in interacting activity systems
```

Direction implication: Human foundations should not assume the individual is always the correct unit of analysis. However, this is precisely why activity-system theory is a cross-cutting bridge rather than a clean Human-only next object.

# S11 — idiographic personality-state evidence (2026)

Source:

“Idiographic Momentary Profiles of Personality Facets,” PMCID PMC12262167 (2026).

Pressure:

The reported experience-sampling analysis found multiple person-specific momentary profiles and situation-predictive profile expression.

```text
Personality != one static point
IdiographicDynamics != PopulationAverageDynamics
```

This source is recent and should be treated as one empirical pressure, not closure.

# S12 — cross-cultural cognitive measurement transport

Representative primary evidence:

“Cross-National Generalizability of WISC-V and CHC Broad Ability Constructs across France, Spain, and the US,” PMCID PMC10455271.

Counter-pressure:

The study reports strict factorial invariance for the tested WISC-V five-factor model across the three Western national standardization samples.

This is retained because the evidence is not one-directional:

```text
SomeAbilityStructuresTransport
!= AllAbilityStructuresUniversal
!= IndividualMechanismIdentity
```

# Evidence synthesis

The direction decision is driven by the conjunction:

```text
stable between-person differences are real enough to predict and persist
+ within-person expression is highly variable and situation-sensitive
+ population factor structures need not equal individual dynamics
+ cross-cultural transport is conditional rather than automatic
+ developmental stability coexists with change
+ tools/AI separate observed task performance from independent retained capability
+ pre-Agent activity/distributed-cognition traditions challenge person-bound units
```

Therefore a deep reconstruction of individual-difference architecture has unusually high falsifier leverage and cross-foundation value.
