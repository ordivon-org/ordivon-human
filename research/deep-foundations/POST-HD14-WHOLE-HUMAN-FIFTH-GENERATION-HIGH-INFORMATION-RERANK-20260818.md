# 7. Part ownership and full-body ownership are not one process by definition

A 2024 Human illusion experiment independently manipulated visuotactile synchrony across several body parts.

It found that local body-part ownership was driven primarily by local synchrony and could occur relatively independently of full-body ownership, whereas full-body ownership depended nonlinearly on how many parts were coherently bound.

Therefore:

```text
BodyPartOwnership
!= FullBodyOwnershipByDefinition
```

while both can interact hierarchically.

This is unusually strong pressure against a single undifferentiated `BodyOwnershipModule`.

---

# 8. Ownership and self-location can interact without being identical

Recent VR/full-body work shows spatial prediction and body-location manipulation can alter ownership/disownership and perceived body position.

Earlier full-body-illusion work already showed selective modulation of self-location without corresponding ownership under some perspective/overlap conditions.

Thus:

```text
BodyOwnership
!= SelfLocation
```

and:

```text
BodyPositionEstimate

OwnershipExperience
without being identical to it.
```

This directly pressures HF20 spatial/multisensory evidence, HF8 represented body state, HF2 experienced mineness and the HD14 spatial boundary.

# 9. Sense of agency is internally plural

Current Human evidence rejects:

```text
SenseOfAgency = one transparent report variable
```

A 2025 two-experiment study showed that implicit hand-kinematic signatures contained sensorimotor-conflict information that exceeded participants' explicit congruence/agency judgments.

Therefore:

```text
ImplicitSensorimotorConflictInformation
!= ExplicitAgencyJudgment
```

A 2024 schizophrenia study separated:

```text
motor control
control detection from sensorimotor information
self-attribution / agency evaluation
```

with the first two more impaired than the final evaluative layer.

Thus:

```text
MotorControl
!= ControlDetection
!= SelfAttributionByDefinition
```

A 2026 preregistered EEG embodied-VR study further found an early condition-specific sensorimotor-conflict response followed by later shared patterns across conflict types, consistent with temporal decomposition rather than one-stage agency.

# 10. Explicit bodily-self judgment can dissociate from implicit body-schema change

A 2025 pilot comparison of Functional Motor Disorder, Irritable Bowel Syndrome and healthy controls found explicit ownership/agency responses could remain present while implicit body-schema drift differed across groups.

The sample is small, so it is not an owner proof.

But it directly pressures:

```text
ExplicitEmbodimentReport
!= ImplicitBodySchemaUpdate
```

and demonstrates why a whole-domain route must type measurement surface rather than infer mechanism from one questionnaire.

# 11. Lesions show real selective bodily-disownership phenomena

A 2025 stroke study examined 105 patients and 55 controls using multiple ownership measures plus lesion/disconnection analysis.

Subtle covert disownership was found in body-part-specific forms involving hands, arms, legs or face, and was associated with distributed disconnection patterns rather than one simple focal `ownership center`.

Thus:

```text
BodyPartDisownership
= real lesion-sensitive phenomenon
```

but:

```text
SelectiveLesionability
!= PeerSemanticOwner
```

The result instead increases the need to distinguish local body-part representation, multisensory ownership, awareness/report and broader self structure.

---

# 12. Causal perturbation confirms agency mechanisms without establishing a new owner

A 2025 double-blind sham-controlled crossover study in 104 healthy adults causally modulated left dlPFC or left TPJ while participants performed an implicit agency task, providing causal pressure on prospective/retrospective agency components.

A separate 2025 BMI/EEG study linked pre-movement sensorimotor oscillations and large-scale connectivity to later agency judgments across an implanted BMI participant and healthy EEG-BMI participants.

Therefore:

```text
AgencyRelevantMechanism = experimentally manipulable
```

but this still does not imply:

```text
AgencyMechanism = HF24
```

because HF11 action prediction/execution, HF20 sensory feedback, HF3 monitoring/judgment and HF8 self/action representation remain plausible typed owners.

# 15. Candidate B — Persistent Human–Agent adaptation remains extremely high value

2026 Human studies strengthen rather than weaken its scientific importance.

A preregistered experiment found passive AI use could reduce self-efficacy, psychological ownership and work meaning, while a more active Human-first collaboration condition largely preserved them.

A separate 2026 experiment found AI guidance can alter reliance and discrimination as a function of users' attitudes toward AI.

General cognitive-offloading experiments show external reminders can improve supported performance while later removal can impair the previously offloaded prospective-memory task below a no-reminder baseline.
