# 1. Deepest HD0 result

The largest Human gaps are not simply missing disciplinary nouns.

The repeated pattern is:

```text
interface-rich
+
mechanism-thin
+
high bridge centrality
```

Examples:

```text
signal → percept
interoception → emotion
learning → cultural inheritance
physical behavior → model of another mind
social interaction → developmental embodiment
symbol → institution
AI assistance → joint capability / internal change
```

Second-generation Human research should therefore search for and falsify bridges.
