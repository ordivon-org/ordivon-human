# HD11-A — Primary Evidence Ledger

## S01 — Speed, Geraerds & McRae 2025 — voluntary visual imagery versus mental simulation

`Dissociating voluntary mental imagery and mental simulation: Evidence from aphantasia.`
Memory & Cognition 53:2674–2685 (2025).
PMID: 40493310.
DOI: 10.3758/s13421-025-01731-y.

Primary result used here:

```text
aphantasic and control participants
showed similar property-verification patterns interpreted as mental simulation
under the studied language-comprehension task
```

while the groups differed strongly in conscious voluntary visual imagery.

Pressure:

```text
ConsciousVoluntaryVisualImagery
!= MentalSimulationByDefinition
```

This does not establish total independence between all imagery-like processing and simulation.

## S02 — Liu et al. 2025 — 7-T fMRI of imagery in aphantasia

`Visual mental imagery in typical imagers and in aphantasia: A millimeter-scale 7-T fMRI study.`
Cortex 185:113–132 (2025).
PMID: 40031090.
DOI: 10.1016/j.cortex.2025.01.013.

Ten typical imagers and ten congenital aphantasic participants performed imagery/perception tasks across:

```text
shape
color
written words
faces
spatial relations
```

Pressure:

```text
subjective imagery experience
!= objective task information by identity
```

and supports explicit measurement separation among neural processing, task performance and experienced imagery.

## S03 — Seeber et al. 2025 — real versus imagined navigation

`Human neural dynamics of real-world and imagined navigation.`
Nature Human Behaviour 9:781–793 (2025).
PMID: 40065137.
DOI: 10.1038/s41562-025-02119-3.

Intracranial recordings showed structured hippocampal theta dynamics during imagined navigation with relationships to route position despite absence of current external navigational cues.

Pressure:

```text
ImaginedScenario can have dynamic spatial state structure.
ImaginedTrajectory != CurrentPerceptualTrajectory.
```

No claim is made that hippocampal theta defines imagination generally.

## S04 — Balaban & Ullman 2025 — dynamic object imagination capacity

`The capacity limits of moving objects in the imagination.`
Nature Communications 16, 5899 (2025).
DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-61021-8.

Nine preregistered experiments, N=313 total, tested continuation/tracking of object motion in imagination.

Pressure:

```text
Imagination can involve dynamic state updating.
DynamicImagination != StaticImageRetrievalOnly.
```

The capacity/error structure motivates explicit scenario-state and transition coordinates.

## S05 — Dijkstra et al. 2025 — distinguishing imagination from reality

`A neural basis for distinguishing imagination from reality.`
Neuron 113(15):2536–2542.e4 (2025).
PMID: 40480215.
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuron.2025.05.015.

Trial-level reality judgments were related to combined sensory activity generated by imagery/perception and frontal-network reality judgments.

Pressure:

```text
GenerationSource
!= SensorySignalStrength
!= RealityJudgment
```

and:

```text
ImaginedContent != KnownImaginedSourceByDefinition.
```

## S06 — Mazor, Firestone & Phillips 2026 — pretense as self-simulation

`Pretending Not to Know Reveals a Capacity for Model-Based Self-Simulation.`
Psychological Science 37(2):136–149 (2026).
PMID: 41632587.
DOI: 10.1177/09567976251409747.

Two game-based experiments, total N=1,001 adults. Participants knew the solution but attempted to act as if ignorant; behavior approximated genuine uninformed play while retaining detectable traces of imperfect simulation.

Pressure:

```text
PretendedState != ActualBeliefState
Pretence != MereFalseReport
```

and motivates explicit simulated-self/information-state representation.

## S07 — Cantarella et al. 2025 — self/time effects on mind wandering

`Face the future! How the self and time shape mind-wandering.`
Cortex 193:105–116 (2025).
PMID: 41213235.
DOI: 10.1016/j.cortex.2025.10.004.

Four groups of healthy young adults were manipulated with present/future and self/other face representations before a reaction-time task with self-caught and probe-caught mind-wandering measurement.

## S11 — Reality monitoring and metacognitive confidence 2024

`Reality monitoring and metacognitive judgments in a false-memory paradigm.`
PMID: 38007192.

Participants perceived or voluntarily imagined word-pair completions and later judged sources. Imagined items were sometimes externalized as perceived, with confidence not uniquely protecting against such source errors.

Pressure:

```text
Confidence != SourceTruth
SourceAttribution != ActualGenerationSource.
```

Used as a direct reality/source-monitoring dissociation.

## S12 — Dream lucidity and source discrimination 2025

`The roles of recollection and familiarity in the positive association between dream lucidity and reality monitoring: Evidence from ERPs and EEG.`
Consciousness and Cognition (2025).
PMID: 41167139.
DOI: 10.1016/j.concog.2025.103947.

Participants imagined or perceived object-related items and later made source judgments; higher trait lucidity was associated with aspects of source-memory accuracy and electrophysiological markers in the reported study.

Pressure:

```text
Phenomenal vividness and source discrimination are separable dimensions.
Dream-related metacognition != dream-generation mechanism.
```

# Evidence synthesis

The primary evidence constrains HD11-A to reject these simple identities:

```text
Imagination = VisualImagery
Simulation = VoluntaryImagery
FutureThought = Narrative
MindWandering = Imagination
Pretence = FalseBelief
InternalGeneration = KnownInternalSource
Vividness = Reality
Confidence = CorrectRealityJudgment
```

The surviving research problem is therefore not `imagery strength`.

It is whether there is a reusable cross-modal process for constructing/updating scenario states under typed reality commitment, source, constraint and control relations, or whether those cases reduce to existing HF8/HF9/HF7/HF2 processes plus stance/use.
