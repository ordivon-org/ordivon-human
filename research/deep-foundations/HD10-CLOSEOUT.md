
# 16. Final result

HD10's strongest conclusion is:

> **A Human's individual differences are not one vector of hidden essences. They are a typed family of partially persistent, context- and history-sensitive projections over a changing Human embedded in relationships, environments and support systems. Shared population coordinates remain useful, but person-level dynamics, trajectories and counterfactual capability require their own evidence and scopes.**

HD10 is therefore complete.
