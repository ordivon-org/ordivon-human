# Ordivon Human

**Researching humans as embodied, adaptive, social, economic, temporal, and tool-using agents.**

Ordivon Human studies how body, mind, capability, resources, relationships, institutions, environments, actions, events, and tools interact over time to shape a person's agency, health, well-being, and life trajectory.

The project exists because the broader Ordivon stack currently represents people mainly as participants, owners of consequences, and sources of goals or decisions. That is sufficient for system authority, but not for understanding human development itself.

## Central question

> How does a human change over time, why do different people diverge under similar conditions, and which interventions expand durable agency without reducing the person to one score or one optimization target?

## Stable responsibility

Ordivon Human owns research into:

- dynamic models of human state, context, development, adaptation, and decline;
- measurement of biological, cognitive, psychological, behavioural, economic, social, and environmental variables;
- longitudinal trajectories, heterogeneity, causal hypotheses, and intervention evidence;
- individual-to-population and population-to-individual inference limits;
- human–AI augmentation, dependence, autonomy, and co-development;
- methods for responsible, privacy-preserving N-of-1 research.

It does **not** own:

- medical diagnosis, treatment, or clinical decision support;
- a universal ranking, personality label, or aggregate “human score”;
- an identity provider, user-profile service, or Ordivon authority layer;
- a personal-data warehouse or public repository of identifiable records;
- generic psychology, economics, sociology, or health encyclopaedia content;
- product features before a research question demonstrates that implementation is necessary.

## Current state

H0 was completed on 2026-08-02.

The initial ten-level human hierarchy was deleted and replaced by a smaller question-specific dynamic model separating:

```text
person state
context
action
event or intervention
time and history
observation and measurement
outcomes and value assumptions
```

H0 also demonstrated why observational group differences, population average effects, and individual causal responses cannot be substituted for one another, and established a minimum evidence boundary for human–AI capability transfer.

Current disposition:

```text
model: retained, reduced
repository: retained, research-only
implementation: one synthetic causal simulation
human data collection: not admitted
platform work: not admitted
H1: ready, inactive
```

See [`research/h0/README.md`](research/h0/README.md).

## Relationship to Ordivon

```text
Ordivon Human     studies humans and human development
Ordivon Computing synthesizes cross-project research and shared system implications
Ordivon Host      preserves Goals, Tasks, commitments, and participant decisions
Ordivon Harness   organizes model cognition
Ordivon Runtime   executes trusted-local work
Ordivon World     connects Tasks to external systems
Ordivon Game      provides bounded interactive worlds for behavioural experiments
Ordivon Security  studies intelligent opposition and adversarial organization
Ordivon Web       publishes selected research
```

Human research may change how Ordivon models attention, autonomy, learning, collaboration, and human–AI interaction. It does not gain authority over a person's choices merely because it models them.
