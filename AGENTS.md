# Agent instructions

Ordivon Human is a research repository, not a product scaffold.

## Object before method

Before adding a research artefact, decide whether it studies:

- a human system or relation — place it under `research/`;
- measurement, inference, or study design — place it under `methods/`;
- implementation required by one admitted study — justify it explicitly.

Do not allow methodology, AI, data infrastructure, or governance to substitute for describing the human phenomenon itself.

## Required discipline

Before adding code, schemas, datasets, dashboards, automation, or process:

1. name the human phenomenon or research question;
2. show why mature disciplinary concepts are insufficient or need composition;
3. identify the minimum evidence and privacy class;
4. state the competing explanation, falsifier, and deletion condition;
5. prefer official frameworks, primary research, and reproducible analysis;
6. distinguish structure, state, capacity, action, context, outcome, and observation;
7. distinguish description, prediction, causal claim, and value judgment;
8. do not add identifiable human data to Git;
9. do not present medical, psychological, financial, legal, or high-consequence predictions as diagnosis or authority;
10. do not create a universal human score, mandatory profile, or comprehensive ontology.

Default to deleting structure that has no complete human case and no demonstrated explanatory value.
