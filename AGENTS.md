# Agent instructions

Ordivon Human is a research repository, not a product scaffold.

Before adding code, schemas, datasets, dashboards, automation, or process:

1. name the research question;
2. show why existing tools are insufficient;
3. identify the minimum data and privacy class;
4. state the falsification and deletion condition;
5. prefer primary sources and reproducible analysis;
6. distinguish observation, inference, causal claim, and normative judgment;
7. do not add identifiable human data to Git;
8. do not present medical, psychological, financial, legal, or high-consequence predictions as diagnosis or authority.

Default to deleting proposed structure that has no current study and no demonstrated analytical value.
