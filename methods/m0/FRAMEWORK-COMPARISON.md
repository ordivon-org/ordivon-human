# Methods M0 Framework Comparison

The detailed case matrix and deletion result are in [`FRAMEWORK-COMPOSITION.md`](FRAMEWORK-COMPOSITION.md) and [`MODEL-DELETION.md`](MODEL-DELETION.md).

Ordivon Human begins by composing mature frameworks rather than inventing a proprietary taxonomy.

## WHO ICF

The World Health Organization's International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health represents body functions and structures, activities, participation, and environmental factors.

Retained lesson:

- health is not merely diagnosis;
- functioning occurs in context;
- activities, participation, and environmental conditions require explicit representation.

Limit:

- ICF is oriented toward health and functioning, not a complete model of learning, economic resources, identity, technological augmentation, or life strategy.

Source: <https://www.who.int/classifications/international-classification-of-functioning-disability-and-health>

## NIMH RDoC

The Research Domain Criteria framework studies dimensions of neurobehavioural functioning across constructs such as cognition, positive and negative valence, social processes, arousal/regulation, and sensorimotor systems, using multiple units of analysis from biology to behaviour and self-report.

Retained lesson:

- study dimensional mechanisms rather than relying only on diagnostic categories;
- integrate multiple levels and measurement methods;
- treat development and environment as essential context.

Limit:

- RDoC is a mental-health research framework and does not cover the full economic, institutional, capability, or human–AI system.

Sources:

- <https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/about-rdoc>
- <https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/constructs/rdoc-matrix>

## OECD Well-being Framework

The OECD framework measures current well-being across material, social, environmental, relational, safety, knowledge, health, work-life, civic, and subjective dimensions, while considering inequalities and resources for future well-being.

Retained lesson:

- income is one dimension rather than the outcome;
- averages must be complemented by inequality and deprivation;
- current conditions and future resource stocks are different analytical objects.

Limit:

- the framework is optimized for population and policy monitoring, not mechanistic or within-person developmental modelling.

Source: <https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/measuring-well-being-and-progress.html>

## NIH All of Us Research Program

All of Us integrates surveys, electronic health records, physical measurements, biospecimens and genomics, wearables, and social or environmental information in a longitudinal research program.

Retained lesson:

- important human questions require multimodal and longitudinal evidence;
- data access and privacy need tiered controls;
- participant diversity and selection affect scientific validity.

Limit:

- the program is primarily oriented toward health and precision medicine, not a general model of human agency or development.

Source: <https://www.nih.gov/allofus/about/protocol>

## UK Biobank

UK Biobank links demographic, lifestyle, self-reported, physical, clinical, imaging, genetic, activity, and environmental data.

Retained lesson:

- biological, behavioural, social, and environmental variables interact;
- large scale does not remove selection, measurement, generalization, or causal-identification problems;
- continuous activity and longitudinal linkage can reveal patterns unavailable from snapshots.

Source: <https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/about-our-data/types-of-data/>

## Life Course Health Development

Life-course models treat development as continuous, path-dependent, multilevel, reciprocal, and sensitive to timing and environmental interaction.

Retained lesson:

- history and timing are structural variables;
- the same exposure can have different effects at different developmental periods;
- adaptation and environment interact across the lifespan.

Source: <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK543722/table/ch2.Tab1/>

## Ordivon Human synthesis after H0

No reviewed framework is sufficient alone, but their concepts remain authoritative within their scope. H0 retains the following composition:

```text
ICF functioning and environment
+ RDoC dimensional cognition, affect, and multi-method measurement
+ OECD plural outcomes, distribution, and future resources
+ cohort multimodality from All of Us and UK Biobank
+ life-course timing, feedback, and path dependence
+ within-person and N-of-1 inference discipline
+ explicit capability, agency, tools, and human–AI co-development
```

H0 did not turn this composition into a universal ontology. It deleted the initial ten-level hierarchy and retained only a question-specific state-transition model. See the H0 evidence for the exact deletions and case tests.
